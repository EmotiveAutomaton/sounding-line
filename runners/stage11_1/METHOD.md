# Frozen initial method and scoring decisions

Read together with TARGET_CONTRACT.md and the commissioned branch brief. Initial
method choices precede all Stage 11.1 scientific model outcomes. The discarded
interface pilot cannot select a scientifically favorable prompt.

The model is the installed, digest-pinned Qwen 3.5 9B. Each pass has temperature
zero, seed 1101, context 16,384, at most 2,048 generated tokens, thinking disabled,
and four CPU threads under explicit Gear 2. Both sequential competitors have two
calls and the same total token ceiling. Direct review has its own first forecast;
it does not obtain a free shared first call. Account construction precedes a
separately persisted forecast request. Invalid intermediate content is retained,
but the second pass receives a fixed invalid-intermediate marker. It does not get
an uncharged repair call. Mechanical account acceptance is never historical truth.

All methods see the same evidence within each tier. The shared retained payload
limit is 4,096 bytes, and roster admission reserves that amount for both sequential
methods. A conservative prompt/schema byte bound prevents silent context truncation.
An earlier preflight roster lost its whole development writer to this bound; that
roster is retained privately, with a pre-model repair that removes duplicated anchor
text and bounds both retained payloads equally. No scientific outputs informed it.

The final discovery roster has 102 episodes from fourteen writers and 23 sessions;
the separate breadth roster has 96 episodes from nine writers and seventeen sessions.
Every available writer and session is considered before within-writer depth.
Inherited partition and exclusion rules remain. All admitted records are exposed:
these are descriptive comparisons. The six development episodes have one writer,
so they do not support learned generalizable calibration. Fixed abstention is 0.75.

Each method answers the same six questions. Proper probability losses for actor,
operation and relation remain separate. Span state, anchor exactness and intersection
over union remain separate from those losses and from secondary handling prediction.
An absent event has no actor claim; no source record warrants review, endorsement or
understanding. Invalids retain loss one and accuracy zero. Zero true-class probability
retains infinite log loss. Probability sums within 0.005 are normalized with the
original sum retained; sums outside the tolerance are invalid.

Useful positive yield counts correctly predicted actor/operation/relation triples
on present events, with a stricter span-correct yield also shown. This measures
recovery of the fixed questions, not the importance or percentage of human contribution.
Whitespace-only continuation is still an insertion; its text amount and nature must
be visible in examples and cannot support a substantive-contribution claim. Contradicted
finite claims and unsupported mental-attribute claims have separate counts. Free prose
does not create scoring opportunities and is never judged by another model as truth.

Fixed coverage curves rank operation predictions by confidence at 10, 25, 50, 75 and
100 percent, with outcome-independent hash tie breaks. Unknown/invalid responses are
errors in this forced-coverage curve; the fixed abstention coverage is also reported.
Report raw and writer-mean risk and participating writer counts. Primary losses first
average slots within episode, then episodes within writer, then equally across writers.
Writer/session/prompt/domain distributions and connected component counts expose
dependence; no episode-level significance or learned-calibration claim is made.

Cheap rivals are the writer-balanced, symmetrically smoothed training marginal and
a low-dimensional alignment mixture. The latter measures literal offer retention,
net append, closest-offer similarity and unmatched continuation. It retains human
typing and edited-offer alternatives. Fixed heuristic weights are not calibrated
probabilities or exhaustive historical identification. The classifier option is
retired for this initial block because fifteen training episodes from five writers
do not support another tuned classifier. Keep the old capped/uncapped controls as
the separately reported S0 sensitivity.

S1 begins with 32 discovery episodes and continues over the frozen remainder.
Completed blocks advance to the named matched comparison, account intervention,
evidence-dose, breadth or earlier-history branch. An early null does not close the
commission. Measure the first 32 attempts, including pilot/invalid calls, and use
their upper runtime when allocating subsequent branches. Hard reservations remain
300 seconds per attempt plus 30 seconds per GPU block. Stop admission before the
reporting deadline, at cumulative caps, on owner STOP/PAUSE, or on uncertain transport.
