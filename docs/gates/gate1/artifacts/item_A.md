# Rivers of Blood, for someone who keeps bouncing off this game

You asked me whether Rivers of Blood is still worth it. Short answer: yes, for you specifically,
and I'll tell you where it stops being true.

## The thing everyone will tell you first, and why it doesn't matter here

Rivers of Blood got nerfed. Twice. People will tell you it's dead. They're comparing it to what it
was in 2022, when it trivialised the entire game, and by that standard yes, it's worse. But you're
not trying to trivialise the game. You're trying to finish it, in forty-minute chunks, without
getting stuck for three weeks on something and quitting again — which is what happened both times
before.

For that, it's still one of the best weapons in the game, and the reason is not damage. It's that
Corpse Piler — the weapon skill — is a ranged attack that costs no ammunition and doesn't require
you to learn a boss's timing. You can chip something down from a distance you're comfortable at.
That's the whole pitch. Everything else about the weapon is a bonus.

## Getting it

It's on a boss in the far northeast, in Mountaintops of the Giants, and you can't get there yet
from Liurnia. Don't go now. Put it out of your mind for maybe ten hours of play.

I'm telling you this because the version of this advice you'll find elsewhere is a route guide for
rushing there at level 30. It works. Do not do it. You will arrive somewhere the enemies are
tuned forty levels above you, get the weapon, and then be unable to actually use it because you
won't have the stats, and you'll be standing in a zone that will kill you in one hit. That is the
exact shape of the thing that made you quit last time.

## What to actually do between now and then

Use whatever you're using. Put levels into Vigor until it says 40. Not Dexterity, not Arcane —
Vigor. Everyone underrates this and it's the single change that will make the biggest difference
to whether you finish.

When you do get the weapon, you'll want Arcane. You do not need to plan for this now, because you
can respec — there's an NPC who will redistribute your stats, and you get enough opportunities to
do it that you should stop treating level-ups as permanent decisions. That's the other thing that
was making you tense.

## When you have it

Two-hand it. Press the skill button. That's it — that's the build.

If you want the number: around 45 Arcane and 20 Dexterity, once you can respec. But honestly the
weapon works badly-built. It'll carry you at 30 Arcane. I'm giving you a target rather than a
table because last time you spent an hour on a stat calculator and then didn't play.

## Where it stops working

Two places, and you should know now rather than discover them.

Some bosses don't bleed — most of the undead-looking ones, and one very large one near the end.
Against those, Corpse Piler does mediocre fire damage and you'll feel the weapon go dead in your
hands. Keep a normal weapon upgraded as a backup. Not for a whole second build — just something
you can swing.

And it's a crutch. That's not a criticism, it's a description: you will not learn to dodge as well
using it, because you won't have to. If your goal is to finish the game, that's a fine trade and I
think you should take it. If at some point you want the game to feel like what people rave about,
put it away and pick something that makes you stand close.

I'd take the crutch. You've bounced off twice. Finish it first.
