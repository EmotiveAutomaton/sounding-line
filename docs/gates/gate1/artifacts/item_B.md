# The Best Build in Elden Ring: A Complete Guide

Elden Ring offers players an incredible amount of freedom when it comes to character
customization. With dozens of weapons, spells, and talismans available, choosing the right build
can feel overwhelming. In this guide, we'll break down the best build in Elden Ring and explain
exactly why it works so well.

## Why Build Choice Matters

Your build determines how you approach combat, exploration, and boss fights. A well-optimized
build can make even the toughest encounters manageable, while a poorly planned one can leave you
struggling against basic enemies. Understanding the fundamentals of build creation is essential
for any Tarnished looking to succeed in the Lands Between.

## The Best Overall Build: Dexterity/Arcane Bleed

The Dexterity/Arcane bleed build remains one of the most powerful and versatile options available.
This build leverages the blood loss status effect, which deals a large burst of damage when the
status bar fills. Because blood loss scales with the target's maximum HP, it is particularly
effective against bosses with large health pools.

### Recommended Stats

- **Vigor:** 50-60
- **Mind:** 20-25
- **Endurance:** 25-30
- **Strength:** 12-18
- **Dexterity:** 45-55
- **Intelligence:** Base
- **Faith:** Base
- **Arcane:** 45-55

### Recommended Weapons

The primary weapon for this build is a katana with the Blood affinity applied. Katanas naturally
come with innate blood loss buildup, making them ideal for this playstyle. Dual-wielding two
katanas allows you to take advantage of the powerful jumping attack, which hits with both weapons
and builds blood loss extremely quickly.

### Recommended Talismans

- **Lord of Blood's Exultation:** Increases attack power when blood loss occurs nearby
- **Millicent's Prosthesis:** Raises dexterity and increases attack power with successive attacks
- **Rotten Winged Sword Insignia:** Similar to Millicent's Prosthesis but with a stronger effect
- **Shard of Alexander:** Boosts the attack power of weapon skills

### How to Play This Build

The core gameplay loop is straightforward. Close the distance with your target, apply pressure with
light attacks and jumping attacks, and watch for the blood loss proc. Once blood loss triggers, you
will deal significant burst damage and your talismans will activate, further boosting your damage
output. Repeat this cycle until the enemy is defeated.

Against faster enemies, you may need to be more patient and look for openings after their attack
combos. Against slower enemies, you can be more aggressive and maintain constant pressure.

## Alternative Builds Worth Considering

While the Dexterity/Arcane bleed build is exceptionally strong, other options are also viable.
Strength builds offer high poise damage and satisfying heavy attacks. Intelligence builds provide
ranged options and excellent crowd control. Faith builds offer a mix of offensive and support
capabilities, including healing.

## Conclusion

The Dexterity/Arcane bleed build stands out as the best build in Elden Ring due to its high damage
output, versatility, and effectiveness against a wide range of enemies. Whether you're a newcomer
to the game or a veteran looking to try something new, this build offers a rewarding and powerful
playstyle. Give it a try and see for yourself why so many players consider it the best option
available.
