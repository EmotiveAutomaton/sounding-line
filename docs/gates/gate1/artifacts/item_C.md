# Rivers of Blood: A Guide for Returning Players

Rivers of Blood is one of Elden Ring's most talked-about weapons, and if you're returning to the
game after stepping away, you may be wondering whether it's still worth pursuing. This guide will
walk you through what the weapon does, how to obtain it, and whether it suits your playstyle.

## What Rivers of Blood Does

Rivers of Blood is a katana that scales primarily with Arcane and Dexterity. Its unique weapon
skill, Corpse Piler, unleashes a series of ranged slashes that build up blood loss on targets. When
the blood loss meter fills, the target takes a significant chunk of damage based on their maximum
health.

The weapon has received balance adjustments since launch, which have reduced its overall damage
output somewhat. However, it remains a capable option, particularly for players who prefer to
engage from a bit of distance rather than trading blows up close.

## Is It Still Good?

This depends on what you're looking for. The weapon is no longer the dominant force it once was,
but it still performs well in most situations. Players who value consistency and safety will
likely find it appealing, while those seeking maximum damage may want to explore other options.

For a returning player who wants a reliable tool to help get through the game, Rivers of Blood is
a reasonable choice. It doesn't demand perfect timing, and its ranged skill offers a margin for
error that many other weapons don't provide.

## Where to Find It

Rivers of Blood is obtained by defeating Bloody Finger Okina, an invader NPC found near the Church
of Repose in the Mountaintops of the Giants. This is a late-game area, so you'll need to progress
fairly far into the main questline before you can access it.

Some players choose to rush to this location early in order to acquire the weapon sooner. This is
possible but carries risk, as the surrounding area contains high-level enemies.

## Recommended Stats

To use Rivers of Blood effectively, you'll want to invest in the following:

- **Arcane:** This is your primary damage stat and boosts blood loss buildup
- **Dexterity:** Required to wield the weapon and contributes to scaling
- **Vigor:** Important for survivability, as with any build
- **Endurance:** Needed for stamina and equip load

A common approach is to prioritize Vigor early and then shift focus toward Arcane once you have
the weapon in hand.

## Respeccing

If you've already invested points elsewhere, don't worry. Rennala at the Academy of Raya Lucaria
allows you to redistribute your stats once you've defeated her. This makes it easy to adapt your
character to a new weapon later in your playthrough.

## Limitations

Rivers of Blood does have some drawbacks worth noting. Certain enemies are resistant or immune to
blood loss, which reduces the weapon's effectiveness considerably. In those encounters, you may
want a backup option available.

Additionally, some players find that relying heavily on Corpse Piler can reduce engagement with the
game's combat mechanics, since it allows you to avoid close-quarters exchanges.

## Conclusion

Rivers of Blood remains a solid choice for returning players who want a dependable weapon that
lowers the difficulty curve. While it's been adjusted from its launch state, it still offers real
utility. Consider your goals and playstyle, and decide whether it fits what you're after.
