### Diagnostic branches

| Timing signature | Candidate causes | Bounded response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Large load component | Context/model switching, unloading or repeated initialization | Inspect project clients and request settings; align only project-owned compatible configurations; test one change. |
| Slow token evaluation with little loading | Contention, memory fallback, thermal/power limits, longer effective context, backend differences | Correlate snapshots and matched calls; measure available headroom; use a smaller context/model only as a separately pinned condition. |

### Precision calculation

The retained fourteen writer-level account-versus-direct differences have mean −0.0747 and sample SD **0.2301**, independently recalculated in this planning pass. A rough two-sided 5%/80%-power detectable paired difference is `2.8 × SD / sqrt(n)` under independent, approximately appropriate units:

| Independent units | Approximate detectable difference at SD 0.2301 |
| ---: | ---: |
| 16 | 0.161 |
| 44 | 0.097 |
| 63 | 0.081 |
| 167 | 0.050 |

At this spread, detecting 0.0858 requires roughly 57 independent units. This SD is **not** automatically the SD of the history contrast or a different corpus; estimate contrast-specific variability from retained development data or a designated pilot. The formula is a planning approximation, not a guaranteed power calculation for dependent, bounded outcomes. Do not count passages, prompts, completions or repeated queries as independent writers.

Use a prespecified useful margin, with 0.05 half-Brier as a planning reference and a sensitivity table around it. A 0.09 universal cutoff or “half the observed winner” rule is not justified. Confirmation sizing must not use the selected winner’s inflated point estimate as truth. Use a conservative variance estimate and independent practical margin, or simulate the declared clustered design.

Small screens can reveal malformed outputs, large structural reversals, missing candidate support and variance. They cannot justify ranking four weakly separated methods by whichever happens to win. If support cannot attain useful sensitivity, run a complete descriptive replication and say so. The plan does not promise a human confirmatory result that the corpus cannot supply.

### Dependency and donor controls

Keep own-history versus no-history as the primary paired contrast. Donor history is secondary, matched on allowed features using a frozen development-only donor bank where possible. Do not create a connected evaluation network by repeatedly borrowing histories across test writers, then bootstrap those writers as independent. Shared prompts and source lineages also constrain generalization. Report writer-level descriptive sensitivity separately from a valid population interval; a leave-writer-out sign check cannot repair Round 1’s single connected component.

### A separate acceptance for understanding the artifact

Every product-facing comparison reports:

- Correctly supported and **correctly located useful events**, with the declared eligible-event denominator.
- Correct witnessed operations and dependencies versus unsupported additions and contradictions.
- Unsupported mental assertions per episode, preserving the existing Stage 11.1 operational measure; also distinguish unverified hypotheses from claims presented as observed/certain.

An all-unknown packet avoids overclaiming but does not demonstrate useful recovery. Compare supported yield and error together, at matched coverage where meaningful. ## 8. Exploratory tree

### S12-A. Capable history and what it enables

Run S-P1 with the admitted larger reader and matched cheap controls. Retain the local reader on a small paired diagnostic subset where affordable; do not spend the entire new roster repeating its known weak regime.

**If correct history helps:** distinguish more accurate generated choices from probability calibration, same-session continuity from maker-specific information, and raw history from a frozen reusable hypothesis. Extend to another allowed prompt/source stratum before another model size. Condition new questions on the same permissible evidence.

**If donor history helps similarly:** test generic task scaffolding, source-format clues and donor matching; do not call it a maker model.

**If neither helps but task capability is adequate:** preserve the bounded null and investigate whether current evidence already suffices or previous history is irrelevant. Use known informative histories as a separate constructed control.

**If capability fails:** one interface diagnosis is allowed; then retain the floor result and continue CPU/control studies. A frontier null is not automatically evidence against human invertibility.

**If the interval is wide:** report estimation; do not choose a mechanistic winner. Additional data must be genuinely useful support, not repeated calls on the same writers.

### S12-B. Process context and local interpretation

Compare artifact-only E0, selected context E1 and witnessed process E2 on the **same** targets. Use mechanical alignment, retrieval and direct reading as rivals to a typed reconstructed account. Keep oracle O separate. A context intervention changes the available evidence, not the maker’s past.

Two directions are separate: infer local goals/functions from artifact or delta with the recorded label withheld; and predict an operation/consequence with a request or goal supplied. The second direction tests conditional construction, not reverse goal recovery. In ARIES, both directions concern external requests unless adoption is independently recorded.

Git work uses parent diffs, deletions, renames/copies, merges, authors/committers and missing history. A commit message is reported intent, possibly incomplete or post hoc. ### S12-C. Continuing maker account and reorganization

Compare raw full history, retrieval, a frozen coherent hypothesis bundle and a predictive answer bank. Count acquisition, updates and repeated-query cost. Exact law-conditioned posteriors remain oracle assistance. Whole hypotheses retain goal–belief–skill dependencies; independent marginals may imply a maker that no candidate represents.

Use controlled cases first and human records only for known targets. Separate new observations, rearrangement of established assertions and new proposed explanations. **Branches:**

- Known evidence becomes more usable without new information: test new vocabulary, query operator and another evidence order.
- Cue supplies a missing law: reclassify as supervision and compare equal law access across rivals.
- Full history wins: distinguish compression loss from access failure using exact and learned-bank references.
- A coherent mixture wins only under ambiguity: retain a conditional mixture policy rather than general complexity escalation.
- All accounts remain equivalent: report that equivalence and ask which next evidence source can distinguish them; do not fabricate a preferred biography.

**Allocation:** first screen is structural, not an effect ranking; then one contrast sized from its own variability. At most two reconstructed-state variants advance at a time.

### S12-D. Tiny-reader causal mechanism

Make the small controlled model primary; avoid a third open-ended attempt to repair the same inadequate cached reader. Train on permitted observation/outcome data, freeze development/test worlds and verify ordinary task competence, no-op hooks and reference consistency. A failed tiny model can be replaced once with a simpler task/model setting, explicitly changing scope.

Reuse existing DAS/alignment code and the exact reference. Compare supervised belief readout and selective interchange against random/shuffled/wrong-variable/full-state controls. Fit only on development pairs. Use new goal/belief combinations. Independent static assignments must commute where the reference requires it; a separate ordered pair of dependent state updates tests genuine noncommutation. Do not confuse those two targets. Record ordinary task damage and off-manifold concerns.

**Branches:** selective improvement transfers to a new query family; decodability without selective use motivates one alternative access site; any intervention changing all answers is not identified maker state. A pretrained larger reader becomes a later transfer instrument only after this consumer works.

J-lens fitting/readout may enter here when it answers a remaining distinction. The two initial model settings are a combined allowance across both repositories, not two attempts each; share admission and failure receipts. The reader’s learned controlled mechanism is not a human neural correspondence claim.

### S12-E. Expertise, audience goals and distribution shift

Use the Ghost local-world exports to distinguish technical success from convincing presentation. Reader competence must be demonstrated on a held-out production task. Compare declarative knowledge, observational learning, actual practice and an exact replay observer, with feedback/sample budgets tracked.

A frozen model with the same prompt, hidden/KV state, tools and randomness is the same instrument regardless of whether a transcript is labelled practice. If there are weight or persistent-state updates, record and match them in the replay arm where appropriate. Mere tool access is a separate treatment.

Vary on-policy versus demonstration distribution mismatch and intact/degraded observation. DAgger motivates distribution coverage as a rival explanation, not a proof that every production advantage is covariate shift. Test the predicted relationship without requiring a monotonic result. Preserve audience goal, prior routine and current objective as separate variables.

**Branches:** practice only improves domain execution, not inversion; practice helps inversion only under degraded evidence; presentation goals reverse apparent errors; or replay matches practice. Every outcome narrows the mechanism. Do not call computational automaticity human flow.
