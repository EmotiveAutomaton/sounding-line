## What the burst would test

Run the 9B and 27B reader packages on the same human/constructed tasks. Ask whether
executable reconstruction adds more than equally budgeted deliberation, whether
correct personal history improves prediction, and whether learned procedures help
beyond concrete examples. Keep direct prediction and cheap controls alongside those
contrasts. The design separates useful prediction from historical correspondence
and model behavior from human mechanisms. No new theory decision is needed for
this finite descriptive experiment.

## Budget and preflight

Current [Modal pricing](https://modal.com/pricing) confirms the specification's
$2.301264/hour reference for L40S, two physical cores and 32 GiB RAM, before other
costs. Preserve the $3 discarded pilot, initial pilot-plus-main $20 envelope,

This establishes affordability in principle; it does not establish throughput
or promise the full target roster will fit.

The [official 27B listing](https://ollama.com/library/qwen3.5:27b) still shows
Q4_K_M, 17 GB and digest prefix `7653528ba5cb`. Resolve the full digest and measure
long-request memory/runtime in the actual pilot. Do not load it on the active
local GPU. Model-file size is not peak memory.

[Resource billing](https://modal.com/docs/guide/resources) uses the higher of
requested and actual CPU/memory. [Preemption](https://modal.com/docs/guide/preemption)
can restart a function; [timeouts](https://modal.com/docs/guide/timeouts) are per
attempt. The proposed durable expiration and retained uncertain reservations
therefore matter. [Workspace budgets](https://modal.com/docs/guide/budgets) cover
gross usage, while spend limits concern charges after credits. Environment budgets
require Team/Enterprise and exclude some workspace charges. Check account usage,
capacity and the available backstop before dispatch; preserve unrelated workloads
and do not buy an upgraded plan for this run. Account/budget availability has not
been verified in this assessment.

## Running alongside current work

Prepare the cloud changes in an isolated checkout with its own output namespace.
Use one remote GPU/model at a time and one authoritative campaign ledger. Do not
merge changed reader dependencies underneath the live, source-pinned Gear 2 worker,
copy its GPU lock, or touch Ghost V17 private/reserved work. A local controller is
lightweight; setup still consumes operator time and must yield to actual local
completion/failure transitions. Healthy workers use transition-only supervision.

A reasonable target is several focused setup hours followed by roughly a day of
bounded execution and analysis, subject to the pilot. This is judgment, not a
measured schedule or a promise to finish the entire tree. If setup approaches a
working day, keep the ready main comparison and document affected deferrals as the
specification requires. No paid call, model download, cloud resource or shared
budget change was made during this review. The archived source's prior acceptance
record is retained; the current request is assessment and filing, not an instruction
to replace the ongoing local study with cloud execution.
