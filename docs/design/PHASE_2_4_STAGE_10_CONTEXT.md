## Question and scope

Does a compact, revisable model of a maker's procedures and goals improve
predictions of withheld behavior beyond direct reading, retrieved examples,
and an equally expensive direct reader? Compare the six prescribed strategies
on the same allowed evidence, with executable Ghost tasks and human revision
records. Goal, procedure, recipient effect and historical control stay separate.
