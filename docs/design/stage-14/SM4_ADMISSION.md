# SM4 local prototype comparison: readout admission

The October 13 commission authorizes the conditional prototype follow-up. The
adapter lane became free on October 14. This document freezes a bounded first
admission check; it does not alter the v11 cards or admit their failed attempts.

## Question and scope

Can initialization from similar writers improve later recorded suggestion
handling after refinement on a writer's own earlier human-typed text? The
aligned outcome has four classes: accept, edit, dismiss and ignore. Previously
completed P03 candidate-selection scores are not handling forecasts.

The first proposed backbone is the already cached SmolLM2 360M Instruct, which
also occurs in the existing local ladder. This is a separately identified
comparison, not a repair or a replacement of any old result. No paid call or
download is required. A refused admission receives its own complete record;
there is no automatic prompt tuning, model substitution or scientific retry.

## Literature and theory check

READ: Tang et al., [Maia4All](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.21488v1), methodology,
discussion, conclusion and the inspected language-model appendix. The paper
uses prototype enrichment followed by individual adaptation. Its language-model
case uses author tokens, learned prototype matching and text-prediction loss;
it does not validate four-way suggestion handling. Rich prototype histories are
a stated limitation. This is inspiration, not a reproduction or a transferred
effect-size promise. The local comparison tests the curator's proposed
similarity-based initialization and correction in reader heuristics section 1;
it cannot identify private preferences or a human cognitive mechanism.

Method review: LESSONS sections 2–5 and CONTROLS. The governing failure risk is
an incapable handling readout turning a useful representation into an apparent
personal-learning null. Validate that readout before fitting prototypes.

## Frozen semantic check

Use the model's native chat template, complete prospective task fields and a
single-letter answer. The system defines the four handling categories. Score
the native next-token logits for A, B, C and D; each must be exactly one token.
Rotate the letter-to-category mapping through all four cyclic permutations,
convert each to probabilities and average in category order. This removes a
fixed letter prior from the primary readout; disagreement across rotations is
retained. No JSON generation, output repair or answer selection is involved.

Sixteen synthetic records, four per category, describe known completed actions
in ordinary language. These descriptions occur only in this semantic check,
never in a real prospective target. They test category understanding and answer
mapping, not forecasting skill or human-study validity. The fixed gate requires
at least 12 of 16 correct after averaging, at least two of four in every class,
and at least 44 of 64 individual rotations correct. All values must be finite
and all native prompt lengths fit the 2,048-token context. Equality passes;
anything below a bound refuses. A separate balanced no-evidence fixture must
give exactly chance accuracy with identical inputs and averaged half-Brier at
least 0.375. Synthetic perfect, uniform and confidently wrong vectors validate
the score algebra independently. The thresholds are engineering admission
floors, not significance tests. Passing licenses only the next preparation step.

Resources: one exclusive GPU lock, at least 4,500 MiB free at entry, 768 MiB at
runtime, at most 78 C, all parameters on CUDA, at least 8 GiB host RAM and commit
headroom, and 20 GiB free disk. Wall cap: 15 minutes. Preserve dispatch identity,
tokenized prompts, model/source hashes, raw logits, probabilities, telemetry and
terminal output. Timeout, missing telemetry or resource failure refuses; an
uncertain native exit retains its lock. No raw human records enter this check.

## Subsequent comparison, conditional on this check

Before any human training, a separately frozen card must instantiate all of:

- The existing SM1 aligned roster and its whole-writer pilot split. Later
  targets never determine eligibility, clusters, training, temperature or steps.
  Historical calibration is separated chronologically from training; complete
  training sessions must end before the recipient's calibration cutoff.
- Four history-only clusters using earlier decision rates and mean frozen text
  embeddings. The recipient cannot train their own prototype. Other writers'
  text and labels also precede that recipient's cutoff. Record all exclusions,
  sparse histories, clustering inputs and complete native token budgets.
- Rank-sixteen prototype and own-text refinement, with prototype fitting fully
  charged in the matched total optimizer budget. A from-base own-text arm and
  a randomly assigned prototype use the same total budget. Random assignment
  is independent of target outcomes; a chance match remains in the control.
- Complete prospective writing prompt, draft view and offered menu; no target
  label or effort. Context overflow is explicit invalidity, not silent clipping
  or a favorable subset. Temperature uses historical calibration only.
- Paired half-Brier and baseline-relative log loss on the same SM1 targets,
  personal-frequency and population rivals, writer resampling and crossed
  writer/prompt sensitivity, invalid system loss and a matched-valid analysis.
  Prototype versus from-base and random initialization are required together.
- An independently replayable pilot, measured conservative whole-block budget,
  finite main continuation and the original Friday reporting reserve. No main
  launch follows automatically from this semantic check.

This document admits only the synthetic readout check. Prototype training is
not yet implemented capacity. SM7 keeps theory files unchanged, and the final
joint packet remains the scientific reporting boundary.
