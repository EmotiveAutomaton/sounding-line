## H-WIKI: known-editor future edits

Question: does strictly earlier editing history improve an editor's next edit-type
and later-revert prediction beyond page/topic and population rivals?

Status: SOURCE POLICY APPROVED; bounded intake pilot complete and internally landed. Full study remains gated. See PUBLIC_SOURCE_PILOTS.md.
Use the official revision API or dumps, not scraping. Before outcomes are read,
freeze namespaces, date window, editor eligibility, bot/anonymous exclusions,
rights, request rate limits and a bounded acquisition ceiling of 20,000 revisions
or two engineering hours. Aim for 64 eligible editors, with explicit shortfall if
the source cannot support them; do not manufacture independent units.

Freeze reader release cutoffs first. All evaluation edits must postdate the latest
admitted reader cutoff. Within editor, train precedes calibration, which precedes
evaluation; global and donor fits stop at each recipient's training cutoff. Keep
page identity as a crossed dependence sensitivity. Revert targets need an explicit
seven-day follow-up horizon; right-censored edits are retained as unscorable, not
negative. Compare editor frequency, page frequency, lexical and embedding heads,
and a matched donor. Predictions cannot see future revision text, summaries or
revert metadata. Editor summaries are declared explanations, never private goals.

Primary: equal-editor half-Brier difference from the strongest calibration-selected
population rival; invalids cost one, margin 0.01, editor uncertainty with page
sensitivity. Secondary: full-support logarithmic loss, calibration and cost.
Ruler: exact parent reconstruction and known revert chains, plus future-field leak
sentinels. Outcomes route to broader editor coverage only if source independence
and precision support it; otherwise retain descriptive bounds. The edit-type
taxonomy must distinguish mechanical operations from semantic labels. Prior art:
[Yang et al.](https://aclanthology.org/D17-1213/), abstract inspected; their labels
are annotated interpretations, not mental-state ground truth.

## H-BLOG: personal text with an observed Git history

Question: does earlier personal prose predict later choices on the same author's
blog, and which revisions can be recovered after withholding their parent?

Status: SOURCE POLICY APPROVED; bounded rights/history pilot complete and internally landed. Full study remains gated. See PUBLIC_SOURCE_PILOTS.md.
Before reading outcomes, select up to 12 independently maintained public blogs
with explicit usable text rights, at least 20 dated posts and 30 document commits,
including at least eight post-cutoff eligible changes. Keep rights for prose
separate from theme/code licenses. Freeze the repository roster from those criteria;
retain exclusions. Author and committer identities, multiple-parent commits and
coauthorship are distinct. Repository holdout is not proven author independence.

Build chronological own, matched topic-donor and pooled corpora, and exact
parent/child pairs. The current README intake cannot substitute for this source.
Compare the established personal head and four-arm text adapter on recorded edits;
use source-available candidate alternatives rather than inventing rejected human
choices. Reconstruction sees final text and only its declared past view; evaluation
holds parents and commit descriptions separately. Report candidate coverage and
conditional ranking separately. Admission requires rights, exact replay, release
cutoffs and a complete memory pilot. Stop after two integration hours if intake
cannot meet those gates; continue existing-source work.

## H02-UNDO: reverse-edit rival

Question: can a learned reverse-edit model reconstruct a withheld predecessor
beyond copying, nearest prior text and a direct local model, at equal evidence?

Status: DESIGN ONLY; depends on authorized source pairs and measured local memory.
First verify released PEER-Undo weights, license and compatibility. Within the
two-hour integration limit use them if genuinely obtainable and resident; otherwise
train a small cached model on authorized before/after pairs. Freeze a 128-pair
execution pilot and a 1,024-pair main with editor/repository and chronological
partitions before fitting. Smaller support is an explicit descriptive reduction.

Generate the previous version and an edit plan separately. Score edit-span recovery,
exact reconstruction, plausible-equivalent histories, hallucinated operations and
cost; plan agreement does not establish historical truth. Include no-edit,
equifinal-parent and misleading-summary controls. Only witnessed parent evidence
is an oracle ceiling. Do not use generated histories as confirmatory labels.
[PEER sections 4 and 6.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.11663v1) were inspected:
the reverse model generates earlier text and plans, but the authors also report
mismatched generated plans and replace them using a separate explanation model.
This is a serious rival and a warning against treating fluent undo as recovery.

## M-MIDDLE: selective failure under drive ambiguity

Question: on logged model-makers, does hiding drive-relevant evidence selectively
harm goal inference while surface affect and expertise reads remain intact?

Status: DESIGN ONLY; matches the supplied errata and Layers section 3. This is an
analogue test of assigned constraints, not a measurement of latent human drives.
No claim of novel literature precedence is made. Mechanistic agent simulation
belongs to Ghost and is not implemented here.

Construct 32 development and 128 disjoint main scenario lineages, crossing two
assigned drive constraints, two independently varied affective styles and two
realized expertise conditions. A model-maker must actually select among logged
alternatives; retain noncompliance rather than conditioning it away. Generate at
most one complete eight-condition block per lineage per admitted maker family.
Record actual choices and resulting text separately from requested assignments.
Audit text identity and controlled witnesses before admitting a reader.

Freeze three reader views: artifact alone; artifact plus non-drive history;
artifact plus the distinguishing drive evidence. Evaluator assignments and choices
stay hidden. Separately score goal, affect and expertise; do not aggregate them.
Primary is the increase in goal half-Brier under drive ambiguity relative to the
mean increase for the other two targets. Demand a goal degradation above 0.05 and
affect/expertise changes inside +/-0.02 with lineage uncertainty; otherwise call
the specific fingerprint unresolved or absent for this construction, not human
theory refutation. These are prospective engineering thresholds.

Rulers include exact indistinguishable twins, drive-evidence swaps, affect-only
swaps, expertise-only swaps, shuffled histories and a source-aware ceiling. NULL:
generic difficulty harms all targets or no target selectively. ALTERNATIVE: the
goal-specific interaction survives the controls. A failed ceiling or assignment
audit makes the fingerprint unanswerable and blocks the main. One diagnosed
repair identity is available, within the shared two-hour integration ceiling.
Independent-family replication requires its own completed pilot; repeated prompts
are not fresh lineages. Outputs include the complete attempted manifest, generator
and reader records, evaluator truth, exclusions, paired scores and resource costs.
