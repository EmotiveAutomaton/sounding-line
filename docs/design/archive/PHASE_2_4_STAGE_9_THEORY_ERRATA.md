## What changes at the level of the theory

1. **Constructive criticism is relative to a proposed maker goal.** A critic can attempt a better local means to that end. The goal estimate, constraints, and proposed improvement remain fallible. Local competence, retrospective explanatory fluency, full production competence, and historical correspondence are separate achievements.
2. **Familiarity is a candidate way to begin reading.** An accumulation of little quirks may produce felt recognition, which itself invites assessment. Confidence does not supply its own accuracy, and an anomaly need not occur first. This refines the existing expertise and closeness priors.
3. **Expertise can serve a changed purpose.** The novelist's ridicule-to-applause example distinguishes reusing a technique from being obstructed by an old habit or learning a new technique. Existing revision measurements do not isolate this mechanism.
4. **The reader has a purpose too.** Learning the game and learning about its player can justify different amounts of analysis of the same move. A decision to stop is not proof that nothing informative remains. This is a reader variable, not another target inside the maker.
5. **Definitions and evidence need to agree across files.** Governing purpose is distinct from a focal subgoal. Identity recovery is not value recovery. A failed reader does not demonstrate that information is absent. Stage 8 passed prediction, failed generation, admitted no reader, and ran no confirmation. Its failed surprise diagnostic does not identify a uniquely surface-based internal mechanism.

The pursuit ledger gains useful distinctions to examine. The warrant ledger gains no new empirical support from the curator's walkthrough.
