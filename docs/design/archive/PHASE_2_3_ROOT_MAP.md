## 1. Which world became more likely

**W4 (process-aware audit) is the day's winner, three constructions deep.** Route
identity, handling-free contribution, and ratification all read at or near chance from
final artifacts while their process-aware ceilings sit at 0.78 to 1.0. What records
can audit, artifacts do not tell.

**W1 (bounded historical inversion) survives in exactly two channels**, both content-
shaped rather than topology-shaped: realized executed choices (0.86 against twins,
echo-independent, from Phase 2.2) and realized handling at long form (0.77 on
concealed-vs-unnoticed once the hedging had room to exist — the L150 wall was
resolution). The constructed-world rulers (handling, ordered accidents) are validated
at ceiling with origin abstention enforced.

**W3 (context-conditioned projection) is confirmed for the context channel
specifically**: a false production fact steers as hard as a true one where evidence is
absent, still wins the toss-up where evidence is strong, and the conflict is named 15
percent of the time. Supplied context gets one direct-reading's weight and no truth
discount.

**W5 (fluent rationalization) claimed the generation strategy**: explicit self-route
generation is null where reading is strong, HURTS by seven points where it is weak,
and induces fabrication the direct form never shows.

W2 (useful reenactment) went largely unmeasured as reenactment-execution; the
reader-enactable route as a SOLVER was the thing W5 killed. W6 (instrument failure)
claimed exactly one cell honestly: the repair-actor question format.

## 2. What changed in the theory

- **The trace boundary has a shape: content carries, topology does not.** What was
  done to the text is recoverable; how the text came to be — route, negotiation,
  control — is records-only, leaving at most statistical surface residue.
- **Handling is real in text at sufficient length, and its carrier is density.** The
  reader matches a five-line hedge counter and cannot quote the sentence it read.
  Classification without localization: integrative access, not evidential access.
- **Adoption is readable, origin is not, and the ruler can be forced to say so** (the
  origin-abstention gate passed at zero violations on the origin-identical pair).
- **Context-trust is a defect class of its own**, separate from evidence-reading and
  from honest abstention, in this reader family.
- **The self-model prior survives untouched**: what died is the STRATEGY of
  externalizing it into generated prose.

## 3. The strongest rival reading

That the day's W4 pattern is a construction artifact: all three blind results come
from corpora where surface form was deliberately matched (routes) or the negotiated
content deliberately survived either branch (ratification), so the artifacts were
BUILT to underdetermine — and natural production, where routes and negotiations leave
unmatched scars, might read better. This rival is testable and is exactly what the
trace-erasure and natural-transfer branches (B1, D1) exist to decide; it does not
touch the two positive channels, which carry their own controls.

## 5. Questions for the curator (three, open-ended)

1. **Is the content/topology boundary a finding or a construction?** Three artifact-
   silent results in one day, all on deliberately matched corpora (the §3 rival). Do
   you read this as the observational-equivalence rule confirmed, or as the corpora
   doing what they were built to do — and which branch (B1 erasure, D1 natural
   transfer) would move YOUR prior most?
2. **Does Wing E run at all?** Its design supplies expertise through context cards,
   and Wing A measured supplied context as an override channel with no truth
   discount. Running E0 as designed would measure card-following, not expertise
   conditioning. Options are redesign (expertise as reader-side capability rather
   than supplied card), defer behind a context-trust fix, or drop the wing this
   phase — your model of what expertise IS in the reader should decide.
3. **What is the product of a records-dominant world?** If Stage 2 confirms W4, the
   honest artifact-only product is realization evidence plus long-form handling
   density plus abstention, and everything else is an audit interface over records.
   Is that the instrument you want this phase to build toward, or does the theory
   need the artifact-only ambition held open longer?
