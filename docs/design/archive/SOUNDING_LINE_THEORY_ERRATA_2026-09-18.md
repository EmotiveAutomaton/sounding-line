## Research texture and its proper place

The attention proposal has relevant precedent without a matching established identity. Initial accuracy judgments protected against repetition effects when relevant knowledge was available; knowledge alone did not protect reliably in another study. Those are human truth-judgment experiments, not demonstrations of selective artistic learning or immunity. [Brashier, Eliseev & Marsh, 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2019.104054); [Fazio et al., 2015](https://doi.org/10.1037/xge0000098).

Keep this literature texture in the handoff/research record unless a normal literature entry is added with its source and read scope. It does not upgrade the new OPEN row. Existing T01/T02/T03 and A02–A05 rows already carry the project's pertinent source-transmission and selective-uptake results. The errata preserve their numerical evidence rather than replacing it with an external framing.

A future analyst should retain three unresolved joints: whether attention mediates the relevant trust effects; when context improves maker correspondence rather than merely reframing experience; and which human judgments survive delegation. Useful procedural learning, accurate historical reconstruction and willingness to update can move independently. This is the recentering texture, not a claim that the weekend miniature will settle the whole account.
