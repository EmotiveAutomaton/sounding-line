### Three evidence tiers

1. Endpoint artifact and genuinely available brief/context. Record whether a brief exists; never infer one from evaluator truth.
2. The same artifact plus before text and the complete displayed alternatives, with mechanically derived differences.
3. A small, explicitly revealed process record, such as the insertion event, edit interval or a source-position trace. This is an assisted condition and must not be pooled with blind inference.

Match evidence exactly within each methodological comparison. All readers get the same specified public fields. Record identities/amounts for cross-tier comparisons, whose point is the additional information. The evaluator alone sees the complete historical trace until forecast commitment.

### Targets that the logs can support

Retain the historical four-way suggestion-handling outcome as a secondary continuity measure. Add a compact common target form for production actor, operation, affected span and relation to another operation. Examples include selecting an offered fragment, changing that fragment, adding human continuation, changing surrounding text, and removing inserted material. Separate formatting-only changes from substantive text changes. Score an absent or unresolved relation explicitly.

A production actor must be `human_writer`, `model`, `tool`, or `unknown`; story characters are not production actors. Distinguish exact span origin from selection or later integration. Logs can witness events without witnessing attention, motive or understanding. `reviewed`, `endorsed`, `understood`, `expert` and value claims remain unsupported unless the presented evidence actually supplies the relevant information; an exact quotation is not sufficient evidence for those claims.

## Branch tree

### S0 — immediate CPU diagnosis and serious cheap rivals

### S1 — can an evidence-constrained account beat direct reading?

Compare four local methods on both blind evidence tiers:

- One-pass direct prediction in the common target form.
- Direct prediction with a second, budget-matched review pass.
- A first pass constructing a typed contribution account, followed by a second pass that predicts from the public evidence and that retained account.
- The CPU alignment rival.

The structured account contains actor/operation enums, real span anchors, event IDs, proposed dependencies, an evidence pointer, alternative explanation and unresolved fields. Mechanically reject broken references, fictional production actors and impossible event order. A consistency check may establish compatibility, not historical truth. Preserve rejected content and its cost. Cap total generated tokens and calls equally for the two-pass competitors; record actual latency. Do not silently give the account extra context or favorable exemplars.

First use 32 discovery episodes, then up to 128 with the frozen implementation. In a separate diagnostic on up to 64 episodes, remove or replace the generated account with an equally formatted unrelated account while holding the public evidence fixed. This asks whether the downstream reader uses it; changed predictions alone are not a benefit. Score accuracy and susceptibility together. Keep this intervention separate from the main comparison.

**Positive:** send the method and the strongest cheap/direct rival to S3 and breadth replication. **Null:** if its event facts improve without improving the handling label, retain that narrower capability and test S2. If both fail, allow one source-grounded repair on development cases, then use fresh discovery units. **Reversal:** inspect whether detailed claims or irrelevant alternatives cause the harm, then test the sparse account with the same information and budget. Maximum two scientifically distinct account variants in this branch before moving onward.

### S2 — ambiguity, additional evidence and selective correction

Use the best two viable methods from S1 plus the CPU rival on the same episodes. Start with artifact-only estimates, then reveal one admissible additional observation. Compare the fixed next observation, a reader-chosen observation from a fixed menu, and a randomly chosen equal-cost observation. Each method receives the same final evidence in the paired inference comparison; evaluate the query policy separately by what its chosen information buys.

The menu can contain before text, displayed alternatives or a short event/position trace, when available. Charge retrieval, additional tokens and calls. Never return the answer label as a purported neutral query. Score corrections of initially wrong or unresolved production claims, preservation of initially correct unrelated claims, new unsupported detail and marginal gain per query. Choosing an observation is not itself evidence of attention in humans.

Include a distinct synthetic stress set of at least 24 paired examples with the same visible endpoint but different executed histories. Construct these from valid transformations, retain provenance, and match all fields visible at the tested tier. They establish ambiguity within the constructed set, not prevalence in real writing. A reader should not claim certainty until discriminating evidence is supplied. Include true, irrelevant and misleading contextual cues; the latter are declared experimental interventions. Test whether context corrects particular relations or merely moves the whole story.

**If evidence helps:** select a minimal useful evidence tier for the prototype. **If only full traces help:** keep the assisted prototype and explicitly retain the blind-inference failure. **If context misleads:** prioritize correction/abstention and preserve that failure for Sunday; do not tune on the scored counterexamples until they have been retired to development.

- [Measuring Faithfulness in Chain-of-Thought Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.13702): interventions can test whether an explicit intermediate account affects predictions. This motivates our diagnostic; it does not establish faithfulness or unfaithfulness of the current reader.
- [Selective Classification for Deep Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.08500): risk and coverage must be assessed together. Its guarantees do not transfer to our small dependent writing corpus or to an uncalibrated model confidence number.

The current theory files remain the conceptual authority. This continuation tests contribution evidence and correction; it does not quietly replace the theory with provenance classification.
