## Part A. What changes

The theory files stop at the 7 November errata. Since then the theory thread ran thirteen rounds with the curator, and the structure moved further than any earlier interval. This errata files that structure. It adds rows and one consolidated block per file, and it removes nothing.

1. **Needs.** Six inherited targets, each a band: Knowledge, Nest, Belonging, being visibly respectable, being visibly compassionate, and the body (G266 to G269). This reverses two clauses of G241: confidence in one's knowledge is now a need, and standing is now two needs.
2. **Channels.** Nine, with SHAME a channel and DISGUST carried without the words on trial (G270). The 4 November trial table stays, and a current table follows it.
3. **Triggers.** A trigger is a change of a certain shape on a need, any channel can serve any need, and the curator accepts the word appraisal for a set of such slopes (G271). A channel has three ways in (G272).
4. **Rows per channel.** FEAR's strength does not follow predicted harm in prompted reports (G273). SHAME, guilt, embarrassment and pride are placed (G274). CARE is a weighted sum with a second trigger that reads care coming back, and gratitude is the second index rising (G275). PANIC/GRIEF, PLAY, LUST and the others are restated (G276 to G279).
5. **Feeling, Stress and the outer loop.** Feeling reports the slope (G280). Stress is the leftover, in three states (G281), read by an outer loop with one aim (G282).
6. **Capital.** The world arranged so that the most future actions stay available; called Freedom and then the stock until the curator took the name Capital on 15 November (G283).
7. **Attention, traits, expressions, displays** (G284 to G287).
8. **The learned layer.** The ladder is the marketplace of ideas (G288). Its four kinds are defined on one scale, what a person would trade each for; the word rule is withdrawn in favour of identity, and with it the separate tier the analyst had built in the 14 November round (G289). Schema, the price of an identity, what lifts a fluency, money, and answer keys from acts (G290 to G294).
9. **What a reader needs.** A trigger reader, page readings kept apart from maker readings, exchange, the voice, and the person as settings (five HH rows).
