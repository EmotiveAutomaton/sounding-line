The groups run from the most consequential to the least:

- **Vocabulary (F1):** a set point is the need itself, a deviation is its current distance, and a drive is one of Panksepp's lower-level emotional systems, a pre-made solution that runs from set points.
  - The lexicon gains a deviations row, and the values row now serves set points.
  - A reading rule says how to read text dated 14 October, which called each drive a set point and counted the bodily needs among the drives.
  - The rule also notes that deviation keeps its older sense in the second inference, a departure from the reconstructed process.
- **Learned ends (F2):** anything can become a set point.
  - G188 ("no learned goal becomes terminal") is withdrawn, and its quotation is marked superseded.
  - G191 admits learned ends with animal learning's test. A solution has become an end when it survives the need it served being met another way; disturbing or satisfying the end then separates it from habit.
  - G192: set points sit on needs, not acts, and a recurring act such as care points first to a decaying bond.
- **Drives and PLAY (F3):**
  - G193: drives are pre-made controllers, bundled with their set points in animals and repurposed by people.
  - G194: PLAY narrows the error bars over unfamiliar situations, and shared play brings people closer.
  - G195, the analyst's proposal: PLAY's reward is compression progress.
- **Two makers (F4):** AI-assisted work is attributed per decision.
  - D-AI-MIDDLE: the AI-made middle carries the maker's deeper goals only through the prompt, the person's selections and corrections, and fills with the model's defaults.
  - D-EDIT-GUARD: examined acceptance and editing protect higher goals.
  - D-ACCEPT-EXPERTISE: acceptances carry expertise; the analyst adds that they show it only where the options differ in what the expert sees.
- **Readers converge with context (F5):** a theorem with a condition (HH-READER-CONVERGENCE). The analyst's gloss is that a reader's own habit is the reference, not the forecast.
- **Aesthetics in three terms (F6):** pleasure at better-than-expected ease, interest as compression progress, and extrinsic value, the pull of bodily and social set points (HH-AESTHETIC-TERMS). The split is the analyst's, and it departs from the curator's placement of food.
- **Attention (F7):**
  - Attention mixes a pull, capture and voluntary direction (HH-ATTENTION-SOURCES, revised). Capture by drives or by learned value is the analyst's reading.
  - Reversals come in two kinds (HH-REVERSAL-KINDS).
- **Entries and sacrifice (F8):**
  - The three entry points are one mechanism, weighted by reliability (HH-ENTRY-RELIABILITY).
  - Sacrifice reads current priority, not structure (HH-SACRIFICE-ORDER).
- **The person (F9):** the person probably shows up more in how they work than in what they choose (G197, the analyst's proposal).
- **Bounded, not irrational (F10):** context sits inside the bound, and the bounds have to be measured independently of the choices they explain.
- **Duplicated structures (F11):** recurring structures read as copies recomputed across depth (G196).
- **The alignment target (F12):** what learned ends mean for it.

([Ginges et al., 2007](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/17460042), read). Order is also visible

same overarching purpose
([Shah, Friedman & Kruglanski, 2002](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/12500810), read). Two orders
therefore stay apart, structural order, whose reference is set by whom, and conflict priority, who

````text
**Duplicated structures** *(2026-10-16)*:

> This stapling of solution channels in between everything in the middle of other problems is
> something that I do wonder whether it has relevance to, like, mechanistic interpretability. Do we
> have the same structures that show up multiple times, and you have these ghosts of multiple
> structures, and these transformations people are doing are simply various ways of getting at these
> multiplying duplicated structures? What if they're multiplied because they're used in multiple
> locations, not because it's the same concept informing everything?

*Curator's walkthrough of the analyst's 16 October theory record, 2026-10-16 (12:57 PDT), in the project's theory and analyst thread; filed {FILED}. Contiguous excerpt; transcription repairs listed in the handoff.*

Across positions the question has a settled answer. A transformer runs the same weights at every
position, so a feature seen at many positions is one copy applied many times. Across depth the
conjecture has support. Networks learn transformed copies of a feature along any axis where weights
are not shared, and vision models learn rotated copies of curve detectors while convolution removes
the need for translated ones ([Olah et al., 2020](https://distill.pub/2020/circuits/equivariance/),
read). In a two-hop composition task a transformer stores a second copy of facts for use as the
second hop, because its untied layers cannot share memory, and sharing parameters across layers
unlocks generalization the standard model does not reach
([Wang et al., 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.15071), read). In real models the second hop
sometimes resolves too late, in layers that lack the needed knowledge, and patching a later state
back into an earlier layer fixes up to 66 percent of those failures
([Biran et al., 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.781), abstract read). The rivals have
direct evidence too. Features persist through the residual stream, and crosscoders exist to merge
duplicate features across layers
([Anthropic, 2024](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2024/crosscoders/index.html), read); backup
heads take over when the main ones are ablated
([Wang et al., 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.00593), read); similar layers ensemble
([Liu et al., 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.05970), read); and dictionary methods can fake copies
or absences ([Chanin et al., 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.14507), read).

The choice the conjecture poses is partly false, since a copy at another depth is both the same
concept and used in another place. The live contrast is one copy read many times, as a recurrent
brain can manage with single regions active across most task domains in a meta-analysis
([Anderson, 2010](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X10000853), read), against one recomputed at each
depth, which a single forward pass forces. Stapled solutions are functions, so single-pass models
should duplicate them wherever depth demands, and looped models or models that reason in tokens
should need fewer copies; that prediction is the analyst's. The affect subspace below rotates
continuously through depth (G40), which a recomputed copy and a persisting feature would both
produce; telling them apart needs evidence on which later components read each copy.
````
