## 0. Executive ruling

The current theory is already substantially aligned with the latest walkthrough. It already
contains the following corrections and must not receive duplicates of them:

- model, engineered human-shaped, and human invertibility are reader-qualified measurements;
- self-reconstruction is useful because similarity supplies a cheap prior, not because projection
  is historically true;
- viewer-coherent reconstruction, a reader-enactable route, and historical process are distinct
  outputs inside the process inference;
- human-readable is graded and relational rather than binary;
- context reweights candidate explanations rather than causing one-step induction;
- conserved affective machinery narrows a distributed solution space rather than containing the
  whole emotional episode;
- mirror-compatible action representation is one evidence channel rather than magical mind
  transfer;
- roughly 27 is a soft descriptive ceiling and not a primitive count;
- drives may bundle inherited strategy and state-dependent motivational pressure, and that
  construct boundary remains unresolved;
- a model-family similarity effect is an engineered analogue and not human evidence;
- ordinary model non-invertibility and deliberately optimized human invertibility are compatible
  claims about different production regimes.

This pass therefore makes only four substantive changes and one evidence fold-in:

1. **The process target becomes explicitly distributional.** A strong reconstruction is not one
   plausible route. It increasingly constrains the maker's conditional distribution over choices,
   including paths not taken, under goals, context, tools, expertise, and history.
2. **Maker access to the focal goal is separated from access to auxiliary motivation.** The maker
   may remember what occupied attention, while still having to infer background goals and compiled
   expertise from their own output. The expert reader lacks the memory channel but is solving the
   same target family.
3. **Three empathy distinctions are kept related but non-identical.** Self-based versus
   target-specific evidence, fast versus slow processing, and affective sharing versus cognitive
   mentalizing may correlate. They may not be collapsed into one serial mechanism.
4. **The proposed indoctrination interval enters as an open reader-updating hypothesis.** A reader
   may acquire an enactable process before source and value appraisal finish. Process uptake,
   belief uptake, and value change remain separate outcomes. This narrows one previously deferred
   issue; it does not canonize the full trust, epistemic-disgust, or plasticity-gating model.
5. **The family-similarity finding receives its two completed discovery follow-ups.** The gradient
   survives accepted Qwen paraphrase at reduced magnitude, capacity remains a poor simple rival,
   and Qwen-paraphraser circularity prevents claim promotion.

## 2. Fidelity protocol for reconstructed curator language

The source is lossy speech-to-text. Punctuation, false sentence boundaries, fillers, repeated
starts, and obvious recognition errors have been repaired. Content has not been made cleaner,
more categorical, or more technically precise than the speech supports.

| class | meaning | may enter a living-theory blockquote? |
|---|---|---|
| **A: high-fidelity reconstruction** | One contiguous thought with transcript debris removed and no proposition added | Yes, exactly as printed in this package |
| **B: composite reconstruction** | One position assembled from adjacent statements or repeated passes; semantically faithful, but wording partly reconstructed | No, unless the curator later adopts it verbatim |
| **C: analyst formulation** | A consequence, distinction, or guardrail inferred from the speech and evidence | Never; use only as AI-authored prose |

Permitted cleanup:

- punctuation and paragraph boundaries;
- removal of fillers, repeated starts, and transcription debris;
- restoration of obvious terms such as Panksepp, PyTorch, and epistemic foraging;
- selection of the intended word where the surrounding sentence makes the recognition error clear;
- removal of a duplicated phrase where no content is lost.

Not permitted:

- joining separate tensions into a resolution and calling it a quote;
- converting “I think,” “I would expect,” “may,” or “I do not know” into certainty;
- replacing the curator's ordinary vocabulary with a more elegant technical term;
- treating transcript disorder as disorder in the underlying model;
- editing an existing quote to resemble a newer reconstruction;
- silently using analyst language inside a curator blockquote.

## 4. Research boundary for the empathy-route addition

The literature supports a relationship among the curator's proposed axes, but not their collapse
into one binary or one serial neural story.

| distinction | evidence that may enter | boundary that must remain |
|---|---|---|
| **assumed similarity versus direct accuracy** | In three daily-diary samples totaling 262 couples, direct accuracy was associated with slower responses while assumed similarity was not ([Sened et al., 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36042967/)) | The timing contrast does not establish that assumed similarity is always fast; neither route is identical to affective or cognitive empathy |
| **self-based versus target-differentiated mentalizing** | Self-referential medial prefrontal activity tracked mentalizing about people rated similar to self, while similar and dissimilar targets recruited dissociable medial prefrontal regions ([Mitchell et al., 2005](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16197685/); [Mitchell et al., 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16701214/)) | Cognitive mentalizing does sometimes use self-information. It cannot be defined as purely non-self-based |
| **emotional versus cognitive empathy** | Lesion evidence produced a double dissociation between emotional-empathy and cognitive-empathy impairments ([Shamay-Tsoory et al., 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18971202/)) | A dissociation does not prove independence, a fixed processing order, or a one-to-one speed mapping |
| **experience sharing versus mentalizing cues** | Linguistic information improved empathic accuracy, visual-only presentation produced more physiological synchrony, and both routes were active in the combined condition ([Jospe et al., 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33010739/)) | Sensorimotor sharing can contribute without being the sole or strongest accuracy channel; verbal target evidence remains load-bearing |
| **sensorimotor contribution to higher inference** | Right-lateralized sensorimotor mu suppression tracked empathic accuracy, with the replication limited to individualized bands and visual stimuli ([Genzer et al., 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35137224/)) | This supports a contribution under some cue conditions, not a complete mirror-neuron account |

The safe canonical synthesis is therefore:

> **Self-based initialization, target-specific correction, processing speed, affective sharing, and
> cognitive mentalizing are partially related coordinates of human social inference. They are not
> interchangeable labels.**

That sentence is analyst prose, not a curator quote. The empirical sources concern interpersonal
mental-state judgments, not finished artifacts. Their transfer to artifact inversion remains an
open hypothesis.
