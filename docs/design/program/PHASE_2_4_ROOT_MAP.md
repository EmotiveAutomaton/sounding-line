## 3. The strongest rival account of the entire pattern

That Stage 1 measured **familiarity with a dialect, not shared generative organization**:
Qwen readers recognize Qwen phrasing, tokenization rhythm, and formulaic habits, so the
graded pattern would survive every control Stage 1 ran while meaning nothing about
organization — and the affect ruler's one surviving grain would be a topic regularity
rather than affect abstraction. This rival is fully testable: it predicts the similarity
gradient collapses under goal-preserving paraphrase (the corpus's mechanical verification
survives paraphrase by construction, so the test is clean), and it predicts a second
maker family would show reader-family-specific advantages rather than a general
similarity law.

## 4. Open theory questions (four, open-ended, before any interpretation)

1. **If the gradient survives trace erasure, what is the human-side content of
   "organization"?** The analogue would then say: relatedness of generative machinery, not
   surface habit, carries inversion advantage. What would you take that to mean for the
   human claim — closeness as shared machinery, expertise as shared machinery, both, or
   something the model analogue cannot carry at all?
2. **If only the exact checkpoint's advantage survived erasure and the sibling gradient
   collapsed, is that self-reconstruction's degenerate form or evidence against the
   shared-organization account?** The theory's own quote says self-reconstruction works
   through similarity; a reader that can only invert itself would be the shortcut with
   the similarity term deleted.
3. **The affect wing's instrument died at power, not at concept — the scrubbed decoding
   grain existed.** Does the theory hold that the conserved-constraint contribution
   SHOULD be readable in a 1.5B reader, or is there a scale below which the ghost is
   expected to be absent? The answer decides whether the rebuilt ruler is a repair or a
   different bet.
4. **The prospective human-process interface is where projection goes to die — and
   everything sits near its floor there.** Is a low prospective ceiling itself a theory
   result (live modeling is the hard part, historical reading the easy part), or an
   instrument gap (no validated reader has actually been aimed at it yet)?
