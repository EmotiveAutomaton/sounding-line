## Scores, gates and reporting

Every result preserves the existing expected half-Brier score, its exact
reference floor, excess error, uniform and applicable cheap rivals, invalid
penalty one and infinite logarithmic losses. Inverse label mappings are verified
before scoring. Copy fidelity, selection information, current forecast quality,
regret and ranking are distinct outcomes. Known exact, wrong-answer, duplicate,
missing-candidate and pre-operation-visibility controls can fail the rulers.

Loss contrasts use paired source-cluster means and fixed-seed 4,000-draw
bootstrap intervals. Benefit/harm requires the entire interval below/above
minus/plus 0.02; equivalence requires it wholly inside that margin; all other
complete comparisons are unresolved. Missing source, defective ruler and
incomplete attempt remain blocked, void and incomplete respectively. LP08's
decision margin is 0.01 utility units. The two LP21 primary contrasts use
97.5% intervals each; no p-values are introduced. Development contrasts remain
exploratory and are not promoted by selecting a favorable subgroup.

LP03 uses a fixed abstention grid 0, .25, .50, .60, .70, .80, .90, 1 and five
fixed confidence bins. It reports invalid denominators, coverage, retained
error, expected correctness and score components, including the finite-bin
remainder rather than falsely claiming an exact binned calibration identity.
ARIES positive/negative ranking retains invalid pair coverage and lexical rivals.
No calibrator or threshold is fitted to the evaluation outcomes.

Every native completion requires actual request/parser replay. Raw failed
attempts, unknown reservations and prior packets remain. Full scientific
write-through happens internally for whole cells; operational updates contain
queue health and coverage, not unfinished per-artifact scores. Final synthesis
must distinguish complete cells, failed instruments and unavailable branches.

Setup status, exact runtime identities, health receipts and publication evidence
belong in the current state and inspection records. A compiler census, synthetic
rehearsal or queued manifest alone is not a running experiment.
