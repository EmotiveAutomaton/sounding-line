## MI4: preserve the level comparison

Operator question: One MIDUS instruction conflicts with the question it asks:
subtracting each person's usual mood would erase the stable mood differences
needed to test whether extraverted people are happier in general. May I preserve
those differences for that one comparison, while keeping the requested
within-person adjustment for the other analyses? The PDF codebooks are sufficient;
this is a design clarification.

Curator answer: **“Preserve differences for that comparison (Recommended)”**

The baseline comparison retains differences between people's usual positive
feelings. The event-related lift and other analyses retain their specified
within-person treatment. The primary remains the difference between the two
registered correlations. The approved exception does not change the remaining
MIDUS designs or permit a different source population.

The existing PDF codebooks supply the dictionary information needed for mapping.
No replacement export is required. Packet amendment AE still governs actual
MIDUS execution: the curator runs the script and verifier locally; only eligible
aggregate totals return. Constructed qualification and the combined script remain
separate from participant execution.

## ES3: compare short-gap with long-gap carry-over

Operator question: For the diary follow-up asking whether stronger reactions go
with longer-lasting feelings, the packet leaves “carry-over” ambiguous. May I
compare each person's anger reactivity with their anger carry-over at short gaps
minus long gaps? This retains the main study's adjustment for a generally bad day;
using carry-over pooled across all gaps would answer a different question. The
main diary studies can continue independently.

Curator answer: **“Use the short-minus-long comparison (Recommended)”**

The person-level secondary compares anger reactivity with that person's short-gap
minus long-gap anger carry-over. The packet's strict gaps under 90 minutes and
over 240 minutes remain. The primary diary comparisons and generally-bad-day
adjustment are preserved. No new decision threshold or theoretical verdict is
created by this clarification.
