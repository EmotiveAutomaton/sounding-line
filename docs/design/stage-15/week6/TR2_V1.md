## Recipe, controls and reporting boundary

Carry TR1's order: qualify the source-specific reliability instrument; estimate
true between-person variance; require its full interval strictly above zero;
then interpret corrected stability. TR1's two candidate reliability formulas
are observed variance minus mean sampling variance, and Spearman–Brown
`2r/(1+r)` from within-occasion halves. Correct cross-occasion correlation by
the square root of the product of the two reliabilities. All quantities remain
unclipped, including negative corrected variance and ratios above one.

TR1 chooses between estimators on its three planted worlds (0, 0.5, 1), with
all errors within 0.1 and smallest 0.5 error winning; sampling wins exact ties.
Its 12-rating OLS/AR(1) model is specific to the phone game. This scaffold does
not transplant that model into binary refusal indicators, per-writer word
slopes or POS fractions. Independent algebra alone does not qualify their
sampling covariance. Setting-specific constructed worlds, support and control
mapping remain required before any human effect or claim.

The inherited TR1 bands are retained: full stability interval above 0.5 gives
"supports a stable sensitivity"; wholly below 0.2 gives "not stable at the
registered band"; entirely inside closed [0.2, 0.5] gives "partly stable";
all other intervals are inconclusive. This is TR1's explicit card-specific
rule, not the general ±bar classification of the appraisal cards. A true
variance interval touching zero reports that no between-person differences
could be measured in this task before any stability interpretation.

Primaries require 2,000 whole-unit resamples, including multiplicities and all
failed draws, complete separate replay, and the sensitivity quantities from
their own bootstrap SE. No draws, p values or sensitivity claim are implemented
by the coefficient fixture. D33 retains its source-specific timing controls;
the CoAuthor reliability mapping must resolve Week 3/4 within-prompt writer
label controls and Week 5 §0.6's CoAuthor timing inheritance explicitly. The
appraisal-card control ruling does not waive controls here. Order-invariant
word-count permutations cannot serve as empirical reliability nulls. Exact
source-specific applicability is pending with the final estimators, not waived.
