For each person in a conversation, the partner rates that person’s humor, how much they like the person and the person’s status. Estimate the association of rated humor with liking and its association with status. The registered contrast is the liking coefficient minus the status coefficient. The predicted direction is positive and the practical bar is 0.05 standardized units. This specification precedes the first scientific run on these ratings.
