# KP1, KP2 and KP3: prospective shared handler

This is a builder proposal prepared without decoding new human rows or computing
new scientific outcomes. The owner reviews it and the exact proposed files.
It does not alter the prior D33 fit, records, cohort or live workers.

## Question and method

KP1 asks whether a writer's unusually long pause, after the frozen word-surprise
model is removed, predicts a nearby deletion or an unexpected continuation.
KP2 asks whether own-history or matched-donor surprise predicts held-out pauses
better. KP3 asks whether time since the writer last typed a word predicts its
pause after the number of earlier uses and the original covariates are held.
The statistical readouts are associations and held-out prediction; none identifies
an attention swap, causal readiness mechanism or higher-order schema change.

Authority is the Week 6 extension, its filed predictions and the owner's scoped
commission. The Round 24 handoff is interpretive source evidence. Its statement
that a future result would supply an attention marker is not causal validation.
No external literature lookup or new literature verdict is claimed here.

## DESIGN CHECK

LESSONS sections 3–5 were reviewed for statistics, model interfaces and runtime;
section 2's source-schema rules also apply. A null world has deletion odds ratio
one, a predictive own-minus-donor advantage of zero, and recency coefficient zero.
Positive and reversed plants preserve their signs. Sentence-boundary-only worlds
must expose the raw omitted-boundary trap. An undefined fit is retained as invalid,
not resampled until it works. Source joins preserve original keys, never select on
the new outcome, and distinguish censoring from an observed zero.

Primary classifications are exhaustive: complete valid positive interval plus
the practical/control threshold gives supported association; an interval wholly
below zero (one for odds) gives opposite association; a positive interval failing
the practical or control threshold is positive below bar/control; an interval
touching/crossing zero is unresolved. A missing fit/draw or failed control is
invalid. Exact threshold equality does not clear an “above” rule. Fewer than
20 eligible independent writers invokes the inherited descriptive fallback and
never masquerades as a null. Continuous terms remain within-writer centered;
the pooled fallback uses a common intercept, remains strictly descriptive and
does not claim the writer-adjusted logistic association. No eligible observation
returns unsupported without generating an artificial bank of zero effects.

The case is testable in constructed word/event logs where a retained D33 row has
an unambiguous onset, six following typed words and measured deletion provenance.
Chronology, UTF-16/codepoint conversion, suggestions, future history and missing
boundaries have explicit fixtures. The smaller route, if measured complete cost
exceeds the authorized allowance, is the packet's whole-writer frozen sample
route with all incurred costs retained. No automatic sampling is implemented and
no lower population, changed seed or cap extension is silently selected.

Operational mapping proposed for exact owner review:

- The complete P6 typed-word stream determines “next words”, not the D33 eligible
  subset and not surviving final text. Words later revised remain in that stream.
- A user-origin contiguous deletion operation must remove at least three Unicode
  codepoints. Disjoint deletes are not added together. API deletion is not a
  writer repair. Prompt/API characters removed by a qualifying user deletion are
  a separately disclosed provenance category; they are included in the any-deletion
  primary and not falsely labeled as human-typed content.
- A deletion window is strictly after the focal onset event and strictly before
  the sixth following word's onset. Missing end boundaries and ambiguous tied
  onsets are not zero outcomes. Deletions can touch earlier and later typed text;
  those two reported flags are nonexclusive and mixed provenance is counted.
- Suggestion open/reopen, or a suggestion selection with inserted text, starts
  the five-word exclusion. A same-delta word is included in that exclusion as
  temporally ambiguous exposure. The source parser preserves ordinal chronology.
- KP3 counts all original P6 typed-word occurrences in the writer's strictly
  earlier completed sessions, including revised occurrences. Last-use time is the
  greatest matching original word-onset timestamp; current/future sessions do not
  contribute. A word absent from earlier sessions is excluded with that reason.
- Raw pause comparison means raw *log* pause standardized within writer, matching
  the frozen response scale, not raw seconds with a new per-second estimand.

## Registered calculations

KP1 residualization uses the exact saved D33 coefficients and variance ratio.
The random writer intercept is reconstructed by the Gaussian conditional mean
from the original evaluation rows; residuals are then standardized within writer
before per-rider exclusions. The source fit is frozen through bootstrap draws.
All nuisance columns and the signed own/donor terms are retained up to their
exact linear span. Focal remainder must remain separately identifiable.

The primary is a logistic coefficient exponentiated to odds per one within-writer
SD. It requires estimate above 1.10, a writer-bootstrap interval above one and
estimate above the 97.5th percentile of all 200 fixed within-writer remainder
shuffles. Any undefined control prevents this support claim. The 2,000 fixed
bootstrap draws resample whole writers; duplicated draws receive distinct writer
intercepts. No controls or draws are dropped/replaced.

The shared grid is: any deletion; deletion touching earlier typed text; deletion
touching the focal/following text; mean personal surprise in the next three words;
the two principal outcomes away from sentence/punctuation boundaries; the two
principal outcomes with standardized raw log pause; and both nonboundary outcomes
with standardized raw log pause. The continuation bar is 0.02 nats per SD.
The secondary rows remain labeled secondary; only the stated primary receives
the packet's three-part primary declaration.

KP2 holds out every entire session once. Each training fold defines each writer's
mean of response and predictors; test rows subtract those training means only.
The base model removes both personal-surprise columns. The other two arms add
own alone or matched donor alone. The generalized mixed-model solver preserves
D33's Gaussian random-intercept likelihood, ratio interval, numerical tolerance,
zero-variance boundary and nuisance-span handling. Predictive squared errors are
paired on the same held-out words. Primary = 100 × sum(donor error − own error)
/ sum(base error). The fixed writer bootstrap resamples the retained paired
prediction errors. It does not re-fit using held-out outcomes. The sign of the
interval identifies own ahead, donor ahead or unresolved at these histories.

KP3 adds log prior-use count and log(1 + hours since last prior use) to the D33
joint predictor set, after within-writer centering. Both new terms must remain
separately identifiable. It refits the joint Gaussian random-intercept model in
every fixed writer draw. The recency point must exceed 0.01 and its interval must
be above zero. Same-session recency does not substitute for earlier-session use.

## Important interpretation and implementation limits

With the same D33 covariates in both adjusted models, residual pause and raw log
pause span the same regression space. Their per-SD coefficients can differ by
scale, but an adjusted fit comparison cannot by itself establish that removing
surprise adds predictive information. The fixture checks this exact algebra.
The packet can test repair association and unexpected continuation separately;
those outcomes do not observe the proposed attention or schema mechanism.

Source occurrence identity is inherited, including revised tokens that can share
characters. Ambiguous within-delta boundaries are flagged; event order is not
equated to millisecond-resolved individual keypress order. This is a scope limit,
not permission to manufacture observations. The full eligibility census remains
private and aggregate exclusion counts accompany every final report.

KP1b has a numerical length/boundary fallback fixture at OR 1.10 and null, but no
human-source adapter is claimed or enabled. Its independent-writer mapping,
masked-character status and exact word reconstruction must authenticate first.
No writer identity is inferred from source-session filenames.
