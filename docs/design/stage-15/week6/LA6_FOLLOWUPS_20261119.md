## LA6.m: incremental history information

## DESIGN CHECK

Prior literature and scope review: the inherited LA6 design and G277's attributed
source history remain the background; no novelty or new literature review is
claimed. LESSONS sections 2, 3 and 5 and the original known-answer design govern
implementation. Null: identical feature distributions give area one half; paired
identical predictions give exactly zero difference. Alternative: separable
constructed labels can clear the original bar and a planted history improvement
has the expected paired sign. Adversarial fixtures must reject future context,
unfinished overlapping history, train/test leakage, inconsistent reused-control
labels and mis-scaled regularization. Boundary fixtures must distinguish an onset
at zero, one and two seconds and a pre-existing overlapping laugh. These gates
guard false positive prediction and attribution, not merely numerical success.
Fewer than twenty meetings is descriptive, and absent act categories keep the
original unsupported disposition. A cost forecast above the allowance is filed
before any reduced-scope successor; no unrecorded population change is allowed.
