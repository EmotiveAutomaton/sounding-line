## Part B: five paired out-of-writer comparisons

The fixed ordered pairs are pride/joy, guilt/shame, fear/sadness,
anger/disgust and trust/joy. Each pair uses five GroupKFold folds, with every
writer wholly outside her training folds. All transformations are trained
only on each training fold. The rating arm standardizes its 21 columns using
training population SD plus the preflight's 1e-9 guard, then fits logistic
regression with C=1, lbfgs and 1,000 iterations. The word arm fits TF-IDF
unigrams/bigrams, minimum document frequency 2 and sublinear term frequency,
then logistic regression with C=10, lbfgs and 2,000 iterations. Convergence
warnings fail the fit. An empty training vocabulary has a declared
intercept-only prediction; it is not filled with a target-informed score.

Compute AUC separately in each held-out fold, average the five folds equally,
subtract ratings from words, then average all five pair contrasts equally.
This matches the preflight's fold averaging rather than pooling scores from
different fitted models. The primary bar is 0.05. Draw 2,000 whole writers
from the union of the five pair rosters, using the same draw across every
pair and arm. Reweight the fixed held-out predictions, preserving their
original folds. This interval is conditional on the frozen fitted folds;
it does not include learner retraining uncertainty. Any draw losing either
class in any held-out fold invalidates the primary interval. Retain its
index; never redraw it or average fewer pairs. Insufficient five-fold
support is explicitly unsupported and cannot be silently replaced by
in-sample predictive performance.

Secondaries are the six largest absolute standardized appraisal gaps and
thirty unigram coefficients in each direction from a full-pair descriptive
fit (minimum document frequency 5, English stop words, C=10). These have no
independent claim verdict. The full-pair descriptive vocabulary does not
enter the held-out primary pipeline.
