is cumulative, not summed again over revisions. Excluding the current attempt
does not exclude its card's preparation. No old card or family is renamed.

The approved OPEN, FREEZE, DRAIN and ADDENDUM timestamps remain November 16

## Why a migration is required before ledger writes

Both old policy readers reject unknown families in either an unclaimed PLAN
**or a preparation budget receipt**. Adding a new family while an old worker
monitors its budget can therefore fail that otherwise healthy worker. The new
launcher guards `freeze_budget` as well as `freeze_plan`, and runtime claim
checks again under the actual admission mutex. The builder creates neither.

Every claimed/owned old Week 6 attempt must first have one authentic terminal,
the original owner/plan hash binding, all captured native identities verified
exited or demonstrably replaced, and a separately pinned owner filing receipt
binding every inspected terminal/native metadata hash. Access-denied/unknown
native queries refuse. Paused checkpoints are preserved, not promoted to complete.
Unclaimed old plans also enter the frozen inventory so a later old plan cannot
appear unnoticed. Native metadata includes the bespoke TR2 `phases/` roots;
scientific output rows are not scanned by this transition guard.

An immutable `WEEK6_R18_TRANSITION_REVIEW_V1` owner review records the exact
legacy inventory, filing receipts, original budget pins, mandatory old/new
runtime and authority source pins, pending already-incurred preparation charges,
and retired legacy launch/write workflows. Every new plan pins the review's
path and SHA. A fresh fail-closed census inspects workspace Python processes;
unexplained helpers or old readers refuse. No process is stopped or reclaimed.
Known new-runtime native owners/children must bind an extension PLAN. The
reviewed current caller is exempt from its own census. Native/metadata errors
are never translated into zero cost or absent processes.

This is a cooperative owner-controlled workflow, not an OS ban on launching
arbitrary old programs. Retiring old writers is an explicit review obligation;
the guard rechecks for late old workers and newly appearing old plans. The owner
must route all subsequent budget writes and starts through this generation.
Unknown helpers are investigated, not broadly allowlisted to make a start pass.

Known fixture/preparation costs that could not yet be posted safely to a new
family are carried in `pending_preparation`. The guarded budget writer can post
their exact reviewed revisions after migration; study admission waits until all
those costs and their evidence pins are present. All earlier budget ancestry
files must remain unchanged. No allocation, receipt or cost is auto-generated.

## Carried workers and sealed fixture boundary

Generic v1/v2 workers continue only after an ordinary authenticated PAUSED
checkpoint and full old-reader exit. Create a new reviewed attempt using the
same scientific module, inputs, semantic settings and budget key, preserving
every predecessor pin and the original `week6_resume_v1` checks. Runtime-only
pins are added. Do not change an existing plan or bypass the old binding.

The bespoke TR2 archive runtime is a budget reader too. Its isolated successor
changes exactly two imports: policy and inherited claim/runtime. Its source
handler, child, extraction, phase-native proof, checkpoint protocol, workspace,
source-runtime identifier and original source pins remain unchanged. It still
requires its own exact ordinary PAUSED handover and source-runtime admission;
do not route that two-phase protocol through a generic scientific handler.

For MI, runtime admission additionally requires `fixture_only=true`,
`data_access=constructed-fixture-only`, and a pinned
`MIDUS_CONSTRUCTED_FIXTURE_V1` manifest binding the exact inputs and module,
declaring no real rows and MR1 refusal. This complements the reviewed fixture
generator; a flag is not proof of the origin of arbitrary bytes. The owner must
review the generator and source manifest. Real MIDUS rows remain outside this
operator's route; the curator runs the sealed study separately, and later
aggregate import is a separate authorized action.
