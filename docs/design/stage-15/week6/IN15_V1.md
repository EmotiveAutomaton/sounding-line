## Primary statistic

For each of the twelve observed feelings, standardize its prior rating over
all retained reader-response rows using the sample SD. These full-source
scales remain fixed through bootstrap draws. A repeated reader can have a
different pre-task state on different reports; her answers are not collapsed
to a trait score. A missing prior column or nonfinite value is refused, never
imputed. A constant prior feeling is reported as unsupported and makes the
remaining primary incomplete/descriptive.

For every report/feeling cell in which at least one of the five readers named
the feeling and at least one did not, subtract the nonchoosers' mean prior
from the choosers' mean prior. Average the eligible cells equally. The bar is
0.10 prior-rating SD. Thirteen choices exist but only twelve prior feelings:
`no-emotion` has no matching prior rating, contributes no primary cell, and
its choices are counted explicitly. Its readers still contribute as
nonchoosers for the twelve feelings. A synthetic thirteenth prior appears
only in the separate supplied-fixture oracle, never in the real consumer.

Resample whole reader identities 2,000 times. Every report for a sampled
reader travels with her, carrying the reader's draw multiplicity. Within
each draw recompute weighted chooser and nonchooser means, recompute which
cells retain both groups, and average that draw's eligible cells. The owner
accepted this operationalization prospectively. Record every draw's total
and per-feeling eligible-cell counts, so changing support is visible.
A draw with no eligible cell remains a failed draw and prevents a primary
interval; it is not redrawn or silently dropped. Fewer than 20 independent
reader clusters yields pooled descriptive inference; fewer than two refuses
population uncertainty. Support failure is distinct from a measured null.

## Descriptive companions

Report the same statistic by feeling. Two further descriptive slopes use
within-report weighted centering, so reports' differing difficulty or content
does not supply the association. The first relates mean standardized prior
negative state (anger, boredom, disgust, fear, guilt, sadness, shame) to the
reader's unpleasantness rating. Its unit is unpleasantness points per SD of
that mean negative state. The second relates the mean of all twelve
standardized prior feelings to matching the writer's label, in correct-label
probability per SD of the mean prior state. These definitions make the
packet's otherwise unspecified secondary scale explicit before outcomes.
Their scales stay fixed and they receive the same whole-reader draws;
they carry no separate supports/against verdict or causal claim.

The primary reports its estimate, interval, bar, bootstrap standard error,
2.8 times that error (sees) and the bar plus that quantity (supports at),
with the normal-approximation limitation. The Week 5 three-way rule gives
supports for a whole interval strictly beyond the bar, against for a whole
interval opposite zero or inside the closed practical band, and inconclusive
otherwise. Exact zero is against within the band; band-null is only an optional
description of that reason. The November 15 label-only correction preserves
all original fixture banks and changes no estimates, intervals or bars.
Failed uncertainty cannot be promoted to a verdict. The first report line
must say limited sensitivity when sees exceeds the bar.
