## Design check

LESSONS §§2–5. Form L uses within-person centered predictors and outcomes, scaled by their pooled within-person standard deviations. A whole-person resample preserves every selected person’s complete reports and recomputes the scale. Original estimation sums centered products by person, and replay uses weighted least squares on individual records. The partial-correlation replay computes correlations from the residuals. Shrinking the matched roster or changing item polarity can reverse a contrast. Constructed checks exercise these cases, a planted sign, an exact null, a singular design, missing ratings and repeated person weights.
