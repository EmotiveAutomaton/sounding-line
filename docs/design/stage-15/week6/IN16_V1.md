## Conditional writer share

Select writers with at least three reports in the selected source scope,
independently of inventory availability. Fit intensity with prompt intercepts,
all 21 appraisals and a random writer intercept. The reported share is
tau-squared / (tau-squared + residual-sigma-squared), conditional on the fixed
effects. There is no additional gate for this descriptive share.

The estimator is restricted maximum likelihood (REML). Profile the residual
scale and fixed coefficients; optimize log(1 + tau-squared/sigma-squared) in
[0,12], including the zero boundary explicitly. Reaching the upper bound,
rank loss, missing residual variance or failed convergence is a retained failure,
not a repaired result. For a writer with n rows the residual covariance after
factoring out sigma-squared is I + lambda times the all-ones matrix. Production
uses the corresponding sufficient-statistic/Woodbury form; independent replay
builds and solves each writer's covariance block directly. Every bootstrap
draw refits the fixed effects and variance ratio. Repeated sampled writers are
independent copies represented by cluster multiplicities, not a merged larger
writer. The residual-share definition is not variance explained by prompt.
