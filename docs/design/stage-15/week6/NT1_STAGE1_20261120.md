# Carry into the next diary prompt after criticism

Round 32 assigns NT1.d and NT1.e to the large status diary (0057). NT1.d asks how much more often people report a bad feeling after a criticised interaction, once criticism at the current prompt and other status events are accounted for. NT1.e reports the same lag coefficient by elapsed-time thirds. [Curator's build order, section 4](PACKET_EXTENSION_2026-11-20.md)

## Source and support

Bind the 21 required fields to the member's reviewed codebook and physical table. The first projection reads identifiers, both timestamps, study day, context and the six status ratings. All recorded writers with a valid identifier enter the person roster, including those with no eligible pair. Only adjacent answered prompts can form a pair. A missing clock, tied answer times, inverted clock or inconsistent study day prevents that person's chronology from being used. The study day uses elapsed UTC creation dates, as in the existing carry study. A missing context or event cannot be bridged by reaching back one more prompt.

The previous prompt must be an interaction on the same study day, at most four hours before the current answer. A missing status rating at an interaction excludes the pair. For a solitary current prompt, interaction-event exposures are zero. An interaction rating at its floor also denotes zero exposure. Count the pairs where criticism at the earlier prompt was above its floor and the distinct people who supplied those pairs. Write the support counts and hash before opening the ten feeling columns. If fewer than 300 such pairs or 150 such people qualify, file the shortfall and end this stage. Keep both the predictor-eligible counts and the subsequent outcome-complete counts.

## Estimates and return

The primary outcome is one if any of the five feelings exceeds its floor in the current context. It is zero when all five are recorded at their floors. With missing items and no known positive it is unknown. Fit Form O with criticism and the other five events at both prompts, elapsed hours and current context. Include within-person standardized terms and their person means. Use 0057's indexed whole-person draw bank for 2,000 fits and reconstruct all 2,000 independently. Preserve a singular or unidentified coefficient as missing.

NT1.d's practical bar is 0.10 log-odds with its interval above zero. Opening the trait stage separately requires a verified positive lag interval and a lag point at least 0.30 times a positive current-prompt point. NT1.e's thirds and cut points use the predictor-eligible pairs before outcomes. Ties go into the lower third. Its three fits use the common units of the overall outcome-complete sample and are recorded with their counts and a single digest.

**Design check.** Constructed cases cover missing and tied clocks, day transitions, the four-hour edge, skipped prompts, exposure-specific support, missing feelings, whole-person weights, independent regression and the distinction between the practical bar and the trait-stage gate. Failure of any binding or support test leaves the trait file unopened. The trait product and RATIO use the supplied ten-world check only if the registered stage-two gate opens.

**Limit.** The same person reports criticism and feelings. The next report concerns a new interaction or activity. It cannot tell whether the earlier feeling continued, a new event produced it, or reporting changed.
