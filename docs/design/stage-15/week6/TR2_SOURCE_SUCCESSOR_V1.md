Why this explicit construction matters: a never-trading indicator over k

exposure alone when all people have the same choice propensity. A common
three-choice repeat removes that mechanical difference before reliability.

For independent replicate indicators R1 and R2 with the same conditional
probability q, the outer mean has noise variance q(1-q)/2. An unbiased estimate
for that noise variance is `(R1-R2)^2/4`. Average it across the original
fingerprints for TR1's sampling-variance correction. The second candidate is
TR1's split-half Spearman–Brown correction between those same two equal-exposure
replicates. The independence is **between disjoint session groups**, not an
assertion that the three choices inside a group are independent. Group-level
exchangeability and a common within-half q are substantive assumptions and
must be tested by the prospective constructed bank.
