# Local research review independent of publication

The curator explicitly removed the reviewed-commit prerequisite on November 19,
2026, and directed that qualified research continue while publication is deferred.
This is a prospective operational override of amendments K and Q and historical
packet wording. It does not change scientific designs, privacy, native ownership,
gear, campaign dates, budget ceilings, source qualifications or final reporting.

## DESIGN CHECK

LESSONS sections 3, 4 and 5 were reread for this implementation. Scientific null
and alternative receive identical treatment. The successful case is an exact
owner-reviewed local plan with complete passed native qualification. Missing,
failed, changed, wrong-owner or wrong-scope evidence refuses admission. A Git
commit or push supplies no substitute for those checks and is no longer required.

The immutable local manifest binds every plan field except its own self-reference,
the reviewed design and complete source closure, this authority, the owner and
review time, exact successful constructed receipts and their actual positive case
counts, source/output hashes, released leases and exited native identities.
Launch and worker admission both authenticate it. Old runtime sources remain
unchanged; a versioned v5 successor adds this route. A real prior-commit route
remains supported, but no fake commit identifier is accepted as local review.

Qualification tests successful native admission and complete fresh replay on a
constructed scratch plan, mutation and missing-evidence refusals, changed scope,
failed fixtures, immutable reentry and unchanged resource/budget gates. It reads
no participant data and charges its measured CPU use to CH14 within the unused
part of the already approved 600-second qualification allowance.

CH14 keeps its original source, model, controls, two thousand draws and full
independent replay. The completed 128-case scientific qualification remains
unchanged. Its final inclusive 80,000 CPU-second cap includes all preparation,
failed and successful attempts and coordination. Stop and file the projection
if unfinished at that cap; no third allowance or idle-family continuation.

Research records are written through locally without waiting for publication.
Publication remains an independent authorized action, never an automatic timer.
