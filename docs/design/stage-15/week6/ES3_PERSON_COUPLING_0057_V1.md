## Uncertainty and limits

The point is the ordinary Pearson correlation of the identified paired person
slopes. It is descriptive and uncorrected for measurement error; a relation here
does not by itself distinguish biological mechanisms, establish causation, or
turn the short-minus-long proxy into a directly measured clearing rate.

Use the canonically sorted union of both eligible parent rosters as the shared
whole-person sampling frame. Each draw samples that frame with replacement and
applies the same person's frequency to both parents. Recompute each parent's
pooled sample scale from its own original qualifying prompts and frequencies,
then evaluate the correlation among identified overlapping people with those
frequencies. This preserves the uncertainty contributed by parent-only people
whose prompts affect calibration, rather than recalibrating the parents on the
intersection or treating those calibrations as known. Source eligibility and
exact identities do not change across draws.

At least two independent identified people are required for the point; a draw
with too few weighted identified people, a constant relation, or undefined parent
calibration is retained as invalid. There are exactly 2000 deterministic draws,
with no redraw and no valid-draw-only interval. The inherited percentile interval
is available only if every draw is valid. The original ES2 0.15 split-half gate
and latent correction apply to its dial study, not this uncorrected secondary.
