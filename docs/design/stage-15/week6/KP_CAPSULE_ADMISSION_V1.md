## Constructed known answer and the native sequence

The invented capsule has 20 writers, two sessions each and 16 rows per session.
For row index i, Walsh column j is +1 when popcount(i & j) is even and -1 otherwise.
The nine frozen covariates receive columns 1–9, with the two binary boundary columns
mapped to 0/1. Log pause is 0.5 times column 1 plus 0.25 times column 10 plus the
writer index divided by eight. Each session has the same balanced columns.

Training-only writer centering removes only the writer offset. Every nonconstant
Walsh column has mean zero and distinct columns are orthogonal. The base and donor
arms therefore have mean squared error 5/16; the own arm has 1/16. The analytic KP2
contrast is exactly 80 percent. The declared numerical comparison permits absolute
error 1e-8; it does not change a scientific tolerance or real-data threshold.
