## Fixed measure and fitted contrast

Families are anger(angry,resentful), fear(anxious,insecure), ashamed, and
sad(sad,lonely,excluded). Each is a separate descriptive panel. Member0056
lacks lonely and excluded: its complete sad-family panel is explicitly
unsupported before source rows, with no substitute item or shortened family.
The four negative items are criticised, ignored, upstaged and sidelined.
Ratings retain the original1-6 scale; empty strings and NA are missing.

Week1 reactivity is the person's within-person slope of the mean scaled family
items on the mean scaled negative items. Pooled within-person sample deviations
use the eligible week1 complete event/feeling rows and are recomputed per draw.
Endorsements count each negative item whose raw rating exceeds1, among rows
with all four negative items present. They are not counts of distinct events.
Week2 feelings are not required for a week2 endorsement total. Answered-prompt
counts are taken before rating exclusions, using the original source reader.
Regress week2 endorsement totals on an intercept, week1 reactivity, week1
endorsements and answered-prompt totals in each week. Each person has unit
weight in the point fit; resampling frequencies supply bootstrap weights.

Retain the qualified eligibility rule: at least ten required complete rows
across the two-week panel, at least two week1 rows to form its slope, and at
least one week2 negative-item-complete row. A constant week1 predictor is
unidentified. The five-parameter joint fit needs at least five identified
people and full rank; otherwise retain descriptive support counts and file
unsupported identification rather than inventing a coefficient. Missing
categories, outside1-14 rows and every exclusion remain separately counted.
No new weekly support floor or selectively complete week2 feeling requirement.

Use2000 whole-person draws at the pre-outcome config seed. Keep every invalid
draw, without replacement; any invalid draw prevents a valid-draw-only interval.
No causal interpretation, reverse-time adjustment, held-out prediction claim,
practical decision threshold or automated descendant is added.
