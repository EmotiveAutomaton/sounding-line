## First admission review, member 0010

**PST9 ruling for 0073, 21 November.** Each form contributes the valence axis once per family. Orient it toward worse mood for the negative event and better mood for the positive event. Reversing the mood direction reverses the mean slope and the interval endpoints. The adjusted variance and its ratio to the squared mean are invariant. Arousal appears only in the descriptive tables. Both families use the same respondents, so agreement across families does not supply independent replication.

Geschwind and colleagues randomized 130 adults with residual depressive symptoms to mindfulness-based cognitive therapy or a waitlist. They tested changes in positive affect, activity appraisal and reward associated with activity pleasantness. Their trial did not specify the within-person fear-minus-sadness contrast for cheerful mood. This secondary analysis uses two distinct observation periods. Baseline is primary and the second period is a companion. Neither comparison identifies a treatment effect. [Source paper](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0024595)
