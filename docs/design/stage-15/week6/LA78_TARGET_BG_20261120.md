# Which speaker a laugh targets in the meeting studies

Amendment BG, approved by the curator on November 20, applies to LA7 and LA8 before the first outcome read. The November 19 packet supplies the original 2.0-second window, role-pair rate denominator, social controls, next-minute contrast and whole-meeting resampling.

At each annotated laugh onset, collect distinct other speakers with an utterance in progress or an utterance that ended in the preceding window, including the two-second boundary. Count each speaker once even when several of their utterances qualify. With no other candidate, record the event separately as self-laughter. This label describes the timing classification. The recording cannot establish whom the laugher intended to address.

Use exactly one candidate for the primary analyses. Before reading outcomes, save the total laughs, self-laughter, single-target and multiple-target counts, the fraction with multiple candidates overall and among laughs with candidates, and the counts and shares by candidate role pair. Keep meetings as the resampling unit. A laugh with three or more candidates can enter several candidate role-pair cells. Publish each denominator. For the limit sentence on each card, compare the single-candidate count with all laughs having at least one candidate. If fewer than half qualify for the primary, say so.

For LA7 report the ordered roles of the laugher and target and the laughs per minute the target held the floor. Beside the primary, assign an ambiguous laugh to the candidate whose qualifying utterance ended last, then tabulate a separate rule giving each candidate equal credit. Record exact end-time ties and share the tied event equally between those tied for last. Each descriptive rule allocates one laugh in total across its candidates. Keep self-laughter separate under all rules. Keep the role, turn position and ongoing-laughter adjustment. When the primary direction for a role pair reverses under either descriptive rule, state the reversal and give that pair no verdict. LA7 has no registered directional prediction.

For LA8 use single-target events only. Compare the target's next 60 seconds of speaking share after joining versus remaining silent in the two-second laughter window, adjusting for meeting phase and the target's preceding 60-second share. Measure voiced time in seconds. Collapse overlapping speech intervals from the same speaker once. Retain both complete minute windows and account explicitly for truncated windows. The registered direction is lower share after an unjoined laugh, with a two-percentage-point bar and an interval clear of zero. The same meeting contributes all its events to each draw.

Both cards retain the assigned-role design-exercise population and the limits of annotated laughter. File the support census with its source and timing pins before fitting an outcome.
