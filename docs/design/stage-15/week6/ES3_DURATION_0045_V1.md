# ES3: reported duration in openESM 0045

Owner build of the full-week extension section 3C, ES3's descriptive duration
secondary. This is an additional measurement of reported duration, not the
carry-over slope, an elapsed-time observation or an individual clearing rate.

## DESIGN CHECK

LESSONS 3-5: declared measurement units, fixed source and column scope,
constructed arithmetic, independent full replay and complete cost accounting.
NULL and ALTERNATIVE have the same descriptive disposition; no effect test,
scientific bar, group comparison or confidence interval is introduced.
Wrong units, nonfinite or negative values, changed sources and incomplete or
changed replay refuse. Zero and large finite values remain; no trimming.
No eligible observation is explicitly unsupported, rather than zero duration.
The case is checked by the published duration field and twenty constructed
adapter cases. The smaller route is a fresh counted full-source retry after a
cooperative pause, within the original ES family ceiling; no selected sample.

## Fixed descriptor and support

The field is a person's estimate of how long the current emotional state has
lasted, in literal minutes. Empty strings and NA are missing. First exclude
missing identifiers, then missing durations, counting each disposition.
Reject unrecognized, nonfinite or negative durations. Among retained rows,
report total rows, distinct people, minimum, the 25th/50th/75th percentiles,
maximum and mean. Percentiles use the existing linear empirical quantile.
Do not trim, winsorize, log-transform or substitute units. Pool reports; do
not present the mean as a person-weighted or representative population effect.
Missingness and repeated participation limit the description. No inferential
support floor is needed because no population interval or comparison is made.
With no eligible row, report unsupported and preserve all disposition counts.
