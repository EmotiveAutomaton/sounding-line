support and product routes, and LD3 in members 0057 and 0056. It does not admit
a research run or revise a prediction. The newly filed theory errata removes
presence from SHAME's working trigger; LD1 still tests its original frozen

DESIGN CHECK: LESSONS sections 2-5 reread. Under both null and alternative,
source-byte and item-meaning invariance are required. Changed source bytes,
unknown rating/context codes, missing required columns and row misalignment
refuse. Allowed missing tokens stay missing, never floor or zero. These checks

The adapter authenticates full source bytes before and after streaming an exact
column whitelist. It materializes no text, demographics or static traits. IDs
group prompts only in private memory and are omitted from returned person
records. Source order and original day values are retained without claiming
temporal order. Both members retain their separate populations. Member 0056's
absent lonely and excluded items are never fabricated.

appropriate interaction or solitary feeling fields, and derives failure as
seven minus success. Each primary or diagnostic requires its explicitly named
common-row mask; a missing secondary must not silently remove primary rows.
LD2's support projection cannot materialize feeling outcomes. It preserves the
partner-count and role codes plus criticism and leadership for the existing
count and coding checks. Its product projection keeps all six events, all four
specified feelings, leadership, self-assurance and superiority. LD3 keeps all
eight activity ratings and every available declared feeling on solitary prompts.
Ratings retain the existing admitted one-to-six scale and reviewed missing-token
vocabulary. Relationship integers outside scored roles remain unscored codes;
the full source review must validate their codebook meanings before admission.

missing day and panel-specific missingness. Missing day does not silently remove
an otherwise complete coefficient row. The panel builder imposes no new person
or variance floor; the full card must apply its declared support checks.
