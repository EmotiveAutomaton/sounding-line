# Retained reporting terms for sealed sources

The archive checked these editions for amendment DN. Every released cell in this project must represent at least eleven distinct people. The original gate checks the selected aggregate table. The source-specific rules below also apply.

| Source | Retained reporting condition | Credit and edition |
|---|---|---|
| MIDUS | The MIDUS Conditions of Use say “Ensure that no cells/strata with n < 10 are produced.” ICPSR requires confidentiality and limits redistribution of the original materials. | Cite MIDUS and the edition-specific ICPSR deposit, use the current funding acknowledgment, and send the citation of a published work to ICPSR. [MIDUS acknowledgment](https://midus.wisc.edu/citation-acknowledgement/) |
| CANDOR | BetterUp license v2.1 §3(b)(iii) permits “aggregate statistics and research findings that do not reproduce the CANDOR Corpus or identify any Participant.” | Reece et al. [Tabular release](https://huggingface.co/datasets/betterup/candor-tabular) |
