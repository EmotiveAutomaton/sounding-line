# AUTH AUC uncertainty: constructed ruler check

This component validates a candidate numerical implementation for the already
commissioned FIC1 primary, stories-minus-arguments AUC. It cannot run a human
contrast or admit an identification claim. The unchanged primary, inherited
permutation requirements and three-role independent-context controls still need
an explicit executable mapping and admission before scientific dispatch.

DESIGN CHECK: LESSONS 3-5 and CONTROLS 4-7 reread November 5. Constant scores
must yield AUC one half and exactly zero genre difference. Perfect scores in one
genre against ties in the other must yield the signed one-half difference.
Collapsed empirical intervals are marked degenerate, never population certainty.
Finite random null worlds must control false alarms; signed plants must recover.

Compute pooled AUC as the probability that a known-same score exceeds a
known-different score, counting a tie as one half. Use exact integer score
histograms. The primary remains the difference of the two separately normalized
genre AUCs; the larger creative allocation does not weight the genres unequally.

The uncertainty candidate resamples the original writer roster 2,000 times,
sharing each writer multiplicity across both genres and both pair roles. A
same-writer observation receives that writer's multiplicity once. A
different-writer observation receives the product of its two multiplicities.
All sessions and matched observations attached to those writers remain together.
This is a candidate vertex resampling rule, not an assertion that its finite
sample coverage is already valid. Calibration may refuse it. Do not use an
anchor-only bootstrap or independently resample the genres. Missing truth classes
in any resample invalidate the interval; no discarded or replacement draw.

**The case and its check.** First validate exact arithmetic, ties, symmetry,
both-role weights, reordered input, empty-class refusal and immutable replay on
constructed data under the actual native runtime. Then use the authenticated
main collection's entire structural graph, with every human reader score removed,
to construct 100 null, 100 positive and 100 negative worlds. No parameter is
estimated from human labels. Freeze seed 20261102 and all parameters in code.
Worlds have stable writer ability and response bias, independent genre-specific
writer variation, shared session and prompt effects, and per-pair noise. Negative
comparisons depend on both writers. The two non-null regimes add a fixed 2.5-score
known-same increment in the designated genre. They are numerical sensitivity
worlds, not a model of human play, genre, or the paid reader's semantic competence.

The numerical ruler gate requires all resamples supported and nondegenerate,
at most 10 of 100 null intervals excluding zero in either direction, and at
least 80 of 100 intervals recovering each planted direction. A two-world-per-
regime timing prefix supplies cost evidence only and cannot pass the scientific
or full ruler gate. All worlds and their outcomes remain; no calibration tuning
or automatic repair is licensed by a failed gate. Fresh-process verification
reconstructs the full calculation with reversed row order.
