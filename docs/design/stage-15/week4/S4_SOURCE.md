# S4 cursor census and landing geometry

The packet's distinction is geometric, not a claim that cursor events reveal a mouse. This census precedes the primary and the separately descriptive menu-choice comparison. Its three raw menu browsing categories and mixed/neither records are retained; canonical menu/choice joins remain a later prerequisite.

**Case and check.** Logged steps and jumps must be separately classifiable, with both observed and jumps at least five percent of valid cursor events. Counts and the full displacement distribution remain visible, including stationary and intermediate moves; this is operational separability, not a claim of statistical bimodality. At least twenty writers need ten complete jump and ten complete step landings, only in distance bins that contain both kinds. Known Unicode positions, step runs, pass-through jumps, API moves, near/far edits and right censoring must reproduce.

**Smaller route.** Charge source construction, native replay, timing and full verification to S4's one-hour original allowance. If the complete forecast exceeds twice that ceiling, freeze whole writers under amendment E with at least the twenty-writer case floor; preserve the full-population gap.

Positions are UTF-16 editor offsets. A step is one character or the adjacent word boundary; a jump exceeds forty characters or crosses a paragraph boundary. A paragraph jump takes precedence over a one-character step at its newline. Nonempty cursor selections, API moves, cursor events with simultaneous text edits and missing positions are counted separately. Sentence geometry uses the existing frozen punctuation-boundary policy and the document as it exists at the move. Distance is from its final nonempty sentence, binned as one, two, three-to-five and more than five sentences back.

Consecutive steps within two seconds and with no intervening text change form a run; only its last step is a landing. A jump followed by another jump within two seconds is a pass-through. Every included landing must have the whole thirty-second window observed, regardless of whether an edit happens. The nearby region is the landing sentence and its immediately adjacent sentences. Its saved UTF-16 boundaries move through later deltas, so preceding remote insertions cannot turn a nearby edit into a far one. Only user edits count as outcomes; all deltas update geometry. Source replay must exactly match the authenticated final document.

No effect or probability is scored in this census. A full source pass and outcome-blind support decision are prerequisites to the fixed writer-cluster comparison and its within-session permutation reference. The initial Unicode test fixture placed the paragraph boundary two UTF-16 units too early; the corrected fixture uses the actual independently counted boundary, with the failed test retained. No empirical source outcome motivated that correction.

A second constructed-fixture assertion expected two landings but had one jump and two separate terminal step runs. The corrected assertion names all three known ordinals, rather than asserting a count. Both development failures are retained; neither involved an empirical comparison or a native scientific attempt.
