# S1.ic: planted-template sensitivity

**Rationale.** The complete S1 primary did not detect the proposed advantage. Amendment C and the packet's S1 route require a planted-template check before that null is treated as evidence against the mechanism. This is the sole registered S1 follow-up; no descendants are authorized.

**Case and check.** Construct earlier own and donor sessions and later eight-word target windows, then run the actual sentence-local skeleton membership, repeated-gram readout and unchanged adjusted statistic. Recover exact positive, negative, identical-history and no-overlap answers before drawing any stochastic worlds. The stochastic curve holds twenty-five writers and eighty windows per writer, balanced within two sessions, with the original twenty-per-arm support floor. The construction isolates template recurrence; it does not validate psychological flow labels or reproduce the entire human logging pipeline.

Draw target sentences from a frozen 150-token alphabet; verify all their skeleton four-grams are unique across targets before populating histories. Include each complete target sentence in the own history with probability 0.4 plus the planted flow advantage, and in the donor history with probability 0.4. Coupled uniform draws across doses preserve the same targets and nuisance values. Non-template filler sentences match total own and donor history lengths. Histories precede target sessions by construction; no target outcome is used to choose a ruler or tune the analysis. Random nuisance values are independent of template assignment. The expected own-minus-donor flow contrast is the planted advantage. These are constructed token sequences, not synthetic participant records.

Freeze advantages 0, 0.01, 0.02, 0.05, 0.10 and 0.20; twenty seeds starting at 20261102, identical across doses. Each complete world runs the parent's 200 within-session label permutations and 2,000 whole-writer resamples. Report the fraction passing the complete original positive gate, mean error, interval coverage and the fraction with a biased reference at every dose, including zero. Count uncertainty is binomial Wilson 95%; no collapsed normal fallback or new significance test. No post-run gate removal, selected seeds or added doses. This small conditional calibration cannot by itself establish human sensitivity or refute the theory.
