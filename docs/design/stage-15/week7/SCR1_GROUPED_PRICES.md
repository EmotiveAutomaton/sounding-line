# SCR1 on the Portuguese scale

The curator approved this binding at 15:45 Pacific on November 22 before the fit. The twenty MFSS items have seven price categories and a refusal. Positive labels run from €10 through €1 million in tenfold steps. On a base-ten log scale the cuts are 0.5, 1.5, 2.5, 3.5, 4.5, 5.5 and 6.5. The €0 category is open below 0.5. Refusals sit above 6.5. A missing response supplies no category.

Fit a separate grouped normal to each item's priced answers conditional on a price at or below the top cut. The second fit also conditions on being above the bottom cut and excludes the €0 answers. Both predict refusals. Put both excesses on the original answered-item denominator. Multiply the observed share in the fitted price bins by the fitted upper-tail probability divided by the fitted probability of those bins. Subtract that prediction from the observed refusal share. The two readings share the same directly observed refusal and top-price dip. Retain €0 shares by item. Use equal item weights and 2,000 resamples of whole respondents, shared between readings. File unavailable fits and intervals explicitly. Excess refusal requires the two intervals to be clear of zero on the same side. The registered direction is positive. The registered dip is negative.

The descriptive count variance uses respondents with all twenty items observed. For the odd/even description, correlate the observed fraction of odd items refused with the mean ordered price step on priced even items. File the denominator and the exclusion for missing support. These are hypothetical compensation responses. Keep numerical results in the internal test record.
