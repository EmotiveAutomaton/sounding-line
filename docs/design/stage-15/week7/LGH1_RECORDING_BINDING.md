# LGH1 recording and frame definitions

The curator fixed the ICSI sampling rule on November 22 and corrected the voicing denominator before waveform measurement. All LGH1 corpora use 40 ms frames at 10 ms steps. For signals expressed relative to full scale, an active frame has RMS amplitude at least the larger of 0.000000000001 and 1% of the largest frame RMS in that annotated interval. The relative threshold is −40 dB in amplitude. An interval with no active frames gives an undefined voiced fraction. An interval with active sound and zero qualifying pitch frames gives a voiced fraction of zero. Divide the number of qualifying voiced frames by the number of active frames. Use voiced frames alone for median pitch and pitch spread. The annotation boundaries supply duration.

For ICSI, select one official transcript for each meeting before the census. Use the original participant-to-channel assignment. Include a timed utterance when its only content is one laughter annotation. Count mixed laughter marks and report each speaker’s fraction of laughter marks that were mixed in a table sorted by fraction, with no names or keys. The denominator includes all that speaker’s laughter marks. Report missing times or channel bindings separately. The duration label is **annotated utterance length**. For AMI, use explicitly timed vocal laughter on the assigned headset and count untimed marks separately.

The reference for relative pitch is the median of the speaker’s voiced speech frames in that recording. Relative level uses mean square over the speaker’s reference speech samples. Reference pitch, reference level, per-laugh features, and voice samples stay inside the job. Every ICSI-derived LGH1 or LGH2 figure carries this limit. **Laughter that overlaps speech is not in the sample, and that may be a kind of its own.**
