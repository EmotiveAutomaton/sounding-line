# Constructed qualifications for the Week 7 operators

The supplied archive is pinned in the intake manifest. The local qualifications use small constructed inputs and compare the study implementations directly with the supplied scripts. TRX1 compares the adjusted trait effect in all six supplied worlds. PSD2 checks co-occurrence and between-prompt falls. The timing correction adds its own clock and sensitivity fixtures.

For rebound compare the person-centred current-stress, fall and rise fit against pf_rebound. For excess refusal compare both the estimated unobserved price tail and the dip with pf_price. The real price bin cutoffs must come from the source dictionary. For the product with squares held, compare the logistic rating interaction with pf_gate, including its smoothed leave-one-out rater response rate and the two squared terms. For the test of kinds compare connected sets at the 0.8 midpoint density bar with pf_cluster on overlapping and separated mixtures. Compare component membership independent of the numeric labels assigned to components.

The sound qualification uses only generated waveforms. See [measurement specification](SOUND_FIXTURE.md). Readiness of a constructed operator does not establish the source rights, roster, grouping or output support for any real run. Each source-specific admission retains those checks.
