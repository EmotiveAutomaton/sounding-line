# Games and scales admission under CN

Use the curator’s storage handback and the filed CF reconciliation before opening these sources. Each binding record must identify the precise release and hashes, dictionary, permissible use, observed units and support, and any coding that leaves a comparison unidentified. Keep country and survey round as the only demographic fields for VAL1. Other projections omit demographic columns.

| Row | Catalogue evidence | Binding required before the first fit |
|---|---|---|
| UGM1 | The ultimatum-game deposit includes round-by-round expected and experienced feelings and a dictionary. CC BY 4.0. | Bind player, trial order, offer, and felt valence and arousal. Bind a documented expected **offer** if present. Otherwise take only that player’s earlier offers and count first trials without a history. Check identifiability of gap and change in the joint fit. Resample whole players. |
| SCR1 | The Portuguese Sacredness Scale records twenty hypothetical compensation responses. The catalogue restricts this release to tests only. | Bind each price category to its euro value or bounded interval, distinguish refusal from missing, and verify item order before splitting items. Restrict figures to the repository’s internal record and theory thread. |
| GAT1, GAT2 | Curator disposition, November 23: tests only. The authors made the distribution public without a stated licence. Figures stay within the repository record and theory thread. | A provider-documented rater key is missing from this edition. Once supplied, bind rater, occasion, choice, reason and gate ratings against that version’s codebook. Preserve the registered controls, scale versions and return restrictions. |
| VAL1 | The European Social Survey export includes the 21 Human Values items, country, round, edition and weights. The recorded terms are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. | Verify edition and coverage, item polarity, documented missing codes, scale points and applicable weights. Compute each variance component on raw values and on within-person-centred values, plus scale points per decade. The one-tenth marker is descriptive. |
