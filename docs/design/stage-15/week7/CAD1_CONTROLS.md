# CAD1 within-speaker comparison

Frozen from the curator’s November 22 ruling at 11:40 Pacific, before any CANDOR outcome was opened for this study. Compare a speaker’s words per second across her conversations against her own directly answered post-conversation enjoyment item, `how_enjoyable`.

| Reading | Terms held in the same within-speaker fit |
|---|---|
| Main | The speaker’s immediate arousal (`arousal`), her mood before the conversation (`pre_affect`), and her partner’s words per second in that conversation. |
| Expanded | All three main terms, plus her overall affect (`overall_affect`), her liking for the partner (`i_like_you`), and her perception of their attention (`my_partner_paid_attention_to_me`). |

The outcome is the speaker's total count of spoken words divided by her speaking time over her turns. Use one transcript edition, Audiophile, with the existing recording clock and boundary-turn exclusions. Count spoken words in the retained utterances. Exclude bracketed annotation tokens. Merge overlap in a speaker’s own intervals for the denominator. Reject invalid timing and ambiguous reciprocal matches. An identifiable partner with usable speech is necessary even when that partner has a single conversation. They can supply the pace control. They contribute to the estimated within-speaker slope only if their own roster includes at least two distinct complete conversations.

Use each reading’s complete cases and state both contributing-person counts and the exclusion for single-conversation speakers, subject to the sealed disclosure rules. Subtract weighted speaker means from outcome and predictors, then fit a common within-speaker enjoyment coefficient. Draw 2,000 whole conversations; both directions receive the same draw weight. Recompute the within-speaker means in each draw. An unidentified fit supplies no signed verdict. The only survey projections are the dyad identifiers, enjoyment and named controls. The second reading has no authority to select a different enjoyment item.
