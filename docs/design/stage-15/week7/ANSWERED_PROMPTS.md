# Answered prompts in the two status diaries

The curator approved timestamp pairing at 10:50 Pacific on November 22 and corrected the card label at 10:52. This addendum covers PSD2’s between-prompt falls and ISO1. PSD3 uses the original chronological halves. PSD2’s co-occurrence and own-event rises use the original same-prompt calculations.

Before fitting a paired outcome, file each diary’s timing distribution from a projection of person, study day, creation timestamp and answer timestamp. Use the existing NT1 chronology checks. Exclude a person from the paired analysis if any clock is missing or malformed, an answer precedes its creation, two answers share a timestamp, or the supplied study day differs from elapsed UTC creation dates plus one. Order all answers by recorded answer timestamp within the person. A consecutive pair must share its study day. A missing context or feeling can exclude that pair at fitting and cannot create a bridge. Time of day uses consecutive four-hour UTC blocks containing the current answer in ISO1. Describe them as recorded-clock blocks, without inferring local time.

File the number of people, the number of pairs, the range, quartiles, median and 90th and 95th percentiles of elapsed minutes. Include counts by fixed gap bands and the number at or below twice the diary median. Freeze the median on all eligible time pairs before reading a paired outcome. The main analysis uses all same-day pairs. The second retains gaps at or below twice this median, including ties. Use the same roster, indexed whole-person draws, and prompt-level coding for both. Keep the same-prompt rises fixed across the two versions of PSD2’s ratios.

For PSD2, the fall is the between-prompt change in pride after criticism or shame after admiration, compared with the change at interactions with neither event, under the registered previous-feeling condition. ISO1 relates current feelings to the next report of no interaction. Report each estimate and interval side by side. For each paired quantity, a change of sign or a change in whether its interval excludes zero makes its diary reading undecided. A missing estimate or interval also leaves it undecided. For ISO1 the registered quantity is shame minus the average of anger and sadness. A discrepancy on either fall leaves the corresponding diary’s fall interpretation undecided. Apply the same check to any paired fall expressed as a fraction of the rise. The co-occurrence bar retains its own reading.

Each paired result carries the limit that a missed prompt can widen a gap, and a missed prompt may not be missed at random.
