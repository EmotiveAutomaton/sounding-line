## E1: does the history pattern survive holding writers fixed?

Question: is the changing performance curve partly a change in which writers
contribute? Use all retained T0c predictions, without fitting or model calls.
Reuse the original prior-decision bins: 0, 1-9, 10-29, 30-99, 100-299, 300+.
Separately report all decisions, prompts novel to that writer, and globally novel
prompts. Score population and personal-frequency comparisons separately, as
comparator half-Brier loss minus backbone loss (positive favors the backbone).

For each bin report equal-writer mean gain, writer/decision counts. For every
adjacent pair report only writers observed in both bins, their equal-writer early
and late gains, late-minus-early difference, positive/zero/negative writer counts,
and the full range of leave-one-writer-out differences. Retain zero-support pairs
as unestimable. Also report a balanced curve on writers present in every bin;
an empty intersection remains empty. Never merge bins after seeing outcomes.

Null control: constant within-writer gains must give zero matched difference even
when writer composition changes. Alternative: planted within-writer increases
must be recovered. Duplicate keys, invalid probabilities or alignment failures
refuse. Positive, zero and negative contrasts are all reported without a winner.
This controls writer composition, not changing tasks, calendar time, global-model
training or selective continuation. It cannot establish a causal benefit of
collecting more history. Historical exposure remains; no fresh holdout is claimed.

## E2: target-field scope diagnostic

Question: do failed person/theme readers classify surrounding context instead
of the target sentence, and does an explicit field boundary help?
Construct 16 target sentences per question: Q6 first/second/third/mixed person,
four lexical templates each; Q10 empty/food-or-body/danger/care, four templates
each. These selected classes diagnose failures; they do not cover every theme.
Keep each target unchanged across three contexts: absent, matching, conflicting.
Cross with the original canonical prompt and that exact prompt preceded by a
target-field-only instruction. Preserve canonical full JSON parsing and schemas.
No extra definition, example answer or ground-truth label enters the prompt.

Run each of the 192 conditions twice with byte-identical request bodies, in two
separately shuffled complete passes (fixed seed 151015). Total ceiling 384 calls,
three hours, one CPU inference thread, zero GPU residency and no paid provider.
Use the same pinned local model, runtime and isolated engine as the finished
literal queue. Exact repeats receive separate dispatch directories, not cache hits.
Retain full request/response/residency records, prompt token counts and timing.
Maximum serialized prompt 3,000 characters; actual prompt count must stay below
1,500 tokens. Service lease, native identity and terminal/disappearance watches
are required; RAM/commit floors 8 GiB and disk floor 20 GiB. No automatic retry.

Report only after the whole diagnostic: correctness/format by question, class,
context and prompt; matched context-change answer flips; exact-repeat disagreement;
and the prompt-by-context difference in correctness. Positive/zero/negative
differences all remain descriptive. Null: a target-only oracle is invariant to
context; a context-copying fixture fails conflicting cases. Alternative: explicit
scope improves conflicting-context correctness more than matching-context
correctness. Repeats distinguish changed-input sensitivity from runtime variation.
Malformed responses count wrong; transport/unfinished outputs stop the attempt.

Even a large gain is diagnosis, not repaired admission: original Q6/Q10 remain
disabled. A successor would need a new full, separately frozen battery and all
natural-data/scientific gates. Failure is also informative: field clarification
would not be enough, and the next issue would be label semantics or model ability.

Literature check: [Ribeiro et al. (2020), CheckList](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.442.pdf),
READ sections 1-2 and section 5's limitations. Behavioral capability tests and
label-preserving perturbations motivate this paired design. They do not validate
labels, data provenance or mechanisms. This is a project-specific diagnostic,
not a new general evaluation method or an adoption of a psychological ontology.

## Interpretation and continuation

The interesting existing evidence is a useful saved-data backbone alongside a
failed incremental cloud-reader screen, and literal reliability that varies by
question. Format-correct profiles have not yet proved predictive usefulness.
E1 probes the first finding's composition sensitivity; E2 probes a repairable
interface hypothesis. Neither substitutes for the commissioned own/donor-profile
comparison, native adapters or a relevant Ghost twin. No additional paid batch.
Full internal write-through and terminal ACK policy remain in force; the existing
six-hour operational clock remains unchanged.
