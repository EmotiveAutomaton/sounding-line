# LKG1 · How accurately did conversation partners judge liking?

For each reciprocal dyad, join the respondent's estimate (`you_like_me`) to the other person's rating of that respondent (`i_like_you`) in the same conversation. A positive gap means the respondent overestimated how much the partner liked them. The respondent's own rating of the other person is a separate predictor. The authenticated v1.1 dictionary and the retained response-key workbook assign both items the same seven increasing values. Accept codes 1–7 and native nulls. Other non-null values void the binding. Validate both directions of the person and partner keys and exclude incomplete or duplicated dyads.

Use the mean over directed reports with both the estimate and the partner's actual rating. Draw whole respondents 2,000 times for uncertainty. Repeated conversations with partners who also recur can make these intervals too narrow. For the three repeat measures use respondents with all three quantities in at least two conversations. Follow the supplied unbalanced one-way ANOVA intraclass correlation, allowing negative estimates. Refit the pooled regression on the respondent's own liking inside each draw before calculating the residual intraclass correlation. The third intraclass correlation uses estimated partner liking minus the respondent's own liking.

The dictionary defines `date` as the survey date. For the first-versus-second correlation and all three moment estimates, use the two earliest eligible, distinctly ordered observations for a respondent. Exclude that respondent from these measures if a date is absent or a tie makes either of the first two ambiguous. Do not sort tied observations by a conversation identifier. Use the three fixed own-liking weights and the cross-conversation covariances in `pf_gap2.py`. File the moment estimates only if the interval for cross-conversation covariance of partners' actual ratings lies above zero. All repeat estimates require at least 20 contributing respondents. A single observation can still contribute to the mean.

For prior fits that included additional filters, the survey-only eligible set is a proven upper bound. Under the inherited joint-disclosure rule, an estimate on an unverified new membership cannot leave. Keep each admissible measure and its real number of people. A withheld component receives its disposition without another invocation.
