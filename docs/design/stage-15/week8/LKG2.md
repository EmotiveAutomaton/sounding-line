# LKG2 · Do a speaker’s liking errors vary with personality or standing?

Use the original CANDOR v1.1 survey, its released trait composites and its two named standing judgments. The two provider dictionaries checked for LKG1 bind the reciprocal liking items to 1–7, the two ladder placements to rungs 1–10, and the five trait ratings to the five-point scale. The trait composites may take fractional values. Treat a native null as missing and void a component whose observed codes fall outside its bound domain.

| Quantity | Survey fields and direction |
|---|---|
| Liking gap | A speaker’s `you_like_me` less the other speaker’s `i_like_you` in the same reciprocal conversation. |
| Standing gap | A speaker’s `you_think_my_status` less the other speaker’s `i_think_your_status`. Higher rung numbers mean higher standing. |
| Own and received traits | Use the released `my_` and `your_` composites for extraversion, agreeableness, conscientiousness, neuroticism and openness. The received rating comes from the partner’s record. Read emotional stability as six minus Neuroticism. |
