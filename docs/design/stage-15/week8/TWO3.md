# TWO3 · Feelings between partners on successive diary occasions

The original couples diary identifies both the couple and each participant. Its SPSS dictionary defines the measurement occasion and four momentary ratings on the same 0–6 scale. Each target report is linked to both partners' reports from exactly one occasion earlier. A missing occasion creates a gap. Duplicate person–occasion rows are excluded together, and couples with ambiguous membership cannot contribute.

Each of the four feelings has its own within-person fit. The predictors are the person's own prior rating on that feeling and their partner's four prior ratings. The comparison is the coefficient for the same feeling minus the mean coefficient for the other three. The return also gives each coefficient, the matched counts and the exclusions. Both members of a couple stay together in each of 2,000 bootstrap draws. A rank-deficient contrast stays unresolved. Estimates below the 20-couple variation floor are descriptive.

**Design check.** The constructed diary recovers a planted same-feeling contribution and rejects nonconsecutive occasions, duplicate reports, nonunique partner assignments and unidentified contrasts. An independent read of the original file reproduces every aggregate. All comparisons are descriptive because a difficult stretch shared by the partners can affect both.
