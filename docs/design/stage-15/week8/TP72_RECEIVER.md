# TP72 receiver and pre-launch release forms

Week 8 registers the following forms before the writing and conversation runs. The receiver checks the real contributor set for every row against the producer’s in-memory observation and the authenticated source universe. All three forms use the original complement check and the installed output gate. A row includes its trained estimate, interval, distinct-person count and the two producer control bands. No identifiers, text, vectors or fitted weights leave either job.

| Study | Fine form | Coarse form | Pooled summary |
|---|---|---|---|
| TMP2 | All eligible rows among the nine registered history tables | Full-history traits, channels and contrast tables on their contributing cohorts | The original pooled diagonal-minus-other correlation contrast, with its interval, controls and writer count |
| TMP3 | Both registered conversation tables | The pooled affect table, including enjoyment | Worry against own recorded affect, with its interval, controls and speaker count |

On writing histories the original DJ projection determines excluded communities and dropped comments. The prepared adapter independently reproduces the residual exclusions within the seal, verifies the count and passes both measured sets directly to the producer. On conversations it supplies the existing reciprocal speaker projection. The observer joins each emitted row to its actual contributors by filename and the producer’s categorical labels. Missing observations, duplicate labels, count mismatches and people outside the authenticated universe fail closed.

The engine has documented this interface. The delivery still needs a verified manifest, independent receipt, established combined read-only source and an end-to-end in-image rehearsal on invented data, including cell counts and both control families. The TP58 fallback retains its separate entry and time boundary. The original PANDORA source check returned code 22.
