# RML1 · Room level before a laugh

Use the 75 ICSI and 171 AMI meetings and assigned speech channels admitted for LGH1. Fit and report each corpus separately. AMI results retain their internal handling. No voiceprint, video, speaker-keyed table or sequence of measured bins leaves the computation.

Divide each meeting into complete 30-second bins. Keep timed lexical speech. Mask every annotated laugh interval across the meeting for the sound and speech-rate measurements. For a mixed ICSI utterance bearing a laugh, mask the entire timed utterance. An untimed laugh cannot locate a clean interval, so omit that meeting from this measurement and count it. Count laugh starts from the admitted timestamped marks. On ICSI spread lexical words evenly over their utterance. AMI has word timings. Intersect both the observable word mass and the length of speaking time with the same clean intervals. Two speakers talking at once contribute on their own channels.

Use 20 ms non-overlapping speech frames wholly inside clean speech. Express each frame’s RMS in dB relative to full-scale input and standardize within channel and meeting. Average eligible frames within channel and bin, then average over speakers who supplied speech. Compute words per second of clean speech for each speaker in the bin. Standardize that channel’s speech rate within the meeting before combining its bin means with the sound measure. Give the two measures equal weight. File their component fits alongside the combined measure. A silent or unmeasurable bin stays missing. Use the common time covered by all assigned channels.

Use the conditional Poisson model and five predictors from the supplied room preflight. For target bin t, signed change is level at t−1 less level at t−3. The level is the mean over t−3 to t−1. Fit absolute change, signed change, level, level squared and a high followed by a fall (a peak above 0.8 in bins t−6 through t−4 and signed change below −0.5). Hold the number of laughs in the three preceding bins. Concentrate out each meeting intercept. Use 2,000 whole-meeting resamples and the preflight’s one-step Newton update. File a model only with an identifiable fit, finite draws and releasable membership. Report a narrowly clear result as unresolved.

**Design check.** On invented annotated sound, add a loud laugh and verify the masked speech measure is identical. Test word allocation across a bin boundary, a shared microphone interval, zero variation, the six-bin history and a planted effect recovered by the conditional count fit. The room’s sound and pace stand in for stress. Laughs are loud and come in runs.
