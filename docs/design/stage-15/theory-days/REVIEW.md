# Review before scientific outcomes

The packet is implementable in stages. Its named rivals, source checks and
three-way rules make it useful for testing the curator's channel account. Word
norms, annotation substitutions, gambling choices and writing actions address
different observable consequences; none alone establishes a neural identity.
The original packet remains unchanged. These are analyst implementation choices
under the curator's authorization to improve rigor, not new curator quotations.

DESIGN CHECK: LESSONS sections 1a-1c and 2-5; CONTROLS sections 6-7.
Under the null, calibrated statistics must retain zero and controls must not turn
selection, leakage or numerical failure into evidence. Under planted alternatives,
the full pipeline must recover both signs where the claim is two-sided. Missing
support, invalid controls, nonconvergence and cost holds are separate from nulls.

1. TD1 retains the packet's fitted-sign level plant and adds both-sign recovery.
   Training means/scales and forgetting factors cannot use held-out ratings.
   Report an interval for held-out error gain as well as the level coefficient.
   The packet's under-0.5% rule is retained but does not, by itself, prove exact
   equivalence; any support wording stays within the measured sensitivity.
2. TD2 keeps curvature and spread together. Standardization is fitted on training
   trials, and mixed-gamble support is counted before fitting. Report effective
   expected-value sign reversals instead of calling all negative interactions
   attenuation. The practical bar is 0.01 training-standardized happiness units
   per standard deviation of the residualized spread component.
3. TD3's null simulations regenerate all choice-dependent history, including
   standing and earlier gamble count. Keeping observed standing while replacing
   past choices would break the control. A missing counterfactual reward path is
   an identification limitation, not permission to manufacture outcomes. The
   practical bar is max(0.01 log-odds per training SD of standing,
   twice the null mean absolute slope plus three null SDs), on the same scale.
4. TD4 reports coverage before any ordering statistic. Zero variance or fewer
   than four covered words makes the relevant component untestable. Its residual
   ratio tests the specified linear shape; it cannot establish dimensionality
   independently of that shape. Published artifacts contain aggregate coverage
   and scores, never lexicon entries or individual word norms.
5. TD5 retains the packet's fixed strongest competitor and conditional interval.
   A companion reselects the maximum in every bootstrap draw. Scientific wording
   distinguishes conditional superiority from selection-aware superiority. A
   practical difference is 0.1 lift units, frozen before any corpus comparison.
   Marginal probabilities are recalculated in each comment-cluster bootstrap.
   Ordered cross-rater pairs exclude the same rater and preserve multilabel
   exclusions. Unsupported competitors cannot become a zero-valued rival.
6. TD6 uses the packet's exact matching and fallback. Bootstrap matched pairs
   within subreddit, retaining both members; matching uses whitespace length,
   while rates use the separately specified lexical tokens. A zero anger rate
   makes the relative contrast undefined. The construct is expressed mind-language,
   not a direct measurement of a reader's willingness to model another mind.
7. TD7 checks actual hovers, canonical future choices, writer support and ownership
   of overlapping windows before fitting. New arm interactions require zero-arm
   parity against the existing kernel and independent finite differences.
8. Backlog intakes remain ordered and source-gated. For chess, low clock time may
   affect move quality directly, so an instrumental-variable fit alone cannot
   license a causal effort interpretation. Retain the requested diagnostic with
   this exclusion assumption explicit; the interaction remains observational.

Known-answer tests, source verification and complete execution plus independent
replay must all fit the same cost accounting. A passing unit test is not complete
source or scientific admission. Existing failed controls and held Week 4 cards
are not restarted under this new namespace.
