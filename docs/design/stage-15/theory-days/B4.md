# B4: effort and skill, before outcomes

DESIGN CHECK: LESSONS 2-5 and CONTROLS 6-7. Correctly signed move quality and
clock arithmetic must recover constructed positive and negative interactions.
Null interaction is inconclusive or against under the frozen rule, never
support. Weak instruments, singular fits and missing annotations refuse.

Use the named April 2017 Lichess standard rated month, CC0. The complete file
is 3,481,200,992 compressed bytes. A source-only 64 MiB probe verified clocks
and evaluations and independently replayed the decompression schema; it took
2.125 total native CPU seconds. Sampling and parsing the complete month is
projected to fit the 7,200-second original ceiling. Preserve the exact full
download hash and a 1% Bernoulli game sample, seed 20261106, selected before
checking annotation availability. Never call an early file prefix a month sample.

Keep standard games with both clock and evaluation annotations. Sample outputs
remain private. Parse only ratings, time control, moves and annotations; public
artifacts contain no usernames, game identifiers or player profiles. For a move,
quality is minus nonnegative centipawn loss, obtained by comparing consecutive
White-perspective evaluations and reversing sign for Black. Exclude mate
evaluations and moves without both adjacent scores. Time spent is the same
player's previous remaining clock minus their new clock plus increment; the
first move uses the initial clock. Exclude nonpositive times at the source's
one-second precision, missing clocks, invalid controls and ratings.

The observational model uses rating, log seconds and their product, with game
phase (move-ply thirds: <=20, 21-60, >60) and absolute prior evaluation as a
declared difficulty proxy. These controls cannot measure all position difficulty.
Standardize rating/log seconds and their product on the admitted complete sample.
Instrument log seconds and its rating product by a prior clock of at most ten
seconds and its rating product; retain all exogenous controls in both stages.
Report OLS beside two-stage least squares. Require full column rank, at least
1,000 games/10,000 moves, and both excluded-instrument partial F statistics >10.
Otherwise design cannot test the IV contrast; retain OLS as descriptive only.

Primary: the two-stage product coefficient, in centipawns per standardized
interaction unit, with 2,000 game-cluster bootstrap draws recomputing both
stages from per-game sufficient statistics. Practical bar: one centipawn.
Supports if the interval lies entirely above +1; against if entirely at or
below zero; otherwise inconclusive. Any invalid draw makes uncertainty invalid,
not silently dropped. The analysis is conditional on the sampled games and
observed players; it does not estimate new-player population uncertainty.

Low clock time can directly impair decisions and correlate with hard positions.
The exclusion restriction is therefore unverified: even a passing IV contrast
is an observational diagnostic, not a causal estimate of effort. Preserve the
curator's effect/effort/power conjecture as the question; do not substitute the
analyst's proxy for its theoretical definition.

Ceiling 7,200 CPU seconds, at most 14,400 cumulatively under S4, subject to the
shared campaign cap. Freeze and validate complete extraction, both fit stages,
signed/null worlds and replay before human inference. If the required 1% month
sample cannot fit the remaining allowance, retain the cost hold rather than
silently reducing it or changing the month after seeing effects.
