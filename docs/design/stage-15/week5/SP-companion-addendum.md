# SP3 companion reporting completion

`week5_sp_analysis_h1.py` leaves all frozen SP primary fits, resampling, direction rules and grids unchanged. It adds the packet's descriptive neutral-location companion to SP3: for each same three-degree outdoor-distance bin, report building and observation counts, average sensitivity, mean neutral and observed indoor temperature, mean outdoor temperature, and median observed indoor range. The table uses exactly the same support, side filter and range matching as its corresponding primary. These are descriptives without new significance tests. Neutral-location movement stays visible beside sensitivity changes, and is not silently called sensory dulling. The actual execute/verify fixture recovers a planted sensitivity decline and reconciles every building into its bin.
