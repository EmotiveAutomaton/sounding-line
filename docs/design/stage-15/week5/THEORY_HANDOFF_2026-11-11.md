## 1. What changed this round

- **Words.** "Mine" is retired as a label. The CARE gate is **in-group** against **outgroup**: how much of my structure I think they share. The published version of that estimate is the kinship index (Lieberman, Tooby & Cosmides 2007), which is built from cues such as living together.
- **Set points.** Three for the body, five more: **Stress, Learning, External action, Belonging, Standing.**
  - Interest and appreciation combine into **Learning** (internal model improvement). Interest itself is the signal SEEKING keys on, not a set point.
  - Order (earlier Security) and the old Mine row combine into **External action** (external fitness: making the things and people that carry my models more predictably good). The name is open. Candidates: Tending, Stewardship, Meaning.
  - **Stress** is total uncertainty in my environment, kept at a level that is set against my own recent level.
  - **Belonging** is connection with minds like mine, through people or any evidence of them. Appreciation lives here and may also feed Learning.
  - **Standing** is under review at Abraham's request. Do not build on it yet.
- **Wiring.** Learning (change the model) and External action (change the world) are the two routes that lower Stress. Each is also a set point of its own, because each can be in deficit while Stress is in range.
- **Table columns** now use control terms: controlled variable, reference, output, disturbance. What moves a reference is kept separate from a disturbance.
- **LUST's After** now reads: stress rises, because there is now low-fitness kin that needs optimizing.
- **Positions to carry:** habit is a lossy, compressed record of previous attention; most jokes are repeats, so jokes are habit too; a rung treated as endlessly exchangeable becomes a target in itself; several higher loops usually share one lower loop, and full alignment lets one variable be optimized.

### Set points in general

1. **Never seen.** Only the pushes show.
2. **A band.** In control terms that's two references with a dead zone between them, the way sweating and shivering have separate thresholds. Many are defended harder on one side. In a phone game with 42,380 first plays, risk-taking changed below the starting score and not above it (*Week 5*).
3. **Most are settling points, some are true set points.**
4. **Nested.** A higher loop moves the target of the loop below. One lower loop usually serves several higher ones (yours: that's the human default). When they ask it for different things, that's conflict. When they line up, as in your runner, one variable can be optimized.
5. **Movable:** by forecast, by a schedule, by long deficit, by the ladder, and by my own recent level (yours: relative stress sets how much stress is okay).
6. **Each has a forecast.**
7. **Precision rises with time spent near one** and dulls under a long deficit.
8. **Failed effort changes which channel answers, not the target.**
9. **Surplus in one can be spent on another** (yours: trade-offs). Standing may be read from evidence of willingness to trade.
10. **A rung treated as endlessly exchangeable becomes a target in itself** (yours: money).

## 4. Tests, ranked

Each entry: the question, the data, the prediction, and what each outcome changes.

1. **Rebuild FEAR's trigger.**
   - Data: the same 547 prompted FEAR reports with 21 appraisals.
   - Compare three profiles: predicted harm (current); unresolved and uncontrollable (unpredictability, low own control, suddenness); urgent (urgency, attention).
   - If "unresolved" fits best, FEAR's row moves toward "an unresolved threat" and its overlap with Stress has to be separated on people, as V27 separated it in constructed worlds. If none fits, prompted fear events are too mixed to read from appraisals, and the row needs a different kind of data.
   - Why first: FEAR is the only tested trigger with no support, and two outside studies point the same way (some dopamine neurons answer unpredicted threat; people take shocks to resolve uncertainty).
2. **Control, FEAR and RAGE, with enough reports to see the registered effect.**
   - If the contrast stays flat with sensitivity passed, the brake cells "no expected control" (RAGE) and control quieting FEAR come out of the table as written.
3. **Where readers miss the writer, registered this time.**
   - Hypothesis: reader error against the writer is larger on stance items (acceptance of consequences, not considering consequences, familiarity) than on event items.
   - If it holds, it is the first measured statement of what a text does not carry, and it sets the engine's priority: the ladder, not the event.
4. **Laughter: fresh against repeat.** (Waits on Abraham's pick in Question 5.)
   - Data: meeting corpora with laughter marks, or chat logs with laugh markers.
   - Split laugh-getting lines by whether the same pair or group used the line or its template before.
   - Prediction (yours): repeats come without a preceding pause; fresh ones follow one.
   - If so, pauses separate habit jokes from made jokes, which gives the departure detector a clean positive class. If not, joking does not need attention and the laugh marker cannot stand in for it.
5. **Laughter with the social layer modeled first.**
   - Fit laugh rate by speaker role, turn position and who else laughed. Then test shared background on the residual.
   - The constructed-world result says the raw marker will mislead. If the residual predicts shared background where the raw rate did not, the three-layer account has its first support on people.
6. **A floor or a ladder.**
   - Data: chess, once storage is qualified; the phone game already in hand.
   - Do players change risk more after dropping below a personal reference (a rating floor, a previous peak) than after rising above it?
   - Floor-only behavior supports reading Standing as good standing. Symmetric striving supports rank. The game result is one vote for a floor, on a score that is not social.
7. **Is a trait a level or a sensitivity?**
   - Data: any source with many sessions per person.
   - A level predicts rebound toward the person's own usual amount after a forced high or low. A sensitivity predicts a bigger response per unit of input and no rebound.
   - Outside evidence says both exist (Fleeson 2002; Jacques-Hamilton 2019).
8. **Edit reasons, personal-habit version,** when coverage allows.
9. **Engine design note, not a study:** infer goal and habit jointly. Do not pick a channel and then re-read.
