## DESIGN CHECK

**Case and check.** Ten null worlds, ten criterion-only worlds, five stable
sensitivity worlds and five reversed worlds retain their original twelve
writers, eight sessions, sparse support, choice generator and recipient-excluded
population reference. Every world requires its original real fit plus all
1,000 support-conditioned reference refits and 2,000 paired writer draws.
The source builder reproduces all proposals and accepted assignments before
fitting. Blocks bind to the exact generated source and global assignment
indices. Missing, duplicate, mismatched or invalid fits cannot enter a passing
reduction. Native fixtures compare the actual original solver, block assembly
and full-count signed summary paths in separate fresh processes.

**Smaller route.** The Week 5 ceiling is 120,000 CPU-seconds, with all old
evidence retained. There is no smaller world or reference bank. Use the first
51 of 1,001 assignments in each selected pilot world inside a counted slot to
measure fit cost, including independent verification, then project all thirty
worlds, source generation and summary time. Work can use smaller execution
blocks without reducing scientific support. Amendment L permits checkpointed
idle-slot continuation within BK's remaining family ceiling beyond doubling.

## Execution partition

`week5_bk_wd1` exposes generated world source, assignment block, full world
reduction and campaign reduction. The source operation uses the unchanged
`week4_wd1_calibration_h2.world` and support bank. The block operation calls
`week3_sdt_newton.evaluate` directly; it never imports the rejected h3 solver.
All outputs and source pins are authenticated before each dependent operation.
Every fitted assignment has a sealed checkpoint; resource requests yield only
at complete fit boundaries. A fresh verifier recomputes every block's fits.

The final world reduction calls the original h2 vectorized summary, retaining
its complete original comparison and paired bootstrap. Campaign collection
requires exact family/ordinal/seed coverage and applies the original gate:
at most one positive in each ten-world null or criterion family, at least four
positives of five stable worlds, at least four negatives of five reversed
worlds, and no invalid or unsupported worlds. Failure remains an instrument
finding and holds corrected human inference. Passing remains a finite recovery
screen, not a calibrated human exchangeability claim.

The original human support source, profiles and permitted exact-profile reuse
remain available to a separately admitted corrected consumer after this gate.
This calibration continuation does not itself fit or score human behavior.
Population-relative sensitivity does not supply independent quality, competence,
payoffs, causal learning or the cost of resisting group convergence (G223/G234).
No participant text or identity enters committed results, queries or URLs.
