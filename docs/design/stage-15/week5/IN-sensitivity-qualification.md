# Complete native qualification of the IN sensitivity consumers

DESIGN CHECK: LESSONS 3–5. This fixture closes a validation gap; it does not
alter IN3.ic or IN4.ic. Their earlier proof exercised all five dose signs and
2,000 bootstrap draws, but only five worlds per dose. This successor runs the
full intended 100 worlds at each of −2, −1, 0, +1 and +2 practical bars, through
the unchanged source handler and complete fresh-process verification.

The IN3 constructed design retains the original FEAR/RAGE mapping, controls,
normalization, clustering and slope difference. The IN4 design retains all
four prompts, five readers per text, ten reader pairs and text-cluster
resampling. Exact nuisance-explained outcomes give known zero residual target
effects; all injected signed contrasts must recover their fixed dose within
1e-8. This is an arithmetic known answer, not evidence about human sensitivity.
The practical bars remain 0.15 and 0.10, respectively.

A separate fixed high-noise, forty-cluster design uses seed99 and outcome
noise scale1000, retaining all100 worlds per dose and2,000 draws. It must fail
the declared sensitivity rule, preserving a reachable failure direction.
Every failed draw remains governed by the unchanged source consumer; none is
discarded or replaced. This fixture does not select an empirical effect size.

Each constructed case runs through the real counted runtime. The first25 of
500 completed worlds produce a pause. A new attempt copies the complete
authenticated predecessor, finishes execution and pauses after the first25
worlds of independent verification. A third immutable attempt finishes that
verification and checks every final output hash, exited identity and released
lease. The fixture wrapper derives its operational quantum from saved world
count; all plans and scientific configuration stay byte-identical. It makes
no changes to the frozen production module or the resume adapter.

The original per-world seals bind all fixed source arrays, outcome, contrast,
draw and world counts and source code. Only constructed parquet/array inputs
are accepted, with a distinct pin manifest. All evidence stays private.
Qualification costs count against the IN family and relevant descendants;
the original IN3.ic half-parent limit is2,500 CPU seconds, IN4.ic5,000, and
the shared family limit60,000. A single attempt is bounded at1,200 CPU seconds.
After the measured first25-world pilot, continuation needs the owner's cost
review; no human run is licensed by a fixture receipt.
