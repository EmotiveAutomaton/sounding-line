## DESIGN CHECK

**Case and check.** Hold the returning population fixed for the first three
actual attempts. Require completed saved plays with counters exactly 1, 2 and 3,
and the original repaired repeat-history eligibility mask. Before fitting,
report every person with at least three saved plays, the people excluded for
missing each required counter, and ambiguous-history exclusions. Those counts
are not effects. Overlapping exclusion categories are explicitly marked.
The same people contribute actual-attempt bins 1, 2 and 3; fourth-and-later
plays can only be observed for those who return again, and that additional
selection is counted rather than disguised as full coverage. Missing counter
records never acquire invented choices, scores or order labels.

**Smaller route.** The first cost prefix belongs to the counted RR1 run.
Measured cost may prospectively select whole eligible people with the original
Week 5 seed, keeping all their eligible saved plays. Such an attempt is labelled
sample-scoped and keeps the same fixed actual-1/2/3 entry requirement. It cannot
be substituted into a full-cohort checkpoint. Remaining card/family allowance
and the unchanged resource floors must be checked before admission.
