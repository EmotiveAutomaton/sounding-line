## Source and fit

Reuse the complete pinned original IN-source numeric table. Read only
`event_duration`, `emotion_duration` and private text/writer join keys from the
already pinned archive. No text, demographic or personality fields are loaded.
Every qualified original row is retained in the private extension with ranks
and exclusion flags. Exact source writer/text matches are required. Missing
duration and `I had none` counts remain separate. Other undocumented category
spellings fail source qualification rather than being guessed. CC BY 4.0;
cite Troiano et al., DOI 10.1162/coli_a_00461, as a derived numeric projection.

The primary contains complete-duration anger, disgust, fear, guilt, sadness and
shame reports. Within each prompt, form raw JUMP = suddenness − predict_event
and NO-REMEDY = chance_control − self_control − accept_conseq, then standardize
each. Standardize intensity and ordered emotion duration within the same prompt.
Scales use the complete eligible source and remain fixed through resampling.

Both regressions have prompt intercepts, the two standardized indices,
unpleasantness, ordered event duration and the mean of familiarity,
goal_relevance, chance_responsblt, self_responsblt, attention and effort.
This excludes every item in either index from the response-style control.
One primary contrast is (JUMP-intensity − JUMP-duration) +
(NO-REMEDY-duration − NO-REMEDY-intensity), bar 0.10. Its four component slopes
are descriptive companions, not four new tests. Event duration in both fits
accounts for the constructed opportunity that a long event permits a long feeling.

Resample whole writers 2,000 times, preserving all their reports and refitting
both regressions each time. Production uses writer sufficient statistics;
independent replay forms weighted individual rows and uses a separate
least-squares solve. Scaling, source recoding and interval/SE calculations
also have separate reference formulas. Every failed draw retains its index;
any failure prevents an interval, with no redraw or selected-valid subset.
Fewer than 20 writers yields pooled descriptive inference; fewer than two
is unsupported. Missing negative prompts yields an explicitly incomplete,
descriptive pooled fit. P's sees/supports-at quantities are normal approximations.
