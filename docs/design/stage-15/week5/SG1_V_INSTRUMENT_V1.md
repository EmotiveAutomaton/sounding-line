must accompany almost no half-clock cases outside tournaments. Missing clocks
cannot dilute the latter rate. Constructed boundaries include 0, 0.45, 0.5,
0.55, 1, missing and outside ratios; independent comparison-based binning must
match the source histogram, while an altered source ratio must be refused.
Two-mode, one-mode, exactly-0.2-percent and denominator-empty cases determine
known pass/refusal answers without seeing human data.

Use the complete verified SG1 table. All zero-increment rows contribute to the
same source's twenty bins. Bins are half-open `[i/20,(i+1)/20)` with the final
bin including 1. The flag band is inclusive `[0.45,0.55]`. The modal test uses
bins 9 and 10, covering `[0.45,0.55)`; its peak must be strictly higher than
the exterior bins 8 and 11. The full-clock mode is bin 19, strictly above bin
18. An intervening bin 11 through 18 must be strictly below both peaks. This
is a declared coarse histogram operationalization, not a fitted mixture model.
The exact 0.55 endpoint belongs to bin 11 but remains in the inclusive flag
count. All raw bins and boundaries are retained for owner inspection.

The non-tournament flag rate denominator is finite observed ratios in `[0,1]`,
not missing or outside values. Those exclusions and the source's broader
observed denominator remain explicit. The rate must be strictly below 0.002;
an empty denominator is unsupported. Passing both rate and modal conditions

owner's separate CH10 admission. Failed checks hold CH10 and change no other card.
