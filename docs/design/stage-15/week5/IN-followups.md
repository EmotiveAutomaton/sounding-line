# IN3/IN4 instrument checks and IN1 itemwise branches

DESIGN CHECK: LESSONS 2–5 and CONTROLS 6–7. Sensitivity is calibrated on the parent's fixed design and original normalization, with all observations of a source cluster moving together. A known injected coefficient must recover its sign and magnitude; a high-noise constructed world must be able to fail the sensitivity requirement. The smaller route uses the parent's existing whole-cluster sample, if one exists, otherwise a prospectively frozen whole-cluster sample under the packet rule. A rank-deficient design is design-cannot-test, and fewer than twenty clusters retains descriptive counts.

IN3.ic preserves the original FEAR/RAGE design matrix, within-prompt standardization and difference-in-own-control-slope contrast; its practical bar is 0.15. IN4.ic uses the original five-reader/ten-pair error calculation, four prompt intercepts and equal-prompt contrast, bar 0.10 rating points. The source outcome is fitted once. Remove only the coefficient component along the tested contrast, preserving orthogonal nuisance coefficients, then generate whole-cluster Rademacher residual worlds. The original normalized design and response units remain fixed; post-plant standardization cannot silently resize the injection.

The fixed grid is −2, −1, 0, +1 and +2 practical bars, with 100 paired-seed worlds per point and 2,000 actual cluster-refitted draws per world. Report coefficient quantiles, interval coverage, zero-exclusion rates in the expected direction, and the unchanged parent's positive-bar classification rate. A truth at one practical bar is not expected to have its interval's lower edge above that same bar; zero exclusion is therefore the minimum-effect sensitivity measure, and never described as the parent's support decision. The predeclared sensitivity pass requires null zero exclusion at most 0.10 and at least 0.80 zero exclusion at both signed one-bar alternatives. Numerical zero uses 1e-10 in standardized/rating units. This is a fixed empirical-design residual calibration, not population power under every possible outcome distribution.

Each completed world is SHA-sealed with design, outcome, contrast, seed, draw count and code bindings. Twenty-five new worlds of the intended 500 form the counted five-percent pilot. A yield writes progress, never a completed result. Independent verification regenerates every world and interval. IN3.ic is capped at half IN3's 5,000-second allowance; IN4.ic at half IN4's 10,000-second allowance, within the existing 60,000-second IN family ceiling and the packet's continuation rule.

Only RAGE and DISGUST have the already-landed supported secondary channels authorizing IN1.w. Each branch fits every non-valence profile item separately in its frozen signed direction, retaining unpleasantness, confidence and the response-style control. It reports all item coefficients and 2,000 writer-cluster intervals; no best item is selected for a new primary. These are descriptive decompositions of the parent association, not independent biological-channel claims. The original 300-text secondary floor and twenty-writer floor retain their descriptive fallback. Each branch is capped at 7,500 CPU seconds, sharing the IN family allowance.
