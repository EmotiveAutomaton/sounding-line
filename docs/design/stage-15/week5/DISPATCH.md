# Week 5 reviewed-plan dispatcher

DESIGN CHECK: LESSONS 3–5. A private queue lists exact plan hashes and explicit
priorities. Only owner-prepared plans enter it; it never translates TODO into
studies. Every launch repeats design-commit, fixture, source, clock, resource,
lease and budget checks. Changed bytes refuse under every scientific outcome.

Completed, failed, paused or already-attempted identities never restart. The
owner must document a recovery and prepare its distinct new identity. Resource
waits occupy no research slot. The dispatcher writes its native identity,
heartbeat and each actual launch; the durable watcher monitors terminal events.
It stops starting at the November 15 20:00 PST freeze and exits at drain.
It never commits, pushes, interprets results or acknowledges landing events.

Targeted fixtures check priority, terminal guards, duplicate identities and
tampered plan rejection. Native scientific launches retain their own complete
runtime evidence. The case is transport and admission, not scientific validity;
the smaller route is the same finite queue check.
