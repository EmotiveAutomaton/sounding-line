# RF1: qualified elections, signed reads and candidate uncertainty

Prospective implementation, November 13, 2026. This extends the frozen signed
index V2 without changing it. The two-day packet commissions RF1; the owner
approved the signed-three encoding and the descriptive against-comment definition
before any source fit. Neither this note nor the prepared native requests is
committed/admitted evidence. R13–R15 supporting handoffs remain absent; the
self-contained packet is the authority. Original V1 and V2 files remain intact.

The question is whether separately reading respect, trust and liking improves
prediction of later votes over every specified two-read pairing. Words about an
office candidate do not establish felt emotion or a general social ontology.

## DESIGN CHECK

- **Case and check.** An explicitly identified election has at least 20 comments,
  an order by its recorded times, early nonneutral votes and later nonneutral
  votes. All three signed reads must add three independent dimensions beyond
  year and early support. Count every exclusion; an absent case is `design
  cannot test`, never a null and never a reason to launch an instrument follow-up.
- **Identity is a source gate.** The original SNAP source has no election ID.
  Candidate, year, outcome, date gaps and record contiguity do not supply one.
  Require a separate native explicit-identifier mapping, original-evidence pin,
  complete fresh replay, reviewed producer hash and owner qualification note.
  Every usable salted original comment ID must occur once. Salt election IDs too.
  Current SNAP intake alone fails deliberately. No alternate source or retrieval
  has been silently substituted.
- **Instrument.** The frozen three-family lists and complete source/manual gate
  remain unchanged: each family occurs in at least 3% of source comments and at
  least 80 of exactly 100 frozen, privately reviewed hits are correct. A source
  hash, template receipt or constructed label cannot qualify human semantics.
- **Ruler and null.** Twelve literal comments retain hand counts and overlapping
  polarities. An authored raw-source world has known TWO-A loss disadvantage
  `0.5 × (log(2) − H(0.8))`. A second world has identical 0.5 outcomes and must
  produce exact zero plus practical-null classification. A third has known
  against-trust log odds and an independently computed probability contrast.
- **Dependence.** Candidates, including all their elections, stay together in
  five fixed folds and in each of 2,000 resamples. Every global draw and invalid
  fit is retained. There is no invalid-draw deletion or favorable redraw.
- **Floor and smaller route.** Below 20 candidates, run the pooled descriptive fit
  automatically, with no interval or sensitivity claim. If actual counted cost
  invokes amendment E, use a prospectively reviewed hash-ranked whole-candidate
  sample, retaining every election of each selected candidate. Its size and cost
  evidence remain unset until measured; no arbitrary sample or forecast is filed.
- **Computation.** CPU only, at most two numerical threads, RF family 40,000 CPU
  seconds. Actual first cost and any required first 5% pilot occur inside the
  counted run. No real-source admission or new-family runtime clearance is
  supplied here. Individual fit latency, total native fixture cost and peak
  memory remain unmeasured.
- **Privacy.** All comment rows, maps, predictions and bootstrap payloads remain
  ignored/local. No participant identifiers enter URLs, queries or committed
  outputs. The SNAP software-licence note does not assert permission to distribute
  the comment text. Only aggregate owner-reviewed records may be published.

## Exact source boundary

`week5_rf_qualified_v1` authenticates the original source and lexical-instrument
native receipts and frozen review labels. It then requires an
`RF1-EXPLICIT-ELECTION-IDENTITIES-1` table, evidence and replay proof inside another
complete native root. The expected module and its current file hash, completion
hash, table/evidence hashes and original archive/source binding must agree with
an explicit owner review. The method is `explicit_original_election_identifiers`;
heuristic grouping is false. Synthetic native envelopes are limited to explicitly
constructed fixtures. No actual identity producer or qualified human table is
currently available; the future producer requires owner review before use.

Within an already identified election, sort by parsed timestamp and original
ordinal for ties. The first `floor(N/3)` comments are early. Report ties crossing
that cutoff. Neutrals stay in the split and lexical denominator but leave both
binomial vote counts. The early support share uses early support and oppose only.
Candidate/year inconsistency or an unparseable clock refuses the source rather
than inventing chronology. Elections lacking early or later nonneutral votes
retain an explicit unsupported disposition.

## Frozen primary

For each of respect, trust and liking, use
`(for-comment count − against-comment count) / early-comment count`. Repeated
lexical hits in one comment do not multiply its presence. Keep all six presence
counts, mention counts, denominators and overlap counts. Both polarities in one
comment cancel only in that signed rate. THREE fits three rates; each TWO adds
the named two rates literally, producing two fitted reads. Year fixed effects
and early support are shared. The unchanged V2 finite unpenalized binomial MLE
retains its convergence, Hessian, training-support and rank refusals; no ridge,
clipping or favorable predictor deletion is introduced.

Candidate folds use the frozen 20261112 hash order. Average each model's held-out
log loss per later nonneutral vote equally across elections. The primary is the
smallest of the three TWO mean losses minus THREE's loss, with the best TWO
reselected inside each bootstrap draw. The practical bar is 0.002 nats. Whole
candidate draws use the original 20261107 global-index seed. Full 2,000-draw
percentile intervals and bootstrap SE yield P's `sees=2.8×SE` and
`supports_at=0.002+2.8×SE`. Unsupported draws invalidate uncertainty rather than
becoming zeros. The pooled below-floor fit is explicitly in-sample/descriptive.

## Approved descriptive against-comment contrast

The packet's per-mention language is operationalized prospectively as one extra
early comment containing the family's against marker. Fit six separate
comment-presence rates, shared year terms and early support on the full primary
cohort. This six-coefficient diagnostic is never the primary THREE model.

For each family, add its against marker to one previously neither-polarity
comment. Hold early N, its for-count, overlap and every other predictor fixed.
This is feasible only when `N − (for + against − overlap) ≥1`. Evaluate every
family on the common feasible subset and report exclusions and candidate support.
The full primary cohort is unchanged. If the fitted against coefficient is
`b`, the election contrast is `sigmoid(eta+b/N)−sigmoid(eta)`. Average equally over
feasible elections. Refit the model in the same whole-candidate bootstrap draws;
report descriptive family contrasts and paired differences. Below 20 feasible
candidates, keep only the descriptive point. Constant or unidentified against
coefficients are unsupported, not zero costs. These fitted associations carry
no causal interpretation or separate scientific threshold.

## Checkpoint and independent replay

The consumer seals four global draws per immutable block. Resume authenticates
source, identity review, configuration, modules, selected rows, block hashes and
global indices. It retains failed fits. Resource requests are checked between
fold fits and draws and at bounded ancestor paths for nested native fixtures.
There is no claim yet about the largest indivisible fit's measured latency.

The formal verifier creates its own replay tree, reconstructs the complete
election table and refits every draw. A separately written Counter implementation
checks candidate weights; direct scalar arithmetic checks binomial loss and
equal-election averaging. The native fixture also creates a new raw-source
reconstruction only in its external verifier phase. Execute-time preparatory
source validation cannot substitute for that fresh process. Every original,
unsupported, checkpointed and replay artifact remains retained.

## Delivery and limits

Canonical files: `week5_rf_qualified_v1`, `week5_rf_analysis_v3`,
`week5_rf_consumer_fixture_v1`, `week5_rf_prefix_v1` and
`test_week5_rf_consumer_v1.py`. The native
fixture candidate covers three complete worlds, including 2,000 candidate draws
each. The targeted suite exercises the actual authored raw-source boundary,
original numerical agreement, refusals, neutral denominators, below-floor and
absent-case routes, candidate weights, descriptive probability truth, sealed
continuation and resource yielding. Neither targeted checks nor full native
execution has run at the time of this design freeze; later receipts must state
their actual scope. Human source IDs, semantic review, measured envelopes,
runtime family clearance and owner admission remain outstanding.

The prefix wrapper supplies separate, explicitly bounded first-cost (four global
draws) and first-five-percent (100 of 2,000 global draws) stages using the exact
consumer. Each creates its own fresh replay before completion. It exposes hashes
and work coverage, never unfinished per-artifact estimates or an invented cost.
Actual native accounting must include both passes and load costs. A no-case or
below-floor path reports zero observed draws and is ineligible for a whole-run
forecast. No complete inference claim can come from either prefix. Any reuse of
these checkpoints in a later full native attempt requires owner-authenticated
continuation under the runtime's existing exact-source rules.
