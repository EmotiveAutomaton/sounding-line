## Exact primary encoding

For each read, the early-vote index is `(comments with a for marker - comments
with an against marker) / early comment count`. A comment contributes at most
one positive and at most one negative indicator to that read, regardless of
the number of matching phrases. If both polarities occur, their contribution
cancels in the signed index only. Preserve each polarity's comment count,
their overlap count, each polarity's total mention count and the denominator.

THREE fits three signed predictors: respect, trust and liking. TWO-A fits
respect and trust-plus-liking; TWO-B fits respect-plus-trust and liking; TWO-C
fits respect-plus-liking and trust. These are literal sums, without adaptive
weights. The same year effects and early support share enter every model.
The six-coefficient polarity representation is an unrun descriptive sensitivity,
not the primary and not a source of outcome-dependent model selection.

Candidate folds, equal-election averaging of per-later-vote binomial log loss,
whole-candidate bootstrap refits, seeds, the 0.002-nat bar and numerical refusal
rules remain as prospectively described in V1. Source eligibility, exact
early/late ordering and all collection gates still require qualified election
identities. The primitive accepts already qualified early membership; it never
creates it from candidate-year, result or a date gap. Negative bootstrap
weights are now refused before active-row projection, a static input-contract
repair detected before RF numerical execution.

## Against-family secondary: separate proposal for owner review

Proposed diagnostic: fit a separate six-polarity **mention-rate** binomial model
with the same nuisance columns, where each rate is total mentions divided by
early comments. For each election and each against family, add one mention to
its early total (a predictor increment of `1 / early comment count`) and compute
the fitted support-probability change while holding the other inputs fixed.
Average that change equally over elections; whole-candidate bootstrap refits
provide intervals for each family and their pairwise contrasts. This is a
model-implied association per additional mention, not a causal effect or a new
primary. It does not change the three signed primary indexes. No practical bar
is borrowed for the secondary. This diagnostic is proposed, not implemented or
admitted pending the owner's scale decision.
