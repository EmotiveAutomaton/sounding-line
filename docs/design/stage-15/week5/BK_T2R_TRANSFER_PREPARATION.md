# T2.r transfer preparation: frozen model and final-text boundary

**Preparation only; owner review and native qualification pending.** The historical
source remains held, and the proposed recent-window derivative in
[the intake design](BK_PUBLIC_INTAKES.md) has not been admitted. This note does
not freeze a changed population, rewrite label, exclusion rule or estimand.

**DESIGN CHECK.** Week 4 T2/T2.r, Week 5 BK/K–M, the original
[T2 design](BK_T2.md), LESSONS 2–5 and the current source qualification apply.
The case is whether a model of recorded process trained on co-writing logs also
detects prior rewriting in final Wikipedia sentences. The check is exact
serialized-model agreement with the unchanged CoAuthor ridge implementation,
known constructed signal/null/baseline worlds, complete feature isolation from
external outcomes, and deterministic markup projection with explicit refusals.
The smaller route remains the packet's whole-cluster sample/descriptive fallback
under BK. An unsupported or ambiguous source cannot become a negative label.

The task is **retrospective process detection from final text**, as the packet
states. Final wording may be a consequence of earlier revision; that is part of
the commissioned signal. It is not a forecast of future revision. An
introduction-text forecast would be a different estimand and is not implemented
or proposed for admission here. Neither final text nor model output may be used
to choose the article roster, parser support policy or rewrite threshold.

## Outcome-independent model export

The exporter requires the authenticated complete T2 feature collection and its
same complete 1,399-session, 61-writer CoAuthor source. Every final sentence and
label must match that source; undefined target-(b) rows remain in the census and
do not become negatives. Dictionary, encoder and feature-width pins must equal
the original collection. Only target (b), the primary ridge implementation and
the position/length baseline are exported. Argumentative, creative and tree
textures do not silently replace the transfer model.

Fit all available CoAuthor target-(b) training rows once after the authenticated,
complete controlled primary writer-separated evaluation, including all 2,000
draws and 200 refitted permutations. A useful result is not an admission condition:
a valid null remains eligible; an invalid instrument does not. The five-fold validation remains separate evidence;
an all-training-row fit is the deployment model, not a new validation result.
Export exact train means, standard deviations, retained columns, coefficients,
intercepts, class mapping, hyperparameters and software version as private JSON.
The fixed classifier and all-constant-column behavior are identical to T2.
No Wikipedia label, article identity, edit time, revision count or external feature
distribution enters training, scaling or hyperparameter selection. Prediction
accepts only baseline and full numeric text-feature matrices. The complete
independent verifier refits both models from CoAuthor source features.

Single-class training, invalid features, changed input coverage or non-convergence
refuse export. The original diagnostic counts remain available from T2; a failed
export supplies no external probability bank. The solver is a restart unit:
owner requests are observed between complete fits, not inside L-BFGS. Actual
fit cost and the maximum uninterruptible interval require measured admission;
this preparation does not claim a sixty-second solver boundary. Any yielded
export restarts in a fresh empty attempt. No copied fit is credited as replay.

## Parser boundary, without lineage labels

The new parser accepts final wikitext only. It drops nested templates and
template variables, balanced wiki/HTML tables and references; visible ordinary
link labels and HTML text formatting remain. Other extension tags are unsupported.
It strips wiki emphasis/list/heading markers, decodes entities after structural
processing, and applies the unchanged T2 sentence-boundary rule. It records each
removed construct. No model, metadata or history influences extraction.

This is a **conservative supported-markup subset**, not a MediaWiki-renderer
equivalence claim. Unknown tags, unsupported link namespaces, unbalanced markup,
nowiki constructs, magic words and signatures refuse the source article with a
fixed reason code that contains no source text. Those refusals must remain in
the frozen roster denominator. The original one-percent collection allowance
does not excuse incorrect projection; support above the refusal allowance
requires a separately reviewed parser successor before scoring. Real-source
parser coverage and a broader rendering comparison have not run. No dependency
has been installed or changed for this preparation.

The unresolved consumer still needs a reviewed exact token-change denominator,
current-sentence lineage through splits/merges/deletions, repeated-hash reversion
exclusions, bot-flag qualification and left/right-censoring contract. The
recent-window source can establish event flags, not historical bot-account status
or independently verified human authorship. Its inactive and ambiguous articles
must remain distinct from negative rewrite outcomes. These choices are held for
owner review and are not decided by the parser or exporter.

The separate feature bridge accepts only private article/sentence ordinals,
final text, relative position and sentence completion. Its exact field allowlist
rejects outcome, edit-history and identity metadata. It reuses the original T2
lexical function, authenticated 150-word dictionary and pinned CPU encoder;
no feature tuning or sentence truncation is introduced. At most 64 sentences
form a batch. A prepared constructed fixture compares its actual encoder and
lexical outputs against direct original calls, then recomputes every forward in
fresh verification. This fixture also has not run. It requires the existing
dictionary and encoder manifests and a separate proposed 300-CPU-second,
1,200-wall-second, 2-GiB counted allowance inside the same T2/BK accounting.

Encoder owner checks occur at whole-vector boundaries in the unchanged reader.
A sentence with many 126-token chunks may exceed the runtime's safe yield
interval. Whole-source length/token support and a measured longest-unit bound
are required before any human batch is admitted; no long sentence is silently
truncated or removed to satisfy that bound. If needed, a separately fixtured
exact chunk-checkpoint successor is the required remedy. This feature bridge is
not a human-source handler and does not release the held lineage construction.

## Independent article uncertainty boundary

The prepared numerical kernel accepts an already frozen prediction/outcome bank;
it has no human runtime entry point or label constructor. It computes the same
paired area-under-the-curve contrast as T2 and 2,000 article-cluster resamples,
with the fixed seed. Resampling includes every article in the frozen roster,
including inactive or entirely censored articles. Within an article, all eligible
sentences receive the same multiplicity. Missing/ambiguous outcomes never become
negatives; duplicate sentences, foreign article keys and invalid probabilities
are rejected. Undefined draws remain recorded and withhold the interval.

Fewer than twenty articles with labelled sentences gives a pooled descriptive
comparison. The interval is conditional on the frozen CoAuthor fit and external
prediction bank, with no training-uncertainty claim. Practical-bar indicators are
statistics only; the kernel cannot certify the held source mapping or license a
scientific claim. It does not add a new outcome-dependent model, threshold or
permutation analysis. The constructed fixture computes the complete interval in
each of its three known worlds and preserves censored/inactive denominator units.

## Prepared validation and cost accounting

The prepared counted fixture fits three complete constructed worlds, serializes
and reloads both models, compares every probability with the original pooled T2
fit, checks sensitivity and exact null/baseline-only behavior, changes external
labels/identities without changing predictions, and rejects malformed training
banks. Parser controls cover nested templates/variables, nested tables,
references, visible links, exact output and seven unsupported-structure refusals.
Both handlers replay every calculation from source; no saved verification prefix
is accepted. A separate actual owner-yield/restart/native-exit test remains
required before admission. The fixture itself has not run.

Suggested counted fixture ceiling: 300 CPU seconds including execute and fresh
verification, 1,200 wall seconds, 2 GiB peak, two numerical threads, charged to
BK and the original T2/T2.r accounting. This is a proposed safety allowance, not
a measured cost forecast. The original 5,400 CPU-second ceiling and BK family
continuation rules remain. Source intake, parser qualification, final model
export, encoder batches, article uncertainty and every failed attempt are charged.
There is no new paid, GPU or Ollama authority.
