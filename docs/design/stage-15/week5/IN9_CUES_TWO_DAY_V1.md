## Directed secondary and full uncertainty

For each qualified family, the 2×2 cells are shame-with-cue (a), guilt-with-cue
(b), shame-without-cue (c), guilt-without-cue (d). Report the unadjusted odds ratio
a*d/(b*c) and its log. Thus the contrast is odds of the shame prompt against
guilt when that literal cue is present, relative to when it is absent. Lists
can overlap; each comparison is separate, without conditioning on other cues
or adding outcome-selected covariates. No practical threshold, causal claim,
supports/against label or p-value is invented for these directed secondaries.

Resample whole writers with replacement2000 times using seed20261113 and a
fixed draw-index substream, retaining every report of each selected writer.
No list, source inclusion or semantic judgment is refitted on these draws.
Every zero-cell or separated resample remains indexed; no clipping, smoothing,
continuity correction, deletion or redraw. Any such draw prevents a finite
population interval for that cue; its supported point estimate and raw cells
remain descriptive. Fewer than20 writers is descriptive; fewer than two has
no population resampling. Exact-zero empirical spread is also descriptive,
never artificial precision. Finite complete banks report the percentile95%
interval, bootstrap standard error and2.8×SE on the log-odds scale. Supports-at
is unset because the packet assigns no cue practical bar.

Production computes cross-products of weighted cell counts. Independent replay
loops over weighted rows, computes the difference of conditional label logits,
and independently sorts/interpolates intervals and calculates sample variance.
All discrete support/failure fields agree exactly; finite values agree within
