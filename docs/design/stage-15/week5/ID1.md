# ID1 chronological and source-support qualification

DESIGN CHECK: LESSONS 2–5 and CONTROLS 6–7. A high saving-human level is not quantity insensitivity. Only the logit-scale quantity slope is primary. Exposure is matched exactly, first-half decisions define the group, and second-half Random scenarios provide the outcome. Orphaned/duplicate pairs, ambiguous chronology, missing character counts, separation and rank failure cannot become null effects.

The admitted SharedResponses archive remains pinned by SHA-256. Only response/session/fingerprint keys, scenario order, intervention/saved flags, scenario types and fictional character counts are read. Source participant demographics are not loaded. Keys are salted before derived storage. Complete choices require two rows, one saved side, complementary intervention states, matching metadata and valid integer character totals. Pure humans-versus-pets cases require one side containing only humans and the other only pets. Species cases additionally have matching strict Species labels and equal character totals; Random cases are read directly from the character columns and the Random source type. Quantity is the base-two logarithm of pets divided by humans.

The archive exposes within-session scenario order but no intersession timestamp. The [original publication](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-018-0637-6) describes completed 13-scenario sessions. The chronological primary therefore admits fingerprints with exactly one observed session, all thirteen complete paired scenarios, and unique orders 1 through 13. Orders 1–6 are the first global scenario half; orders 7–13 are the second. Missing or additional orders and multi-session fingerprints are counted and excluded. Source encounter order is never called temporal order.

First-half Species exposure must be at least three, preserving B2's minimum three-scenario support rule. Steady refusers saved humans on every such first-half Species case. Only second-half pure-human-versus-pet Random cases are tested. Exact exposure strata must contain both steady refusers and other fingerprints; unmatched strata are counted exclusions. The required case is at least 500 outnumbered Random scenarios among steady refusers. The case census runs before any effect fit. If a complete single session cannot supply this support, the primary is design-cannot-test with descriptive counts, not a substituted random-half result.

The separately named multi-session companion uses a fixed seeded random held-out half of retained relevant scenarios because intersession chronology is unavailable. It is non-temporal and descriptive; it cannot replace the chronological primary or license a claim of temporal preference stability. The same three-case exposure minimum, exact exposure matching and outnumbered support count apply.

The logistic model has exposure-stratum intercepts, the steady-refuser level indicator, quantity, and their interaction. The interaction is steady-minus-other quantity slope, the single primary with bar 0.2 log-odds per doubling. Both group slopes and the group level difference are reported. Whether the steady slope is actually closer to zero is stated explicitly; a positive difference alone is not silently described as flattening when both slopes point the other way. All 2,000 fingerprint-cluster draws refit the likelihood; inactive exposure-intercept columns are algebraically removed only when their bootstrap weight is zero. Primary rank loss, separation, non-convergence or any failed draw is unsupported, without discarding or redrawing it. No regularization is added to force a finite answer.

The initial ID1 ceiling is 20,000 CPU seconds inside the 40,000 family ceiling. An in-slot source prefix may measure cost and schema only; it is never a scientific population. Source replay, case census, all fits and independent verification count. Source data, per-fingerprint rows and predictions remain private; aggregate case and result receipts alone may be published.
