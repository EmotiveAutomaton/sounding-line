## MD1 and MD2

First ten ratings train, final two test. Training ratings alone determine
participant means and standard deviations; constant training ratings are counted
and excluded. A seeded 5,000-participant exploratory split tunes the ridge arm
and is set aside from primary inference. The shared forgetting grid is
0.05, 0.10, …, 0.90. Common coefficients and participant intercepts are fitted by
within-participant training centering. MD1 keeps M0's selected forgetting factor
for its level arm. MD2 jointly includes spread and absolute-EV curvature.
Spread centering/scaling uses chosen training gambles only.

Participant sufficient statistics exactly implement the pooled least-squares
fits. All 2,000 cluster bootstrap draws refit and reselect the common forgetting
factor from training loss; no held-out outcome enters selection. MD2's reported
standardized coefficient multiplies the raw coefficient by the original
training residual standard deviation after projecting spread on the other
regressors. This reference scale is held fixed across resamples. Private
checkpoint blocks retain actual draws, seed and full input/implementation
bindings; partial output is not a completed card.

The original MD1 decision bands stay: at least 1% held-out gain with a nonzero
level interval is against the momentum-only claim; below 0.5% with zero in the
interval supports only within demonstrated sensitivity; all other cases are
inconclusive. Both signed level plants are reported, and the fitted-sign 2%
training residual-variance plant must clear the original 1% error-gain control.
MD2's practical bar is 0.01 training-standardized happiness per residualized
spread SD. Its floor is 10,000 participants with at least five chosen mixed
gambles and absolute mixed-spread/absolute-EV correlation below 0.9. Below that
floor the pooled fit still runs, explicitly descriptive, without upgrading it.

The published 2014 smartphone cohort is not byte-identical to this later archive:
the source documents subsequent deletions affecting fewer than 2% of records.
The requested 92 blocks of 200 consecutive participants are reported as a
historical sign-pattern consistency check on the current source, never an exact
original-cohort recreation. A missed sign pattern is retained, not silently
turned into a pass. The bundled script's numerical/count checks are reported
separately from construction validation.

## MD3

The inherited TD3 logistic model, both standing slopes, recent-outcome and
earlier-gamble controls, whole-participant resampling, case counts and dynamic
null trajectories remain unchanged. Counterfactual simulation draws fair gamble
endpoints from the actual offered alternatives and regenerates every dependent
history; simulated endpoints are labelled constructed, never recovered actual
counterfactual outcomes. Its bar is at least 0.01 log-odds per training standing
SD and at least twice the 20-null mean absolute slope plus three null SDs.

The pre-outcome Week 5 companion is below-zero minus above-zero slope. Positive
favours a defended reference point over a smooth falling value; near zero fits
falling marginal value; negative is a rival pattern. House money is named only
when both slopes are positive; break-even only when the lower slope is negative.
A game score remains a weak proxy for security. Repeat plays use best earlier
completed score as an explicitly incomplete-history reference when missing
attempts intervene. Counts and missing histories accompany that comparison.
