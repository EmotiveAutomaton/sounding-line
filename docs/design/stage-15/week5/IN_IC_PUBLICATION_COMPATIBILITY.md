# IN sensitivity publication compatibility

This operational build completes the production entry for the already
commissioned [IN3.ic and IN4.ic](IN-followups.md). Their original
[constructed qualifications](IN-sensitivity-qualification.md) used the isolated
UUID publisher. This adapter makes that publisher available to the original
matrix, calibrator and native execute/verify paths without editing admitted
sources. No hypothesis, source, estimator, seed, dose, threshold or refit changes.

DESIGN CHECK: LESSONS sections 3–5 and CONTROLS sections 6–7 reread.
The exact constructed IN3 and IN4 cases must recover zero under the null and
both signs at their fixed planted doses, passing their original sensitivity
gate. The original high-noise case must fail that gate. All cases retain five
doses, 100 worlds per dose and 2,000 cluster refits per world. Every saved world
and the complete summary must equal the qualified baseline, including during
fresh independent replay. Any difference refuses qualification.

The publisher is installed only around the original calibrator and is restored
on both success and exception. Checkpoints must form the original ordered prefix
with exactly one matching seal per world. Extra, changed or orphaned files are
refused. An original failed fit may terminate the prefix and remains an invalid
sensitivity result; it cannot count as a complete successful calibration.
Rank-deficient and below-floor source dispositions remain unchanged. Verification
compares every individual world as well as the summary, including failed results.

The immutable configuration contains only phase, 100 worlds and 2,000 draws.
The first execute and fresh-verify invocations stop after the original 25-world
pilot; the complete authenticated prefix determines the operational quantum on
continuation. No numerical configuration changes between attempts. The native
qualification compares the actual baseline and production entry points on the
already completed IN3/IN4 constructed source files and original high-noise array.
The latter exercises the same production calibrator; it is never human evidence.
Three native attempts per case cover execution pilot, verification pilot and
complete independent replay. No partial control bank admits human studies.

Each attempt is bounded by 400 CPU seconds, 3,600 wall seconds, two numerical
threads, three GiB projected memory and two GiB retained output. Preparation
and focused checks retain a conservative 300-CPU-second charge, split equally
between IN3.ic and IN4.ic. Reconcile all previous qualifications, failed attempts,
supervisor allowances and human work before each admission, retaining the
existing unmeasured preparation reservation. IN3.ic retains its 2,500-second
ceiling, IN4.ic its 5,000-second ceiling, and IN its 60,000-second family ceiling;
the packet's existing continuation rules apply without inventing an allowance.
