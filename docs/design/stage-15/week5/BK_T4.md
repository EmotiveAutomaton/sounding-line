## Frozen calculations

The point contrast remains later-half mean log probability of the observed
choice: individual-temperature minus shared-temperature model. The training
half alone estimates all parameters and scaling. Dismissal keeps its own
writer intercept in both versions, outside temperature. Positive direction
was the original packet criterion; no new practical bar is manufactured.
With 2,000 complete writer refits, an interval entirely above zero supports a
scoped predictive benefit, entirely below zero is against, and otherwise is
inconclusive. No causal competence identification follows from this contrast.

Whole-writer bootstrap draws resample twenty-five writers, retaining every
early and later menu. Each occurrence of a duplicated writer is a separate
bootstrap group. Training standardization is recomputed from each draw's
retained real options and state values; impossible padded alternatives remain
excluded. Refit both models in every draw. Report the spread (standard deviation
of log temperature), temperature quantiles and the requested descriptive
correlations of temperature with the original identification margin and mean

Missing auxiliary data never changes the primary sample.

A fixed writer's parameter interval cannot come from resampling only whole
writers. Therefore a separate conditional parametric training-choice bootstrap
generates choices from the frozen individual fit, leaves the observed feature
and state paths fixed, and refits the same individual model 2,000 times. Its
per-writer intervals are explicitly conditional on that model and observed
paths; they do not represent unconditional histories or causal ability. They
remain private. This obligation and its cost are included before admission.

Two hundred reference draws permute labels within writer, temporal half and
number of offered options, so no choice becomes padded and dismissal remains
possible. Both models refit. This is a feature-association diagnostic whose
exchangeability is not asserted: it is reported separately, without a p-value
or a claim that it controls the human contrast's false-positive rate.
