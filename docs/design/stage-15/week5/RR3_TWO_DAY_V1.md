## Frozen computation

Input is the complete independently verified RR1 actual-attempt continuation.
Its producer plan supplies the original MD-source.h1 and MD3 dependencies.
Authenticate complete output manifests and native metadata, original archive/MAT
pins, full source replay, gamma 0.65, and exact RR1 census. The fixed cohort has
completed actual attempts 1, 2 and 3 and unambiguous repeat eligibility. Keep
all its later observed plays; fourth-or-later absence is counted, not imputed.
Retain all original missing-counter and ambiguous-history exclusions.

For each trial, subtract the chosen option's expected value from the observed
outcome. A gamble's expectation is the equal-probability mean of its two offered
outcomes; a certain choice uses its offered certain value. RECENT is the mean
of the preceding three such errors in the same play. Trial 2 uses its one prior
and trial 3 its two priors. Exclude trial 1, which has no prior outcome; do not
impute a zero error or borrow the end of the preceding play. Count each of these
support cases. Current and future outcomes never enter the current predictor.

The first eight columns and binary gamble outcome are the unchanged RR1
play-start model. Keep the original global first-play standard deviations,
including the original common standing standard deviation for both distance
slopes. Divide RECENT by its standard deviation over trials 2–30 of the same
global first-play source. These scales are fixed before bootstrap and common
to all panels. Participant intercepts absorb the constant centering offset;
the division convention matches RR1. Do not normalize by attempt bin.

The one primary pools every eligible observed play in that fixed population:
absolute RECENT coefficient minus the larger absolute distance coefficient.
The practical bar is 0.05. Report a complete percentile 95% interval, bootstrap
standard error, `sees = 2.8 SE`, `supports_at = 0.05 + 2.8 SE`, limited
sensitivity and degenerate-bootstrap flags. These approximations are optimistic.
Above the positive bar favors the recent-error association; below the negative
bar favors the distance association; an interval inside the practical band is
a band-null; otherwise the comparison is inconclusive. Choices are not feelings.

RECENT/below-distance and RECENT/above-distance correlations on the primary

Every result has `record_kind=claim`, `theory_tags=[H15-T6]`,
