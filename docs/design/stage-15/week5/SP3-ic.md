# SP3.ic: fixed-design sensitivity at the thermal-distance bar

DESIGN CHECK: LESSONS 3–5 and CONTROLS 6–7. The registered SP3 comparison
is inconclusive. Its sensitivity is checked at the fixed practical size,
never chosen from the empirical point estimate. Signed known alternatives
must recover their direction and a high-noise construction must be capable
of failing the sensitivity rule. Failed draws remain in the denominator and
invalidate that world's interval; no support redraw or deletion is allowed.

Keep the exact parent source projection, building eligibility, whole-building
resampling frame and three-degree outdoor-distance bins. Compute each building's
original sensation slope by the parent's complete summary handler. SP3 fits
the weather-linked naturally ventilated buildings, but its bootstrap samples
all eligible buildings before selecting that subset. Preserve that distinction:
sampling only the analytic subset would change the parent's resampling law.

The construction fixes every observed building and distance. Within the
analytically eligible naturally ventilated subset, fit slope on distance-bin
centre. Remove only that fitted distance coefficient, preserving the fitted
intercept and each building's residual magnitude. For each of 100 worlds,
multiply residuals by independent whole-building Rademacher signs and add
exactly one of five fixed distance coefficients: −0.02, −0.01, 0, +0.01 and
+0.02. The bar is the original 0.01 change in sensation-per-degree slope per
outdoor-distance degree. Signs are paired across doses with seed 20261107;
they never depend on an observed primary estimate's sign or size.

Refit the original OLS contrast for all 2,000 original whole-frame building
bootstrap draws per world. Linear sufficient-statistic operators may reuse
the unchanged design and draw allocations, but an independent expanded-row
implementation must match every draw on native fixtures. The operator keeps
the original sampled full-frame multiplicities and every support failure.
Recomputing within-building slopes after duplicating a whole building is
algebraically identical; a raw-response planted fixture explicitly checks
this identity. The human sensitivity check remains conditional on estimated
building summaries, just as the parent's building bootstrap does.

Report every signed dose's coefficient distribution, 95% interval coverage,
zero-exclusion rate in its expected direction and original negative-direction
primary classification rate. Sensitivity passes only when the null's zero
exclusion is at most 0.10 and both signed one-bar alternatives exclude zero
in at least 0.80 of worlds. Truth exactly at a bar is not expected to have
its interval wholly beyond that bar; zero exclusion is distinct from the
parent's directional support decision. This is conditional empirical-design
calibration, not population power for all noise laws or evidence of absence.

At least 20 eligible full-frame buildings and 20 analytic buildings are
required for the calibration decision. Below either floor, retain the pooled
descriptive slope, support counts and a below-floor label; no new directional
claim. An absent or constant weather-distance predictor is design-cannot-test.
All original missing weather, cooling category and building eligibility
exclusions are retained. No identifiers or response rows leave private storage.

Each complete world saves all 2,000 estimates and a source/design/code-bound
SHA seal. The first 25 of 500 worlds is the exact 5% counted pilot. Yield at
that boundary, on resource requests or the admitted wall quantum; a fresh
attempt copies only an authenticated complete stopped manifest. Independent
verification reconstructs every world, summary and numeric draw array before
completion. All failed worlds, failed draws and prior costs remain recorded.

Native controls require full signed/null worlds, all-draw equality to direct
expanded-row OLS, raw-response-to-building-summary parity, a rank/support
failure, an achievable high-noise sensitivity failure, immutable pause/resume,
tampered-checkpoint refusal and complete independent replay. No source run
starts until these and owner cost admission pass.

The first-generation follow-up ceiling is 5,000 CPU seconds, half SP3's
10,000-second allowance, within the shared 40,000-second SP family. Count
qualification, setup, pilot, failures and full verification. If necessary,
freeze a packet-seed whole-building sample before outcomes and retain all
records of selected buildings. Floor failure is descriptive; insufficient
remaining budget is cost-held. No smaller resample count or selected dose
grid is a fallback. This note and unqualified consumer authorize no launch.
