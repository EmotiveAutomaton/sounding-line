# SP3.ic publication compatibility

This is an operational qualification of the already commissioned [SP3.ic](SP3-ic.md).
It adds no hypothesis, dose, source, bootstrap rule, threshold or human admission.
The full constructed exact and high-noise controls previously qualified the
instrument through a child-local UUID JSON writer. The direct human-facing
entry point still inherited the parent's PID-named writer. This isolated wrapper
exposes that same qualified publisher without editing any admitted source.

DESIGN CHECK: LESSONS sections 3–5 and CONTROLS sections 6–7 reread for this build.
The original exact signed/null construction must pass its existing sensitivity
gate; the original high-noise construction must fail it. Both constructions
retain five doses, 100 worlds per dose and every one of the 2,000 full-frame
building bootstrap draws. Numerical results must be exactly equal between the
qualified baseline and the new entry point. Any difference fails qualification.

The wrapper scopes and restores only the parent's JSON writer. It reuses the
qualified complete-prefix world-bank validator, refusing orphaned, extra,
changed or incomplete sealed records. The original calculation still checks
every binding and computes every array. Native execute and verify call the
actual baseline and successor entry points separately; every array and complete
summary must agree. The independent replay also repeats the original expanded
ordinary-least-squares arithmetic witness. Both handlers keep their first
25-world execution and verification pauses; native continuation copies the
complete authenticated checkpoint to a new attempt and retains its predecessor.
No shortened numerical bank qualifies the wrapper. Unit checks exercise writer
restoration on failure, strict input/grid rejection, orphan/hash refusal, pilot
propagation and a numerically changed but correctly sealed comparison.

Use the already completed constructed sources and their exact source manifests.
Each case runs an execution pilot, an immutable verification-pilot continuation,
and a complete continuation, under the qualified counted native runtime.
Every attempt has at most 600 CPU seconds, 3,600 wall seconds, two numerical
threads, three GiB projected memory and two GiB retained output. Before each
attempt, reconcile all previous SP3.ic source, qualification, preparation,
failed and supervisor charges against its original 5,000-CPU-second ceiling
and SP's 40,000-second family ceiling; the attempt fits only actual remaining
room. Budget 300 CPU seconds for this build's preparation and focused tests.
This is charged work, not an extra allowance or a per-component reservation.

Pre-mortem: publication or checkpoint handling could diverge while headline
numbers still agree; full-array parity, strict bank coverage, immutable
continuations and fresh verification are required. On failure retain the entire
attempt and charges. No reduced grid, changed numerical tolerance or automatic
human admission follows. The smaller route is to preserve the paused full grid
and review its remaining cost; it never drops worlds or buildings for validation.

After both complete native cases pass, human SP3.ic still requires its original
source, support, cost and independent replay admission. Until then this adapter
is built and locally tested, not natively qualified.
