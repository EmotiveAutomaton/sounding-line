## Numerical definition and DESIGN CHECK

LESSONS 3-5 and CONTROLS 1-7 were read during this November 1 D5 build; the
source, support and reader contracts, canonical process identification and
relevant historical-trace theory were reread before this consumer. This is an
implementation and instrument check, not a new research question or novelty claim.

Use the frozen whole-prompt halves and fixed candidate roster. Each eligible
half-A session is a probe; each half-B gallery profile averages its supported
sessions. Keep all candidates and each channel's original component masks.
All channels have their original support checks before numerical scoring.

Character scoring is the existing exact linear-kernel cosine, equal weighting
of character orders two, three and four. Process scoring generalizes the
canonical PMAP squared Euclidean distance to multiple session probes per writer:
concatenate the unchanged selection and timing components, compute gallery
feature means over observed cells, impute partial missing cells to gallery
feature means, and divide by gallery population standard deviations. Drop only
features absent throughout the gallery or with standard deviation at most
1e-12, recording counts. Whole missing components remain unsupported. An
all-constant observed process gallery ties exactly; it is not missing evidence.
All nearest-score ties use the inherited absolute and relative tolerance 1e-12
and receive fractional credit. No random identity tie-breaking is introduced.

Retain literal other-writer context and the separately approved independent
donor rotations. For each target session, context is the component mean of
supported sessions on its prompt in its donor pool. For partially observed
process vectors, use observed-cell means separately per feature. Then form the
same gallery means and use the same probes as the real comparison. Independent
donors come from the complete source census and are disjoint from every scored
writer. Real and context fits each use their own gallery-only scaling; no probe
feature enters scaling or imputation. Literal exclusion remains diagnostic.

The slope uses frozen bin midpoints, writer fixed intercepts and equal total
weight per writer's probes with known model-share bins. Missing bins remain
counted but cannot enter a numerical slope. Writers with no usable bin or no
within-writer variation contribute zero slope information. Resample the entire
fixed probe-writer roster 2,000 times with seed 20261026, holding galleries fixed.
Undefined bootstrap draws remain in the bank and invalidate the interval;
do not redraw. A homogeneous empirical interval is labelled degenerate, never
exact population certainty. No approximate normal probability is produced.

Under a no-identity construction, identical profiles must tie at reciprocal
gallery size. Unique repeated person coordinates must identify the correct
writer, and reversed truth must fail. Independent donor pooling must erase
that direct person-coordinate signature; the literal diagnostic may retain
its exclusion signature and cannot license the correction. Planted declining
and reversed correctness must reverse the slope; clustered resampling must
match an independent explicit-row oracle, retaining undefined draws. Missing
donors, candidates, entire components, malformed identities or nonfinite input
refuse. Exhaustive states are usable, unsupported/undefined, or invalid source.
These are known-answer numerical checks, not empirical scientific size/power
calibration. Full null/population sensitivity admission remains separate.
