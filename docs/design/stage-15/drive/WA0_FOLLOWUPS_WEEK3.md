## Second reader, WA0.h1.p

On the identical support and original role partition, replace squared Euclidean
distance with correlation distance after the same gallery-only feature means,
partial-cell imputation and standard deviations. Center each resulting profile
over its retained coordinates and normalize its length before the dot product.
Features absent or constant throughout the gallery follow the original policy.

A zero-length centered profile carries no shape. Its similarity to every profile
is explicitly zero, including another zero-shape profile. This is a declared
neutral extension, not an estimated Pearson correlation. A wholly constant
gallery therefore ties at exact fractional chance, preserving every writer.
One remaining coordinate likewise supplies no shape. Missing entire profiles
still invalidate fixed support. Ties use the parent's tolerance and fractional
credit; identities never break ties. No readout is selected from its result.
