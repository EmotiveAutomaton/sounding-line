## Frozen target and complete workload

Retain the complete original 110 constructed worlds, their rows, writer and
session assignments, menu features, chronology and original train/calibration
keys. For R-both only, use the exact existing mean of the two swapped split
likelihood-ratio statistics. Relearn half-specific population baselines,
standardization, taste directions, dimension, habit decay and variance fits
in every generated dataset. No old fitted directions or outcome histories
may stand in for a new fit. The optional Gaussian cone reference is not needed
to calculate this statistic; the original R-both value must agree exactly in
constructed validation and against the saved outer-world statistic at admission.

Each outer world requires 199 complete simulated null datasets. Its probability
is (1 + count of null statistics at least as large as observed) / 200. Ties count
against detection; no missing draw is discarded, redrawn or replaced with zero.
Complete outer calibration and original labels/bands remain decisive. A partial
bank supplies no probability, detection rate or revised label.

## Fitted no-taste law

The null is conditional on the fixed original B-pop probabilities and menus.
Fit the existing Laplace random-coefficient logistic objective using an intercept
and a causal exponentially weighted acceptance-history deviation, with no taste
coordinates. Choose decay among 0.5, 0.8 and 0.95 on original chronological
training/calibration by half-Brier loss, breaking exact ties by ascending decay.
Then refit the selected nuisance model on every observed row, retaining a fitted
intercept and habit coefficient for every writer. The full nuisance refit also
covers writers absent from original training. No row is removed for this reason.

Simulate each writer's acceptance probability using their retained conditional
mode coefficients and the current simulated habit state. Distribute remaining
probability among edit/dismiss/ignore in the fixed B-pop proportions. Process
decisions by opening time/key; update histories only when a generated outcome
has resolved. Keep original timestamps and menus. Each simulation uses a fresh
fixed seed and starts from the original whole-writer baseline center.

This is a plug-in conditional parametric bootstrap: fitted nuisance parameters
do not give an exact finite-sample test. Its adequacy must be judged by the full
original outer no-taste and planted-taste calibration. The null fit and its
writer shrinkage are explicit assumptions, not a proof that every human habit
law is covered. No human taste result or downstream route is admitted here.

## DESIGN CHECK and cost preparation

Under null and alternative alike, source roster and menus are identical,
finite four-action probabilities sum to one, and only resolved generated
outcomes affect subsequent histories. Known offsets change acceptance in the
declared direction; zero habit removes dependence on history. Future observed
labels cannot alter generated outcomes under a fixed fitted law and seed.
Known-answer validation checks these conditions, exact R-both parity, complete
199-draw denominators and ties, whole-handler reentry, fresh replay and refusal
of changed outputs and source scope. Numerical nonconvergence is never a null.

The first executable is cost-only: the fixed replicate-zero, strength-0.4 world
from each of the three original plant families; fit its null, refit the observed
R-both pipeline, and refit one independently generated complete null dataset.
All three original corpora remain full-sized. A constructed fixture rehearses
one plant, explicitly without scientific admission. Whole workload is projected
from the slowest measured (null fit + observed fit + 199 generated fits), times
110 worlds, times two independent passes, times 1.5. No parallel-speedup credit.
The cost stage never returns a scientific probability or licenses a smaller bank.
