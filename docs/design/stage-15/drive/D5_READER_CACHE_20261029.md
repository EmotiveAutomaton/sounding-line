## DESIGN CHECK

LESSONS sections 3-5 and CONTROLS sections 1-7 reread for this build. For both
null and alternative, retain every original session, writer and prepared text
length. Character columns are exact Unicode n-grams, without hashing, pruning
or learned vocabulary. Divide each count by its order's available n-gram
opportunities; give orders two, three and four equal weight in the inner
product. This normalizes representation length; it is not a depth measure or
an estimate of cognitive contribution.

Cache the session inner-product matrix separately for the full final document
and all six human prefix lengths. Weighted profile inner products can then be
computed without repeatedly expanding the character vocabulary. This is exact
linear algebra, checked against an independently constructed dense oracle.
Numerical agreement is required to absolute and relative tolerance 1e-12;
the completed execution and fresh verification must reproduce saved arrays
exactly. All process components remain separate, with original missing values.

An unsupported character row has a separate mask. Its storage zeros may never
be interpreted as an observed zero feature or a candidate profile. The reader
primitive refuses missing or zero-norm profiles. Identical valid profiles give
fractional ties; orthogonal profiles identify their matching vector and reject
reversed truth. These are constructed numerical checks, not a scientific
calibration or a claim about the real corpus. Original and independent-donor
context controls, complete writer-label and within-writer reference banks,
and the model-share comparison remain required in the next consumer.

Authenticate the completed support producer and every feature source it binds.
Recheck the session joins, actual producer boundary, tamper refusals, canonical
ordering and immutable handler reentry. No source capsule or completed producer
is edited. Participant text and feature matrices remain in ignored raw storage.
