   inclusion probabilities. Benchmark policy performance on the full retained
   cohort; any weighted population estimator needs a separately validated definition.

   that asking goals is necessary or sufficient. Report it as an event-availability
   planning rule. GEN1's mixed-genre fallback answers a different, explicitly
   exploratory question and cannot inherit the original confirmatory verdict.

   October 25 before 20:00, draining by 22:00. The Sunday 20:00 digest must say
