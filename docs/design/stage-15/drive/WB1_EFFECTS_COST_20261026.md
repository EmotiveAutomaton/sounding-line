## DESIGN CHECK

LESSONS sections 3–5 reread October 26. Null and alternative datasets have the
same operational admission. The sample depends only on fixed session identity,
never the observed effects. All eligible candidates form a workload superset;
there is no candidate discovery verdict in this probe. Changed inputs, missing
sampled units or a failed fresh replay refuse. Known-answer tests cover source
binding, deterministic selection, actual serialized replay, mutation refusal and
cooperative yield. Constructed embeddings do not validate the real encoder.

The projection uses the largest ratio of full-to-sampled sessions, occurrences
and distinct candidates, scales the shuffle count to two hundred, includes two
passes, and applies a threefold safety multiplier. Full source iteration and
full-shape bootstrap overhead are counted separately. It is a conservative
forecast, not a mathematically proven upper bound. Full admission still requires
owner review of tail workload and the complete cumulative budget. The complete
study never silently adopts the pilot population or reduced shuffle count.
