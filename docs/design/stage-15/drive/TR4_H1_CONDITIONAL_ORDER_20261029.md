# TR4.h1: conditional temporal-order reference

Registered before execution on October 29 under the curator's explicit validity,
healing and refinement commission. This is a separate control design at TR4's
rank, not a healing retry of its failed scientific gate or a descendant of TR4.ic.
The original human result, biased-reference flag, complete planted check and
volatility-reliability limits remain intact. No new corpus, GPU, spend or holdout.

**Question.** Does the observed ordering of handling choices improve the adaptive
forecast beyond what the same writers, prompt contexts and choice counts explain?
This is a predictive process question. It cannot date the decisions that formed
a value or establish what a writer currently endorses (G186, G233).

## Design check and reason for correction

DESIGN CHECK: LESSONS sections 3–5 and the TR4.ic correction; CONTROLS sections
5–7; the relevant Triple Inference residue/age rows and afterword and Reader
Heuristics current-trust account were reread for this design. Under the NULL,
session count vectors are jointly invariant to permutations within each writer,
prompt and total-choice-count cell. The two predictors need not have equal mean
loss under that null. Under the ALTERNATIVE, within-cell temporal information
can reward adaptive tracking. Therefore a nonzero reference mean alone is not
an invalidity criterion. The conditional rank tests ordering, and a positive
paired interval separately requires an actual average forecast advantage.

Literature check: Hemerik and Goeman, [Exact testing with random permutations](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6405018/), Theorems 1–2 and their proofs **READ**. Under group invariance, uniform random group draws including the identity give a valid rank test; zero-centered statistics are not required. This is an established method, not a novel statistical test. Adversarial check: Winkler et al., [Permutation inference for the general linear model](https://orbi.uliege.be/bitstream/2268/210172/1/Winkler%20et%20al.%20-%202014%20-%20Permutation%20inference%20for%20the%20general%20linear%20model.pdf), introduction and theory through choice of statistic **READ**. Serial dependence and nuisance effects can violate exchangeability. Conditioning does not prove human exchangeability. The human readout, if admitted, is explicitly conditional on this null and cannot distinguish enduring preference change from other within-prompt temporal dependence. No blanket stationarity or causal claim is licensed.

## Frozen primary comparison

Import the original primary forecast, source preparation, eligibility and
uncertainty helpers without changing their bytes. Retain every source row,
writer split, strict temporal prefix, last-third score mask, four handling
classes, pseudo-count, Gaussian model grid and whole-history comparator.
The statistic is still the test-writer mean of whole-history minus adaptive
log loss. Secondary volatility and its failed reliability control are outside
this correction; preserve their original results explicitly.

Draw 200 independent permutations with replacement from the product of the
full symmetric groups within writer × prompt × total-choice-count cells.
Zero-choice sessions stay fixed. Missingness, sample sizes, prompts, membership
and score masks therefore stay fixed. Refit the complete original primary
forecast sequentially for every draw. Retain all draws, including duplicates
and identity outcomes. Probability is (1 + number at least as large as the
observed statistic) / 201. Use a conservative 1e-12 relative/absolute tolerance
for numerical ties. Do not subtract the reference mean or require it to be zero.
Store the old 0.25-SD diagnostic, but it has no admission role in this new card.
Seeds: reference 20261029, writer bootstrap 20261030, 2,000 paired writer draws.

At least three scoreable test writers must have a nonconstant exchangeable
cell; otherwise status is unsupported. All other originally scoreable writers
remain in the statistic, including fixed ones. Never silently select a smaller
population. Exhaustive statuses: unsupported if support/finite uncertainty
fails; positive conditional temporal-order evidence if the lower paired
interval is above zero and probability < .05; negative forecast advantage if
the upper interval is below zero (descriptive, not a two-sided rank claim);
otherwise not detected. The last state is not evidence of equivalence.
