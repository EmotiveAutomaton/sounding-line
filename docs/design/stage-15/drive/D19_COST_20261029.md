## DESIGN CHECK

LESSONS sections 3–5 and CONTROLS 1–7 read for the current build pass. Relevant
reusable-piece hypotheses and their afterword reread. Factors are mathematical
components of this representation, not identified drives, goals or mental parts.
NULL independent features can still have a low-rank approximation; reconstruction
alone is never evidence of a mechanism. ALTERNATIVE exact nonnegative mixtures
must reconstruct their known matrix; scaling and component permutation are not
treated as different mechanisms. Negative inputs, nonfinite data, changed source,
unconverged fit and missing observations without explicit masks refuse.

Use the fully completed PMAP recovery's authenticated 1,399 session rows and
61 writers. Its source replay and canonical choices remain bound by that
completion. Process features: four handling proportions; content and housekeeping
edits per typed word; requests per typed word; accepted-original deletion share;
log typed/final word counts; and log median within-word/onset intervals. Each
missing denominator/timing cell has a separate flag; zero imputation is a numeric
encoding paired with that flag, not a measured zero. No unsupported writer is
dropped from source construction.

Content features are the canonical accepted-option mean embedding and dismissed-
menu mean embedding, separately. These are suggestion-content choices, not the
writer's final-document content. Retain all 384 coordinates for each, split each
signed coordinate into positive and negative parts. Missing accepted/dismissed
means use zero with separate flags. This representation choice is an explicit
exploratory operationalization. It cannot license all possible content recipes.

Within each block, divide each column by its full-source root mean square (unit
scale for an all-zero column), then divide by square root of block width. This
transductive, outcome-free normalization gives process and content blocks equal
mean squared magnitude. Preserve the frozen transform across cost bootstraps.
No writer identity, prompt identity or score enters fitting. These identities
remain only for group sampling and future reliability tests.

Fit sklearn NMF with coordinate descent, Frobenius loss, nndsvda initialization,
seed 20261026, max_iter 1000 and tol 0.0001; zero regularization and no shuffling.
Convergence warnings invalidate the fit. Every rank 5–15 runs on the full matrix
and on one whole-writer bootstrap sample. Fresh native verification reconstructs
the features and refits every matrix, comparing the full saved factors and fit
receipts. Measure complete per-rank times and bytes, not just one solver step.

For accounting only, project 2,000 whole-writer stability resamples and 1,000
within-prompt writer-label reliability references across the entire rank grid,
both native passes, plus 120 CPU seconds for reporting, at a factor of three.
This is a conservative workload envelope. The stability matching, writer-loading
reliability, context controls and scientific null calibration are not implemented
or admitted by this probe. No component or partial fit is publicly interpreted.
The initial fixed cumulative cap is 3,600 CPU seconds including validation,
tests, preparation and replay. Full dispatch needs separate measured admission.
No GPU, paid work, data intake, external transfer or delegation.
