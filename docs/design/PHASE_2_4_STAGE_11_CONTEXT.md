## What the walkthrough changes

The following are analyst paraphrases of the walkthrough supplied in this thread and filed September 18; its recording date was not separately supplied:

- Human decisions embodied in this work are the main reason the curator would investigate it. Elapsed time is a cue; accumulated expertise can make a brief selection consequential. An idea first proposed by a model can still undergo substantial human recognition, development and integration.
- Delegation may remove layers of the human's practiced decision structure. The size and shape of that loss are hypotheses, not an exponential law already measured.
- Selecting a work, reviewing a passage, endorsing its content and forwarding it to another person are distinct events. The video example describes attribution to a sender of decisions the sender may not have made. Do not infer comprehensive review from a forwarding act.
- Desired display: passage-linked production hypotheses, proximal-goal hypotheses and warranted maker implications. Uncertainty is central to its usefulness.

Do not turn this into a generic AI detector, an effort score, a detector of moral worth, or a count of mental decisions. The present source records selection and edits; it does not record everything someone noticed, understood, endorsed or thought.

## Scope: one scientific comparison and one display

### 1. Recover a completed writing episode's handling

Use the already prepared CoAuthor source and its raw local logs. The source documents human–GPT-3 writing at the keystroke level; offered suggestions, selections and edits are available for replay. Its research-use material stays in the existing private analysis paths. [CoAuthor source description](https://coauthor.stanford.edu/).

**Episode:** one displayed suggestion menu through the instant immediately before the next new menu, or through session end. A reopen belongs to the same episode. The output artifact is the document at that cutoff. Call it an *episode-end artifact*, not the final essay unless it actually is session end.

The four scored outcomes retain the reviewed source semantics:

1. A verified suggestion insertion remained unedited through the cutoff.
2. A verified suggestion insertion was edited before the cutoff.
3. The menu was explicitly dismissed without a verified selection.
4. The menu was left without a recorded selection or explicit dismissal.

These outcomes describe recorded handling. An unedited insertion does not establish unexamined acceptance; an edited insertion does not establish deep review. Dismissal and ignoring may be indistinguishable from the artifact. That ambiguity belongs in the probability forecast and in the display.

**Two evidence views, declared before any scientific calls:**

- **Resulting artifact:** episode-end document, permitted task brief, and the common fact that a suggestion menu had been available. No menu contents, cursor position derived from the answer, selected-index field, handling label, author identity or process transcript. This is artifact plus minimal workflow context, not an unrestricted test of whether any AI was involved.
- **Artifact with available alternatives:** exactly the same evidence plus the actual pre-menu document and the displayed suggestion menu. Still withhold selection, edits-as-events and handling labels. This tests historical reconstruction from a before/after artifact and available alternatives. It is an information intervention, not a same-evidence method comparison against the first view.

The target is now historical. An episode-end artifact would leak the answer in Stage 10's next-action task; it is legitimate evidence for this newly declared reconstruction task. Implement a separate adapter and namespace, never weaken the old prospective guard.

**Cohort:** first target 24 evaluation episodes; predeclare up to 24 more as an extension, with at most three episodes per writer across both tranches. Select by stable hash within the existing eligible evaluation source partition, round-robin across writers; retain source and context exclusions and writer, prompt and duplicate-text separation. A writer and a connected dependency component are different units. Prefer both writing domains where the eligible pool allows it. Do not select on reader success or vividness. Freeze the actual available cohort before scientific calls, even if it is smaller. Fourteen eligible writers, for example, permit at most 42 episodes under this cap; do not change the split or add repetitions to reach 48. Extension admission depends on available independent coverage and runtime, not on a positive first-tranche result.

Use existing eligible training data to fit the cheap rivals; cap the new retrospective training projection at 96 episodes with the same per-writer balancing rule. Use at most eight development episodes for schema/cost checks. The historically exposed corpus is descriptive even with a new local freeze. A single development writer cannot validate calibration or a routing policy.

## Validity checks that matter

Before the first scientific model block, require a small executable rehearsal from source to final report. Known-answer fixtures must cover:

- UTF-16 indexing, surrogate pairs, deletion overlapping an accepted insertion, a shifted span, reopening, complete deletion, duplicate offered texts and malformed source events.
- The episode cutoff: the next menu's evidence and all later text stay outside the earlier artifact; final-session closure is correct. The original replay has no independent final-document reference, so fixture equality and source consistency remain distinct evidence.
- Byte-identical public evidence between method arms; only the declared additional documents differ between evidence tiers. Opaque IDs cannot encode the answer.
- A source with two distinct handling histories producing the same artifact. Artifact-only readers receive identical bytes and cannot be rewarded for guessing the private history with certainty. An explicit later record can resolve that ambiguity.
- Missing span support, unsupported goal statements and a sharing-only act. No code maps these automatically to “little human contribution,” “endorsed” or “unreviewed.”
- Raw-response reparse, exact score reconstruction and resume without additional calls. Hash agreement alone does not establish semantic replay.

Target these checks; do not reopen every historical test bank. Read `docs/method/LESSONS.md` §§2–5 and the matching controls when building the new adapter. Include the required DESIGN CHECK block. Verify the actual literal command and actual output namespace, not only helper functions.

## Scores and what may be claimed

Primary descriptive comparison: contribution-account versus direct reader, **separately within each evidence view**, using half multiclass Brier loss (half the sum of squared probability errors; lower is better). Also report maximum-probability action accuracy, negative log score, invalid rate and actual cost. Cheap controls appear in the same complete table. These targets reuse handling labels but are not numerically interchangeable with Stage 10's prospective task.

Weight writers equally after averaging their retained episodes. Preserve prompt/session/duplicate dependencies. Report connected component counts; if there is one component, do not manufacture a population confidence interval by resampling individual events. Report the observed paired effects and per-writer/prompt spread. No fresh human confirmation or significance-based expansion is commissioned.

Present all-attempt scores, including worst-loss treatment of invalid forecasts under the frozen rule, alongside valid-only diagnostics. At the deadline, identify the complete registered tranches and any partial remainder separately. Never pool whichever arms happened to finish or hide source exclusions.

Freeze the probability parser during the rehearsal: four finite nonnegative probabilities, sum within a declared tolerance of one, with any permitted rounding normalization retained in the trace. Otherwise mark the forecast invalid. An invalid forecast receives half-Brier loss 1 and accuracy 0 in the all-attempt analysis. Report infinite log loss explicitly when the realized outcome has zero probability; do not silently smooth a prior or drop those cases after seeing results. Any diagnostic clipping must be declared and reported separately.

For each proposed event, distinguish exact support, contradiction and unresolved history. A mechanically valid quote is not semantic proof. Score only relations the logs actually observe; the rest are labelled hypotheses. Do not grade a proposed proximal goal or enduring value against an analyst's plausible story. A same-evidence method gain would establish a useful representation in this task, not historical uniqueness or human-level mind reconstruction.

Keep the two outputs separate in the report: the four-way handling score measures a coarse historical event; the contribution map proposes a richer event network. The score does not validate every link in that network. For the six displayed cases, audit each observable proposed operation against the retained log and label unsupported or contradicted links. This case audit is descriptive. It does not measure a writer's total decisions, embodied expertise, contribution percentage, or the calibration of arbitrary narrative claims.

Clicking a passage should show three panels:

1. **Possible creation history:** actor and operation, alternatives and dependencies, supporting text, the elicited four-way handling probabilities, competing history hypotheses, and what additional evidence could distinguish them. Do not invent numerical probabilities for unscored narrative events merely to fill the display.
2. **Possible immediate purpose:** local goal hypotheses and supporting details. Display “not established by these records” where appropriate.
3. **What this suggests about the person:** narrowly supported choices or demonstrated capacities; separate unresolved hypotheses about habits or values. No personal diagnosis, ungrounded biography or inferred review from mere selection.

If transparency encodes confidence, label that explicitly; a faint passage means uncertainty, not absence of human contribution. Overlapping human/model operations may both be shown. Keep uncalibrated model probabilities visibly distinct from measured calibration. Observed logs can prove a selection or edit; they generally cannot prove attentive review or total mental effort. ## Research context and Sunday decision

Attention is a plausible control mechanism, not an established identity for trust. Initial accuracy judgments protected against a repetition effect when relevant knowledge was available; knowledge alone did not reliably protect in another study. These motivate separate uptake measures, without establishing a mechanism of artistic appreciation. [Brashier, Eliseev & Marsh, 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2019.104054); [Fazio et al., 2015](https://doi.org/10.1037/xge0000098).

Our own T01/T02/T03 history is especially relevant: learnability, truth and useful uptake dissociate; reconstruction and technique instruction have sometimes added narrative or blanket distrust without improving discrimination. Keep those failures visible. This weekend's contribution study does not claim to measure ideological change. The companion Ghost handoff tests a bounded procedural-learning mechanism.

By Sunday deliver: one short report, the full complete-cell comparison table, source/attempt/cost dispositions, the six-case local viewer, a replay command and the resource-index update. Use normal internal FINDINGS/theory/TODO write-through for results, scoped to the new evidence; do not rewrite curator quotations or install the tentative attention identity as fact. Public pushes may contain only the project's allowed code and results.

The report should let the curator discuss actual cases: **Did the reader recover a decision worth attending to? Did it confuse selection with review? Which further piece of context would change the reading?** Offer at most two new high-level questions, chosen from results, with answer banks separate from the initial questions.
