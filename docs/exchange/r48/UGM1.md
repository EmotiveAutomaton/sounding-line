# UGM1 · Do unexpected offers change responders’ feelings?

**Registered prediction.** The offer gap adds information about valence, and surprises of either sign raise arousal.

**Source and sample.** Class P. Heffner, Son and FeldmanHall, Study 1, [source study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-021-01213-6). R48 permits publication of the aggregate comparisons from this publicly obtainable source. Size counts contributing responders with within-person variation. Observations count fitted trials.

**Registered quantities.** Valence regressed on offer, difference from expectation and change from the preceding offer. Arousal fits use surprise magnitude separately by sign. Intervals resample whole players.

**Limit.** Within-player associations cannot establish which feature of an offer caused a feeling.
