# LKG2 · Do liking errors vary with personality and perceived standing?

**Registered prediction.** Lower Extraversion and stability predict a wider liking gap. The analyst expected weak agreement between the liking and standing gaps.

**Source and sample.** Class S. BetterUp Labs, BetterUp, Inc., CANDOR Tabular v1.1. License v2.1 permits non-identifying aggregate statistics. Reece et al. (2023), Science Advances 9, eadf3197. [Tabular release](https://huggingface.co/datasets/betterup/candor-tabular). The table identifies contributing people or paired observations for each estimate.

**Registered quantities.** For the released comparison, subtract the partner’s rating of the speaker’s standing from the speaker’s estimate of that rating. Resample whole speakers.

| Quantity | Estimate [interval] | Size and unit | Observations | Reading |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| Estimated minus partner-rated standing, rungs | -0.166355 [-0.251125, -0.0785919] | 1415 people | — | Descriptive |

**Verdict — descriptive.** Speakers underestimated the standing their partners assigned them on average. The trait prediction and correlation between gaps are undecided.

**Limit.** People may use the response scale similarly when rating themselves and estimating a partner’s view. A partner can recur across speaker draws.

**Withholding and release condition.** Only the pooled standing gap returned. The finer trait, stability and cross-gap comparisons need releasable eligible populations.
