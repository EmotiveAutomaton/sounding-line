# GAT2 · Does a partner’s advantage over the rater predict a yes?

**Registered prediction.** The floored mean intelligence and ambition advantage has a positive coefficient after adjustment for partner rating and its square.

**Source and sample.** Class P. Columbia Speed Dating Experiment. No explicit distribution licence was stated. R48 permits publication of the aggregate comparisons from this publicly obtainable source. Size counts raters. Observations count encounters.
