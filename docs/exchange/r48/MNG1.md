# MNG1 · Does caregiving lead daily activities in meaning relative to happiness?

**Registered prediction.** Child and adult care should lead, above paid work and housework. Games, sport and socializing should sit at the other end.

**Source and sample.** Class P. US Bureau of Labor Statistics, ATUS Well-Being Module and Basic Activity/Who files, public-domain tables. [BLS module](https://www.bls.gov/tus/dictionaries.htm). The table identifies contributing people or paired observations for each estimate.

**Registered quantities.** Subtract each respondent’s average rating on each scale, then compare the meaning and happiness departures. Weight the four years equally and resample whole people within year. Category intervals describe separate means.

**Verdict — mixed.** Both care groups were above housework in point estimates, but paid work was above both. Volunteering had the largest activity estimate. Television had the lowest. Pairwise ranking intervals were not registered.

**Limit.** One word measures meaning. People select their activities and typically rate only three episodes. Early survey years had an activity-selection error, and the last wave omitted January and February.

**Withholding and release condition.** All registered activity and company means are reported.
