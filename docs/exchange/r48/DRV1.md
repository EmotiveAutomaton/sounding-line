**Verdict — undecided.** The full and short-gap readings establish no positive contrast. Anxiety was flagged in every report in both diaries. In 0056 the sadness indicator was also constant, so its exact zero cannot test the mechanism.

**Limit.** A second difference of noisy standing ratings is noisy. Only two diaries were available and missed prompts may be selective.

**Withholding and release condition.** All registered fits and clock checks are reported. A varying anxiety indicator is needed to distinguish the channels.
