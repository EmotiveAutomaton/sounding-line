# LGH7 · Whose laughter gets joined more often in meetings?

**Registered prediction.** Other speakers join a higher-ranking speaker’s laugh more often.

**Source and sample.** Class P. AMI Meeting Corpus, Carletta et al. (2005), including the OpenSLR SLR16 supplement under its modified CC BY-NC-SA terms. R48 permits publication of the aggregate comparisons from this publicly obtainable source. Meetings are the analysis units. The replay did not tabulate distinct people. ID denotes industrial designer, ME marketing expert, PM project manager and UI user-interface designer.

**Verdict — undecided.** The AMI contrast includes zero and overlaps the permutation reference. ICSI had no documented rank.

**Limit.** Assigned meeting roles are an imperfect measure of rank. Leaders may speak more often.

**Withholding and release condition.** The ICSI comparison needs a documented role binding. All AMI role and joining rates are reported.
