## 2. Finite experiment tree and allocation

The table defines the spending order. Amounts are campaign envelopes, including overhead attributable to that part; they are not minimum expenditures. Targets are starting roster sizes, reduced using the discarded pilot's measured cost before scientific outcomes are opened.

| Node | Question and work | Target | Envelope |
|---|---|---|---:|
| P | Can the literal cloud package run, resume and return complete evidence? | Small discarded two-model pilot after local checks | $3 |
| A | Does the larger reader benefit more from reconstruction than from direct prediction or matched deliberation? | 64 human opportunities plus up to 12 eligible Ghost public tasks; two models, three methods | $17 |
| B | Does the correct person's history matter beyond an equal-length other-person history and no history? | Up to 24 eligible human opportunities; two models, direct and executable readers | $7 |
| C | Do reusable procedures help beyond concrete examples, and do their names matter? | Up to 24 human and 6 Ghost tasks; two models, three memory methods | $9 |
| D | Do the principal patterns persist across additional sources or public task conditions? | Next unused source-balanced blocks from the same reviewed pools | $4 |
| Reserve | Interrupted work, verified operational repair, artifact recovery, storage/transfer and billing uncertainty | No new model family, training project or corpus adapter | $10 |
| **Total** | **All cloud expenditure attributable to this campaign** | **Initial P+A ≤ $20; ordinary planned work ≤ $40** | **$50** |

P failure stops only the unusable model/source path. After one scoped repair, persistent failure closes that path with its evidence and cost. If no useful paired comparison remains, close the burst rather than spend the full allowance on repetitions of failed apparatus.

After A, run B and C when their required sources and native methods are ready, irrespective of A's effect direction. D prioritizes new writers, prompts and structural cases. A missing branch's unused allowance may move to another **already specified** branch, keeping the initial $20 and total $50 limits. The reserve does not become an automatic extra science allocation.

**Sizing:** A's full target is 456 forecasts and at most 760 model calls: per task and model, direct uses one call, deliberation two, and executable reconstruction at most two. B adds at most 192 forecasts / 288 calls when its true-history counterparts are already in A. C adds at most 180 forecasts / 300 calls. These are accounting identities, not throughput promises.

Choose the largest affordable source-balanced roster after P, allowing 25% over measured execution demand plus observed startup and result-flush time. Build each complete task comparison before expanding the roster. A smaller completed tree is preferable to many half-produced cells.

Freeze an ordered series of small comparison blocks, each within one source family, target and evidence condition: normally eight human opportunities or up to four Ghost public tasks, with every required model/method counterpart. Finish the current block before admitting the next. Completed blocks may be reported together with source-balanced weighting and the full planned/attempted/unstarted inventory. A budget-cut block remains incomplete. Do not silently present the completed prefix as the whole intended population; slower cases may be systematically different.

There is no five-day occupancy requirement. Aim for a few focused setup hours, substantially less than one working day, and roughly one day of cloud execution plus analysis. If setup approaches a day, reduce scope to ready branches and issue the concrete remaining blocker. Do not restart the old Stage 9 setup program or manufacture new infrastructure obligations to fill time.

## 3. Scientific contract

### A. Reader capability and useful reconstruction

Use the existing Qwen3.5 9B reader and the quantized Qwen3.5 27B reader. Run all compared forecasts in the cloud under the same declared environment. Existing local results provide context and implementation checks; they are not substituted for one side of the cloud comparison.

A uses **one process-record task per human opportunity**, containing the current draft, displayed suggestion menu and permitted earlier handling. The 64-opportunity target does not mean both CoAuthor views. The Ghost target counts public task records across admitted families/views, not twelve cases multiplied by every query. B supplies the declared human artifact-only comparison.

Methods:

- **R0:** direct prediction.
- **R2:** matched deliberation, using the existing two-call allowance.
- **R3:** proposed executable process/handling hypotheses, using the existing native executor and two proposal/refinement rounds.

Retain R0 beside every R3–R2 contrast: beating deliberation while losing to direct prediction is a materially different outcome. Both models use the same public task, options, evidence, permitted training pool, context limit within a comparison, and maximum 768 generated tokens across a complete route. A route may use less. Count actual input/output tokens, executor work and elapsed time; equal output allowances do not imply equal compute.

The principal comparison is the **difference in reconstruction benefit between readers**. With L denoting source-balanced system half-Brier loss:

`interaction = [L(27B,R2) - L(27B,R3)] - [L(9B,R2) - L(9B,R3)]`

Positive favors a larger reconstruction benefit for 27B. Report both bracketed contrasts, R3 versus R0 for each reader, and the direct 27B-versus-9B contrast. Compute uncertainty from the same paired source components, never by subtracting unrelated interval endpoints.

This is a comparison between two reader packages. Parameter count is not isolated from training and quantization. It tests neither model-family similarity as such nor a human empathy mechanism.

Reuse the four current CoAuthor controls: training class prior, surface-feature logistic prediction, always-ignore and previous handling. Recompute only where the cloud cohort/evidence differs. Use already supported source-specific cheap controls for Ghost; do not invent a new exact Bayesian oracle merely for this burst.

### B. Specificity of personal history

Use human process-record tasks from A. Keep draft, suggestion menu, question, target outcome and option order fixed. Compare:

1. correct predecision history;
2. an actual other writer's history of exactly the same length;
3. the artifact-only view, without history.

Use R0 and **R3**, fixed in advance for both models. This avoids selecting a method on the single-writer development set. The current `human_history.py` already supports the executable-rival path; reuse its validated donor logic. Prefer a changed history under its deterministic identity-based rule. Retain unmatched opportunities and their reasons; do not pad or truncate histories to manufacture matches.

Select donors from the permitted public records of the same evaluation allocation. Never source an outcome after the donor's cutoff or transmit target evaluator labels. Record donor dependencies, including any repeated donor. Fit no model or rule to evaluation outcomes.

**Implementation trap:** `comparison.paired()` correctly rejects changed public evidence. Do not weaken it globally. Add a narrow intervention comparison that joins on the underlying source event, verifies the declared history-only delta and unchanged remaining fields, then applies the same paired loss/grouping logic. Treat the artifact-only contrast as a separately declared evidence-view change.

Report the correct-versus-other-history benefit, correct-versus-no-history benefit, both readers' calibration, the matched population's class composition, and the previous-handling control on the corresponding evidence. A shift alone is sensitivity; improved held-out prediction is useful specificity. This manipulates the reader's evidence, not the human maker's historical process.

### C. Concrete examples, procedures and names

Use the existing R1 storage-matched retrieval, R4 opaque procedure names and R4 grounded procedure names on overlapping tasks from A. Compare all three under identical permitted training data and the same total representation-storage allowance. Names count toward storage. Definitions and retained concrete episodes remain identical between the naming conditions; names must come from definitions/training uses, never private maker labels.

Report grounded R4 versus R1, opaque R4 versus R1, grounded versus opaque R4, and R4 versus the same task's R3/R0. Keep the existing human rule language, lapse assumption and Ghost native learner fixed. Empty libraries and invalid proposals are outcomes. This is the already implemented LILO-inspired adaptation, not a reproduction of LILO's benchmark.

A naming effect would identify a useful access/representation issue. It would not establish that the reader recovered the maker's historical internal procedure.

### D. Broader coverage and bounded transfer

Expand A first by taking the next source-balanced blocks in a frozen, identity-based order. Where B/C are ready and inexpensive, extend their matched comparisons too. Prioritize underrepresented writers, prompts, source cases, public task families and evidence conditions. Do not select examples because their target labels or pilot scientific scores look favorable.

A second human corpus is eligible only if its Stage 10 adapter, chronology and literal reader path are already validated at handoff. Otherwise mark it deferred. No new scraping, author contact, repository reproduction or corpus-adapter project belongs on this burst's critical path.

Use existing reviewed changed-condition Ghost tasks if available, with the corresponding recorded or independently executable changed-world outcome. Changing a prompt's stated constraint while keeping an incompatible original answer is not a counterfactual test. Never access V17's private confirmation namespaces or interrupt its active campaign to obtain examples.

Do not fit a new adaptive-effort router in this package. The current single-writer development support cannot justify it. Produce a source-support census for a later routing study; more calls on that one writer do not cure the deficiency.

If the cloud pool contains too little independent support for a broad comparison, report the available support and reduce the scope; do not construct a population claim from repetitions.

Keep previously discarded pilot components excluded. For cloud transport rehearsal, reuse discarded source examples under a new cloud-pilot namespace; they remain excluded from science.

All current exposed sources remain **descriptive evaluation**. A new model, new task identifier, new random seed or new draw from a small existing support does not create untouched confirmation. Freeze each scientific roster and its complete method matrix before producing it. Use the same source selection for both models. Do not select the 27B sample from 9B failures.

Keep human handling, Ghost opportunity prediction and Ghost procedure-reading results separate, including their evidence views. A human four-way handling outcome is recorded behavior in mixed human/model writing; it is not a known primary goal. Narrative goal conjectures remain unscored where historical ground truth is absent.

Reuse `docs/design/STAGE10_COMPARISON_FREEZE.md` and `runners/stage10/comparison.py`:

- Main finite end-to-end measure: half multiclass Brier loss, with invalid attempted forecasts assigned system loss one. Also show valid-only scores.
- Preserve zero true-outcome probability as infinite log loss. Report finite-pair log differences with their denominator; do not smooth silently or subtract infinities.
- Separate the model's generated choice, executed probability mixture and elicited probability forecast. Show class composition, calibration, calls, tokens and costs.
- Weight writers/source cases equally. Repeated events, evidence views and model calls do not create makers. Link shared prompts, constructors and history donors in the uncertainty graph.
- With fewer than ten dependency components, show individual component contrasts and leave-one-component-out ranges descriptively. Otherwise reuse the fixed-seed component bootstrap. No confirmatory p-values, universal-null conclusions or equivalence claims.
- A scientific null, harmful extra effort and an invalid instrument are distinct dispositions. A complete cell includes recorded invalid attempts; an unattempted budget-cut tail is incomplete and is not scored as if finished.

For the history intervention, build the common dependency graph from both sides, including donor links, before estimating paired uncertainty. The shared scorer's ordinary same-evidence checks remain intact.

A publishable interpretation requires a whole frozen comparison and its named controls. Produce one final curator packet. Operational monitoring may inspect completion, validity and spend, but must not choose expansions by peeking at incomplete scientific scores.

## 5. Model and cloud configuration

**Default hardware:** one L40S GPU in a Modal Function, one active scientific container at a time. Start with two physical CPU cores and 32GiB host RAM, with explicit request/limit settings. Run one model at a time and bound model concurrency. Do not reserve a local GPU or reuse the local repository's GPU lock from a cloud worker.

**9B:** retain `qwen3.5:9b`, full digest
`6488c96fa5faab64bb65cbd30d4289e20e6130ef535a93ef9a49f42eda893ea7`.

**27B:** use `qwen3.5:27b`, Q4_K_M. The inspected listing reports a 17GB package and digest prefix `7653528ba5cb`; resolve and record the full digest before the pilot. A prefix is not an executable pin. Check measured memory use at the longest permitted request; package size alone does not establish peak VRAM. [Official model listing](https://ollama.com/library/qwen3.5:27b).

Preserve the current `/api/chat` protocol, JSON schema, temperature zero, seed 1001 and `think: false`. Freeze the server version, full model identities, quantization, request builder, options and environment. Use a common **16,384-token context ceiling** for both models throughout A–C, while preserving each route's existing evidence and representation-storage allowance. A larger ceiling does not add evidence. Forward this profile through every nested call and retain the conservative no-truncation check. Unexpected thinking output must be recorded and investigated as a protocol difference, not labelled zero reasoning.

The existing client hardcodes its model identity. Add an explicit reader profile and bind it into requests, outputs and resume identity, with the current 9B profile remaining the default. Use a cloud protocol/version namespace so no prior attempt is reconstructed under changed defaults. Do not spoof the digest or monkeypatch global model constants to pass an old guard.

Start the pinned Ollama server and existing batch reader inside the same bounded Function. Cache weights and prepare input archives without occupying a GPU where possible, recording any associated cloud cost. Load models in blocks to amortize startup while preserving the frozen complete comparison roster. Use existing native Ghost code through its reviewed public export; do not substitute a newly invented simulator.

L40S is the only hardware profile commissioned here. If it is unavailable, the local program can continue. An A100/H100 or different serving stack is a separately specified option if it materially improves cost or solves a demonstrated memory problem, not an automatic setup escalation. Modal recommends L40S as an inference starting point; its ordinary A100 selector guarantees only 40GB, so any future 80GB proposal must name that version explicitly. [GPU guidance](https://modal.com/docs/guide/gpu).

The native controller cancels an expired Function even if it is still starting. A reset per-attempt timeout is not a campaign budget. ## 7. Execution, closeout and decision product

At handoff, check the current head once and record the implementation commit plus exact source/model/configuration hashes. Honor the requested stable execution point. Do not restart a healthy local run to make the report look current.

Preserve this package's declared comparisons; do not select cloud evaluation cases from favorable or unfavorable local outcomes. A newly demonstrated source or scoring defect delays only its affected branch.

The deterministic controller continues through the specified branches within their budgets. A healthy run ends the coding agent's turn. Native supervision handles completion, failure, disappearance, a budget intervention and the one P-to-science decision. No periodic LLM liveness checks, recursive queue wakes or idle model turns.

Write terminal dispositions for complete, incomplete, blocked and retired branches, each with its cost and reason. Verify all returned records once, score whole cells locally with evaluator access isolated, and complete the usual FINDINGS/theory-status/TODO write-through. Theory updates remain limited to appropriate existing rows and afterwords; this burst does not authorize rewriting the curator's theory or adding quotations.

The final report must answer:

1. Did 27B improve direct prediction, reconstruction's incremental benefit, both, or neither?
2. Did correct personal history help beyond other-writer history, no history and cheap sequential persistence?
3. Did learned procedures help beyond stored examples; did grounded naming change access to them?
4. Which advantages, reversals or failures persisted across the available source groups and conditions?
5. Which next experiment earns a larger allocation, and what exact remaining blocker prevents the others?

Return the full comparison/cost tables and a small fixed selection of worked examples from observed advantage, reversal and failure categories. Choose examples by a predeclared stable hash within each category after scoring; say when a category is absent. Explain the artifact, evidence, prediction and actual outcome before naming the method identifier.

Keep two judgments separate: **pursuit value**—what newly seems buildable or worth exploring—and **warrant**—what these model readers on these recorded/constructed tasks establish. Useful reconstruction can be historically wrong; a working procedure is not a recovered human value system. A valid negative result describes this method, reader and evidence combination. It does not settle the general possibility of appreciation or intent inference.

The final cost account includes setup, science, failures/recovery, artifact handling, unused allowance, credits and unresolved billing. Close when the finite useful work is complete or the budget is reached; do not spend the remainder merely because it was approved.
