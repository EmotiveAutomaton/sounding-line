## What the stage establishes

**Question.** Does earlier maker-specific evidence improve prediction of later
recorded choices beyond strong population, donor and no-history controls?

**Method.** Use replayed human co-writing process records, strict earlier-history
cutoffs, separate training/calibration/evaluation, and matched complete targets.
Compare text adapters, lexical/semantic choice models and a cloud reader with
their fixed rivals. Keep four-way suggestion handling separate from predicting
which offered continuation was selected conditional on uptake. Report writer
uncertainty and crossed writer/prompt sensitivity; retain failures, invalids,
historical exposure, sparse text and shared-evidence dependence.

**Found.** Stage 14 does not establish a useful maker-specific advantage against
its strong controls. This is a limit of these tested constructions, not a finding
that personal information is absent. The useful result is a verified process
ledger, bounded extraction and missingness instruments, matched baseline controls,
and a much clearer separation between observed choices and inferred preferences.

## Validity boundaries

The source census contains 16,959 usable opportunities from 61 writers. It is
historically exposed; absent exposure witnesses do not certify freshness.
The adapter studies cover overlapping writers and fixed prompts. Repeated seeds,
epochs, ranks and token caps are not independent human replications. Higher token
caps often add no text for sparse writers. Full final text mixes human and model
contributions; accepted suggestions are selected model prose. The target records
observed selections, not private motives, stable values or endorsement.

The cloud main contains 234 targets from 31 writers, excluding three pilot
writers. Its four arms make 936 calls. Ten invalid replies remain in system loss;
none is clipped or reasked. Shared histories/donors limit writer-independence
assumptions, and crossed prompt resampling does not remove that dependence.
Original character-matched donors sometimes miss their nominal band; the fixed
full-history and length-matched sensitivities remain part of the report.

SM4's synthetic handling readout fails semantic admission, so human prototype
training was not run. The original API profile pilot fails with two output-limit
stops; its reader pilot/main and five Opus primary comparisons are unrun. Those
are missing comparisons, not negative results. Stage 15 R3 is a separately
authorized repair using profiles as features, not a continuation of the old judge role.
