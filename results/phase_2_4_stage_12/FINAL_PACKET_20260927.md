1. **What can the reader now tell us about production and purpose?** The completed
   work supports bounded recovery of witnessed operations and annotated request/edit
   relations, with explicit location and refusal controls. It does not establish a
   reliable account of private purpose from an endpoint. The broad capable-reader
   human-history main has no complete comparison.
2. **What changes with evidence, capacity, state or mechanism?** Exact witness access
   and correct answer assistance change observable performance; incorrect assistance
   and saved false-frame replies can cause harm. Raw history can beat compressed
   account access. The intended broad capacity and persistent neural comparisons
   remain incomplete or unadmitted, so those interventions are not interchangeable.
3. **What is the strongest simplest method, and where does it fail?** Exact extraction
   leads when the witness contains the answer; cheap priors and alignment are strong
   on the exposed human records. Their advantage is target and access dependent.
   They do not recover missing private goals or uniquely identify identical endpoints.
   No universal method ranking is supported.
4. **What survives new support?** The reserved revision roster supports the declared
   saved false-frame penalty within the same law and reader. It is fresh-support
   replication of a scoped comparison, not a new mechanism, model family or human
   validation. Other findings retain their descriptive, exposed or constructed scope.
5. **Are throughput and exposure resolved?** The submitted executor drained before
   the requested stop time, but the missed narrow-history handoff and failed families
   left the broader program incomplete. Records and costs reconcile. Historical human
   source exposure is a limitation, not something a successful replay can erase.
6. **What exact distinction comes next?** Whether durable process information improves
   useful, correctly located recovery beyond the same raw records and cheap controls
   at matched coverage. This is an unresolved scientific distinction, not a new
   commission or permission to resume Stage 12.
