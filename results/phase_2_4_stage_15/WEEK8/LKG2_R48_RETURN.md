# Do liking errors vary with personality and perceived standing?

Speakers underestimated the standing their partners assigned them on average. The trait prediction and correlation between gaps are undecided. Estimated minus partner-rated standing, rungs = -0.166355 [-0.251125, -0.0785919] (1415 people).

For the released comparison, subtract the partner’s rating of the speaker’s standing from the speaker’s estimate of that rating. Resample whole speakers.

People may use the response scale similarly when rating themselves and estimating a partner’s view. A partner can recur across speaker draws.

Only the pooled standing gap returned. The finer trait, stability and cross-gap comparisons need releasable eligible populations.
