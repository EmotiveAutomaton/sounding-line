# Who joins a meeting leader’s laugh?

The study asked whether a colleague was more likely to join the designated leader’s laugh. The 75 selected ICSI meeting editions have no documented rank or role for this contrast. That arm is void. AMI supplies the project manager and three other assigned roles. Its source-specific estimate, interval, random-label reference, role-pair rates and exclusions are filed internally.

Two independent processes reproduced the same aggregate file byte for byte. The public receipt authenticates the restricted report. The original sound was not needed for this study. An assigned meeting role is an imperfect measure of rank. Leaders may speak more often.
