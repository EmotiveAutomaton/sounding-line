# Does caregiving lead daily activities in meaning relative to happiness?

Both care groups were above housework in point estimates, but paid work was above both. Volunteering had the largest activity estimate. Television had the lowest. Pairwise ranking intervals were not registered. child care = 0.241778 [0.195043, 0.290355] (5098 people).

Subtract each respondent’s average rating on each scale, then compare the meaning and happiness departures. Weight the four years equally and resample whole people within year. Category intervals describe separate means.

One word measures meaning. People select their activities and typically rate only three episodes. Early survey years had an activity-selection error, and the last wave omitted January and February.
