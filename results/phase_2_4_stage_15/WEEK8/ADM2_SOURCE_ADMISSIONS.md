# Three original releases admitted to the source catalogue

The archive census covers 179 registered entries and 13 additional physical holdings, aliases and fixtures. We located earlier study or source-check records for 57 entries. The other 135 appear first in the [holdings inventory](ADM2_ARCHIVE_INVENTORY.md).

The following table records what the two original-source checks established and the scope of each admission.

| Original release | Verified structure | Reuse and scope |
|---|---|---|
| World emBODY, Volynets and colleagues | 3,954 body-map files. The inspected raster has 522 by 171 pixels and 14 planes. The metadata have named timing and pixel-count fields for all 14 feeling names. | CC BY 4.0. BMP1 independently binds names to planes and the signed raster before aggregating. |
| Continuously Annotated Signals of Emotion (CASE), Sharma and colleagues | Thirty participants have matched annotation and signal files. Valence and arousal use a 20 Hz annotation clock. Eight physiological channels use a 1,000 Hz clock. | CC0. Align elapsed milliseconds within each participant and video. |
| openESM 0046, Ringwald, Vize and Wright | The original header and provider dictionary bind person, day, prompt, self and perceived-other affect, and three empathy fields. | CC BY-NC 4.0. Internal noncommercial use. The same person rates both sides. There is no independent event item for the atlas. |

Both independent processes reproduced the same census and binding receipts byte for byte. The [admission design](../../../docs/design/stage-15/week8/ADM2.md) and the accompanying receipt give the edition and original-release digests.
