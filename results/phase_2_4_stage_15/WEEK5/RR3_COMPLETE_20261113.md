**Hypothesis.** Does the next choice in a phone game follow recent better-or-worse-than-expected outcomes more strongly than distance from the play's starting score? The comparison tests the packet's choice-based proxy for slope versus level, not pleasure or a biological need.

**METHOD.** Reuse the verified cohort with recorded actual attempts one, two and three, including every later observed play. Fit the existing grouped logistic choice model with its eight original covariates and one added predictor: the average outcome minus the chosen option's expected value over up to three strictly earlier trials in the same play. Exclude each play's first trial and reset history at every play. Compare the magnitude of the recent-error coefficient with the larger magnitude of the below-start and above-start distance coefficients, using fixed global first-play scales, a practical bar of 0.05, two thousand whole-person resamples per panel and complete separate computational replay.
