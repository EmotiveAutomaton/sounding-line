# Five comparisons in parents’ evening diaries

Of five registered parent-diary comparisons, 2 met their directional and practical bars. The row-by-row results retain all five estimates and both practical-bar readings.

One parent rated the events, their own feelings, the child's feelings and their shared activity on the same evening. A generally difficult day can affect all of those answers. These three weeks during Germany’s first COVID-19 lockdown provide a specific setting, and the observations establish association.
