**METHOD.** Use the complete qualified roster and a fixed split of whole writers: 3,370 discovery reports from 1,204 writers and 3,188 confirmation reports from 1,170 other writers. For every one of thirteen labels and twenty-one appraisal questions, learn the label-versus-rest direction in discovery, fix discovery scaling, and estimate the corresponding confirmation contrast with the declared response-style adjustment, excluding the outcome item from that adjustment. Refit on 2,000 whole-confirmation-writer draws. One simultaneous 95% maximum-standardized-error band covers all 273 cells; that band governs each cell's 0.10 standardized-unit bar. There is deliberately no overall primary scalar.

These single-cell normal approximations are especially optimistic for decisions made using simultaneous bands.
