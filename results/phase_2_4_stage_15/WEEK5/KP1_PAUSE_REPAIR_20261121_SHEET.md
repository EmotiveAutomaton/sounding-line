# Pause and surprise over the next three words

The deletion comparison could not estimate an odds ratio. Longer pauses predicted a modest increase in surprise over the next three words, including away from sentence boundaries. The raw pause showed the same pattern. Dots show change in natural-log units per standard deviation of pause. The table also lists all six unestimable deletion comparisons.

The typing log records revisions and word choices. It contains no independent observation of an attention switch. With identical covariates, the adjusted raw-pause and residual-pause models span the same regression space. Their per-standard-deviation coefficients use different scales.
