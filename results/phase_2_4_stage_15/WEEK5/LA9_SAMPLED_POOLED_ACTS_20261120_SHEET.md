# Matched laughter by dialogue act (log odds ratio)

In the selected meetings, AMI social utterances had higher odds of coinciding with the matched laughter label than information exchange. The odds ratios were 5.97 with prior laughter in the model and 5.63 without it. The two models supplied 20 nonreference intervals in total. The figure uses log odds ratios. Blank cells have intervals that include equal odds. The full table reports odds ratios and both interval bounds.

Corpus annotation systems describe different speech functions. The matched label can include laughter already underway. Timing and topic do not identify the speaker’s intent or the cause of laughter. ICSI coefficients use the AMI information-exchange reference, so they combine differences in corpus and speech function. The full-source pilot projected a cost above the allowance.
