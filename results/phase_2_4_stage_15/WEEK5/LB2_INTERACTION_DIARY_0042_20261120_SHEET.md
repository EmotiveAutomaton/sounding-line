# Anger and nervousness in diary 0042

The registered anger-minus-nervousness interaction in cohort 0042 was supports. The matched analysis used 340 people and 11,766 interaction reports.

The same person rated their behaviour and feelings on one occasion. Anger may have preceded the reported dominance. Unequal response noise can change above-floor slopes. The authors’ signed dominance range was used. A secondary catalogue label disagrees at the lower endpoint.
