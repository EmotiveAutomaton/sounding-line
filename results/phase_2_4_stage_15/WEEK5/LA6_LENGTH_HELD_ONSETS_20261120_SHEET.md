# Predicting new laughter after matching word count

For both windows and both feature sets, prediction remained below the registered area of 0.60. The dot shows the held-out area under the receiver operating characteristic curve. The line covers the middle 95% of 2,000 whole-meeting draws. A value of 0.50 is chance and the practical threshold was 0.60.

The annotation identifies a new vocal laugh in recorded meetings. It cannot establish surprise, shared inference, a causal role for length or the proposed PLAY mechanism.
