# AT SOURCE COMPLETE

**Hypothesis.** Can the complete writing record supply aligned typed-word timing and reading contexts without silently dropping unsupported cases?

**METHOD.** Reconstruct every session from authenticated P6/timing records and the raw roster, preserve each production occurrence and its eligibility, form frozen causal-context batches, and repeat the complete source projection in a fresh process. Inspect every output/pin and original native exit.

**Found.** The complete writing source passes independent replay and supplies frozen final-context and at-typing batches; reader scoring remains separately admitted work.

All 1,399 sessions from 61 writer clusters replay. The projection retains 460,507 production occurrences: 401,845 typed words and 58,662 punctuation occurrences. Timing is supported for 344,937 words and unsupported for 56,908; final context is supported for 365,260 and unsupported for 36,585. The preselected at-typing source covers 280 sessions. These counts describe eligibility, not scored scientific effects. The full source and all unsupported dispositions remain private and immutable. Combined native execution and verification cost is 5,074.734375 CPU seconds, charged to AT.
