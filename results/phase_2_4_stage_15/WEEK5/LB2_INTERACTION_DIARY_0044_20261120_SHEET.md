# Anger and nervousness in diary 0044

The registered anger-minus-nervousness interaction in cohort 0044 was inconclusive. The matched analysis used 386 people and 4,916 interaction reports.

The same person rated their behaviour and feelings on one occasion. Anger may have preceded the reported dominance. Unequal response noise can change above-floor slopes. This cohort rated how they felt at the prompt, which can differ from feeling during the interaction.
