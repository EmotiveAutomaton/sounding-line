# Does a simple visuomotor task weaken mood drift relative to rest?

**Hypothesis.** Does a simple visuomotor task weaken mood drift relative to rest?

**METHOD.** Fit a mood-on-elapsed-minutes slope within each first adult task block, average slopes equally over cohort-qualified person records, and use 2,000 whole-person bootstrap draws independently by condition. Compare visuomotor minus rest at the frozen 0.002 mood-unit-per-minute bar; retain random gambling and all three declared companions.
