**Hypothesis.** Does a person's stronger reaction to negative experiences in the first elapsed week accompany more negative endorsements in the second, after accounting for earlier endorsements and response opportunities?

**METHOD.** Run all seven source-supported configurations from the committed first-week design through the already qualified population-guard pipeline: four feeling families in openESM0057 and the three available families in0056. **Found.** First-week fear reactivity has a nominal positive association with later negative endorsements in the first diary source, but none of the three available second-source intervals excludes zero; this descriptive pattern does not establish a shared sensitivity or causal exposure.
