# Feeling-word profiles across settings · 0056

The smaller diary supplies a four-anchor description of feeling words across the two settings. Rows identify the feeling and setting. Columns identify anchor words. White and red dots show positive and negative weights whose intervals exclude zero. Dot area scales with the absolute weight.

People rated the situation and their feelings in the same prompt. The analysis assumes each anchor is sufficiently specific. It cannot identify a biological trigger or establish a word's meaning.
