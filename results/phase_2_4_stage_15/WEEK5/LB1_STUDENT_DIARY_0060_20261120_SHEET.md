# Seven comparisons in a student diary

Of seven registered student-diary comparisons, 2 met the predicted bar, 0 had an interval on the opposite side, 4 were inconclusive and 1 lacked an interpretable interval.

Students rated their own situations and feelings. The opportunity item asks about potential partners present. It measures no desire. Earlier reports are observational and ordering does not establish causation. Dominance may follow anger. Unequal answer noise can mimic differences in occurrence slopes.
