# AT1 DONOR SUPPORT

**Hypothesis.** Can the required earlier same-genre donor histories match every writer's own history without borrowing future text or dropping a writer?

**METHOD.** Reconstruct exact earlier-session and prompt-excluded token budgets for all retained recipients, compare them with the union of every eligible donor, and independently replay every writer's sealed support audit. No probabilities, timing outcomes or regression fits enter the audit.

**Found.** The frozen writer-history comparison lacks donor support for a required writer even with the entire eligible donor pool; its primary and declared donor-count variants are unsupported on that roster.

One of the 35 required writers fails even the full donor-union capacity bound. Necessary capacity passes for 20 writers at one donor, 33 at three, and 34 at five and twenty; these counts are upper bounds, not verified usable calibrated comparisons. The original five-donor point remains undefined. Changing the regression to the declared below-floor pooled fit cannot create missing donor histories, and none of the registered donor-count variants can repair an impossible union on the unchanged full roster. The descriptive support census is the faithful fallback. No remaining 2,000-draw run is launched, no unsupported writer is silently removed, and no absence-of-effect or negative claim is licensed. This is a design support disposition, not an instrument sensitivity failure; no automatic sensitivity follow-up is created for it.
