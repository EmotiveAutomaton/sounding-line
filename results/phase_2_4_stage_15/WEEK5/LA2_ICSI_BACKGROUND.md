# Do ICSI speakers with the same recorded English variety or region join each other’s laughter more often?

**Hypothesis.** Do ICSI speakers with the same recorded English variety or region join each other’s laughter more often?

**METHOD.** Separately project the two recorded metadata fields onto every participant pair, count missing metadata and undefined no-laugh fractions, and calculate the symmetric share of laugh onsets joined within one second. Regress each share on same versus different recorded background and both speakers’ base laugh rates with within-meeting centering. Refit 2,000 whole-meeting bootstrap draws for each frozen comparison.

**Found.** Both ICSI background comparisons are inconclusive; recorded English variety and region do not establish a shared-inference mechanism.

The table reports each distinct background construct, the number of analytical meetings and pairs, its same-minus-different coefficient in joined-laugh share units, and its meeting-cluster 95% interval. Both use the prospectively fixed 0.02 practical bar.

| Recorded background | Meetings | Pairs | Coefficient | 95% interval |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Recorded English variety | 75 | 999 | 0.00956351 | [-0.00186308, 0.02133276] |
| Recorded region | 64 | 215 | -0.01129047 | [-0.04261036, 0.02422023] |

The complete metadata source has 1,466 possible participant pairs in 75 meetings. English variety supports 999 pairs and leaves 467 unsupported. Region supports 215 and leaves 1,251 unsupported; these supported pairs occur in 64 analytical meetings. Unknown values and undefined event fractions remain counted exclusions, never zero effects.

**Meaning and limits.** Both intervals include zero and the positive practical bar. The regional comparison also permits a negative difference larger than the bar. Neither establishes absence, equivalence or a causal background effect. English variety and region are separate recorded constructs, neither a declaration of native language, nationality or the proposed shared internal model. Familiarity, roles and shared experience remain unmeasured. Meeting resampling preserves within-meeting dependence but does not randomize background or account for every repeated-speaker relation across meetings. Signed/null numerical controls, the actual-format metadata projection fixture and complete independent replay pass; minimum-effect sensitivity in the observed designs remains to be quantified. No new p-value is introduced.
