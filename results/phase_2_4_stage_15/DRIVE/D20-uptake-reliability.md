# D20: reliability of dismissed-suggestion uptake

**Hypothesis.** A writer's tendency to reuse content from dismissed suggestions is consistent across their writing sessions.
**METHOD.** Reconstruct CoAuthor's dismissed menus and subsequent 50 completed typed words; subtract the original other-session prompt/progress-matched overlap. Correlate each writer's average uptake in odd and even chronological sessions. Use 2,000 paired writer-node resamples, 200 within-writer outcome permutations and 200 permutations of whole even-session writer identities.
**Found.** Uptake reliability is null, and own-writer pairing does not beat fake-writer pairing. All repeated outcome references trigger the predeclared bias warning. **Means.** This extension does not establish a stable person-specific uptake tendency. Original matched overlap remains positive, but the repeated controls do not supply a clean zero-centered reference or causal exposure evidence.
