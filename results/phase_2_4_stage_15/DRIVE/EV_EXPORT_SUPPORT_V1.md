# EV first-100 support export: verified complete

**Hypothesis.** The pinned engine reader can export the required support cohort,
retain original writing-log identities and reproduce its output within the
existing allocation.

**METHOD.** Export the first 100 sorted authenticated final documents, reconstruct
their source records and repeat the uncached reader in a fresh process. Verify
every input and terminal output hash, exact word joins, retained typed fragments,
phrase coordinates and support declarations. Before inspecting corpus phrases,
check the signature counter on positive, zero, negative, single-section and
false-declaration fixtures. This is an instrument/support check, not a test of
writer specificity or pause association.

**Found.** The whole pilot reproduces exactly and exceeds the required signature
support floors. **Means.** The support gate permits building EV2; full-population
execution still requires validated reuse and cumulative-cost admission. No
scientific claim or engine badge follows.

All 2,040 source pins and 29 terminal artifacts verify. Independent reconstruction
matches the saved original-row join and typed fragments. Fresh uncached exports
match exactly; exact native workers and known descendants exited, and the counted
slot was released. Engine commit, exporter, reader revision and environment are
unchanged from the [build and dispatch](EV_EXPORT_SUPPORT_V1_BUILD.md).

**Continuation admission remains open.** Reusing the already verified first 100
source records and exports would leave 1,299 documents. A linear measured-rate
estimate for that remaining source preparation plus fresh reader/replay is
24,837.876 seconds; adding every prior charge gives 27,301.945 seconds against
the one-time S4 ceiling of 28,800 seconds. That leaves only about 25 minutes and
includes no uncertainty allowance. It is a forecast, not a guaranteed fit or
permission to extend the ceiling again. Authenticated reuse, checkpoints and
full independent verification must be implemented and validated before dispatch;
the original completed artifacts remain immutable.
