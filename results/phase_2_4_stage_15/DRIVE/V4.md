# V4: taste and habit reader calibration

**Hypothesis.** Readers aimed at writer-specific taste and habit can distinguish planted structure from no structure.
**METHOD.** Run the complete fixed calibration: three planted families at four strengths, with 90 calibration worlds and 20 separate shuffled-identity null worlds, then replay every saved likelihood test and held-out forecast score.
**Found.** The combined reader rejects all 20 shuffled null worlds and every zero-effect cell; it therefore fails calibration. The oracle reader detects the medium-strength plants with acceptable null rates. **Means.** The fitted reader is not admitted for personal taste or habit claims. This is an instrument limit, not evidence that writers lack those properties.

The constructed choices use the exposed human-menu scaffold; these are simulated
outcomes read by exact fitted statistical programs, not human taste measurements.
The lexical taste plant uses a shared four-dimensional writer space within 16
lexical components. Its matched companion uses the reader's embedding components.
Habit is generated causally from each world's prior choices with decay 0.7 and
writer-specific strength. The retained clarifications use mean offered-option
projection, never the selected option, and frozen base-probability centers.

The separate mandatory shuffled-identity null check has 20 worlds per reader.
Counts are false detections: R-kernel 2, R-taste 20, R-both 20, R-oracle 0,
and R-Phase0 0. A reader must also have at most one detection in each ten-world
zero-effect cell. The kernel-only reader fails that latter requirement despite
passing the separate check. The earlier Phase 0 reader never detects a plant.
Both taste-bearing fitted readers report effects even when nothing is planted.
Thus their high planted detection rates cannot establish discrimination.

The matched-representation combined reader has a mean held-out gain of 0.003046
at strength 0.4; eight of its ten writer-bootstrap intervals are positive. This
is retained useful structure in that construction, but it does not repair the
failed existence-test calibration. Its lexical-taste and habit mean gains at the
same strength are slightly negative. The oracle's corresponding means are positive
for all three plants, and it passes the frozen detection/null criteria. Information
is present under the oracle's supplied feature; a better fitted reader or calibrated
test remains a separate engineering obligation, not an already validated result.

The implementation estimates taste directions using training outcomes, chooses
reader settings by calibration forecasts, and applies the stipulated likelihood
reference to the selected fit on training plus calibration data. Its source
explicitly requires the independent size check rather than assuming that reference
is conservative after selection. The observed false-positive failure is established;
selection effects, representation and model misspecification are not isolated
causes by this run. In particular, the oracle-only route does not justify claiming
that representation alone explains the failure when the matched arm also fails
its null check. The frozen outputs and thresholds remain unchanged.

All 110 world checkpoints are complete. The three original pilot worlds are reused
byte-identically, with all nine retained files checked. Fresh-process verification
recomputed every saved likelihood ratio, held-out writer-bootstrap score, recovery
curve, label and primary. Owner review separately verified all 455 completion
output hashes and 36 plan pins, required cell sizes and expansion rules, every
reported detection count, null gate, label and primary contrast, exact native exits
and CPU-slot release. Verification replays saved fits; it does not independently
refit every model or prove the model's conceptual adequacy. The source and numerical
rules are frozen; all generated choices and participant-linked inputs remain private.

The completed calibration therefore gives the required labels: **the lens is blind
to taste at every tested size** and **the lens is blind to habit at every tested
size**. Nulls interpreted through that lens remain instrument-limited; no general
absence of writer taste or habit follows. FX1 and personal taste/habit panels remain
unadmitted. Existing population-only presentation remains the allowed scope. This
does not replace the separate controls and scope of direct process-record cards.
No paid call or GPU work ran, and no calibration retry is automatically dispatched.
