# P7 CPU execution pilot: valid scores, full pass held on cost

**Question.** Can the commissioned CPU reader score complete visible prefixes correctly and finish the full workload within its allowance?
**METHOD.** Authenticate the completed production ledger, raw logs, cached GPT-2 medium and all joke texts; score a length-selected typed session and joke, retain native-token losses, and independently reconstruct source coverage and fresh forward checks.
**Found.** The execution pilot passes numerical and source validation, but projects 13.03 hours for the full corpus before verification. **Means.** The reader is numerically usable on this pilot; the full pass remains unadmitted because that estimate exceeds the six-hour threefold allowance.

The complete pilot contains 612 produced-word/punctuation targets, split evenly
between the median typed session and the longest joke. These were selected by
length before scoring from 1,399 authenticated sessions and 150 original jokes.
This is an instrument/timing sample, not a hypothesis result or family verdict.

All 44 output hashes and 55 source pins pass owner inspection.
All retained native losses, word/session reductions and denominators reconstruct.
Six selected source targets and six synthetic cache cases are independently
checked against fresh CPU float32 forwards. Maximum absolute token-loss error
is 1.23977661e-05 nats, below the fixed 0.0001 tolerance.
The synthetic cases cover prefix extension, revisions, repeated queries,
Unicode and native-context overflow. Thirteen targeted checks also pass,
including saved-block resume, mutation refusal and actual counted-runtime
execution/fresh verification. Controller and children exited; the slot released.

Typed scoring took 30.384 seconds and joke scoring
27.414 seconds. Per-domain target-rate scaling gives
46913.732 seconds for the corpus, excluding final verification.
The first-tenth estimate for the small pilot itself did not trigger a yield.
The separate full-corpus projection exceeds twice the two-hour forecast and
the six-hour threefold allowance. No full-pass dispatch follows. This is a
cost/admission limit, not a negative scientific result or a failed worker.
