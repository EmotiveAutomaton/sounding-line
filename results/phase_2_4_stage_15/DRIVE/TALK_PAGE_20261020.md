# What the completed work supports

**Question.** Can a reader recover useful traces of a writer from words,
choices and pauses without claiming to read the writer's private goals?
**Method.** The completed comparisons use recorded writing sessions, fixed
feature definitions and explicit control comparisons. Engine tests use the
pinned preview engine. This page summarizes the
[October 19 integration](DIGEST_20261019.md); it adds no experiment or verdict.

Two engine readouts have positive, scoped results. Phrases selected as a page's
signatures recur more in other work by the same writer under the tested
comparison. Words flagged as departures are associated with longer preceding
pauses after the recorded adjustments. The first supports a cross-work
recurrence readout; the second supports a hesitation-related readout. Neither
identifies a person's goal, proves attention caused a pause, or establishes
automaticity. Final-text signatures can include model-suggested wording.
[Signature evidence](EV2_SIGNATURES_V1.md);
[pause evidence](EV1_PAUSES_V1.md).

The separate log-based comparison identified 44 of 45 writers from recorded
rhythm (97.8%), 21 from typed words and punctuation (46.7%), and seven from
handling model offers (15.6%). These are observed accuracies in a fixed gallery,
not population accuracy estimates. The rhythm advantage passes its comparison's
reassignment check; the style advantage fails that check. Recording or device
context can contribute to rhythm. [Log comparison](PMAP.md);
[rhythm comparison and controls](PMAP-rhythm.md).

The table gives completed estimates and 95% uncertainty intervals obtained by
resampling whole writers. Identification differences are percentage points;
the other rows retain their own units. These are conditional, descriptive
comparisons, not independent-population validation.

| Completed comparison | Estimate (95% interval) | Scope and control limit |
|---|---|---|
| Rhythm identification minus handling identification | +82.2 percentage points (+71.1 to +93.3) | 45 writers; this contrast's reassignment check passes, but original channel-level warnings remain |
| Style identification minus handling identification | +31.1 percentage points (+11.1 to +48.9) | 45 writers; biased reassignment reference, so the observed advantage is not a clean specificity result |
| Signature recurrence in own versus other writers' work | +1.38 occurrences per 10,000 words (+0.05 to +3.14) | 52 supported writers; fixed phrase roster and corpus; reassignment check passes |
| Pause associated with one unit of engine departure | +7.57% (+6.21% to +9.03%) | 44,494 surviving scored words from 41 writers; within-session reference passes; association, not intervention |

One departure unit is the page's middle-half spread in the engine's word score.
The signature interval's lower bound is close to zero. Neither engine estimate
extends to excluded words or unsupported source comparisons.

The engine's habit-identification comparison fails its controls. Its observed
identification contrast therefore does not license a validated habit badge.
That is a failure of this instrument, not evidence that people have no stable
habits. The separate taste instruments likewise remain unable to license a
personal-taste claim under their fixed gates.
[Habit controls](EV3_IDENTIFICATION_V1.md);
[complete validity screen](DIGEST_20261019.md).
