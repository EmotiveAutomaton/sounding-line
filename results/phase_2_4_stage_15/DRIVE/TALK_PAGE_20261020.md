# What the completed work supports

**Question.** Can a reader recover useful traces of a writer from words,
choices and pauses without claiming to read the writer's private goals?
**Method.** The completed comparisons use recorded writing sessions, fixed
feature definitions and explicit control comparisons. Engine tests use the
pinned preview engine. This page summarizes the
[October 19 integration](DIGEST_20261019.md); it adds no experiment or verdict.

Two engine readouts have positive, scoped results. Phrases selected as a page's
signatures recur more in other work by the same writer under the tested
comparison. Words flagged as departures are associated with longer preceding
pauses after the recorded adjustments. The first supports a cross-work
recurrence readout; the second supports a hesitation-related readout. Neither
identifies a person's goal, proves attention caused a pause, or establishes
automaticity. Final-text signatures can include model-suggested wording.
[Signature evidence](EV2_SIGNATURES_V1.md);
[pause evidence](EV1_PAUSES_V1.md).

The engine's habit-identification comparison fails its controls. Its observed
identification contrast therefore does not license a validated habit badge.
That is a failure of this instrument, not evidence that people have no stable
habits. The separate taste instruments likewise remain unable to license a
personal-taste claim under their fixed gates.
[Habit controls](EV3_IDENTIFICATION_V1.md);
[complete validity screen](DIGEST_20261019.md).

The timer-related ending comparison is null. The crossed comparison of local
and pervasive pauses remains unresolved, and its paired own-history advantages
are null. The shared-word variant could not meet its fixed support requirement
before fitting, so it is void rather than negative.
[Timer result](D24-week-continuation.md);
[crossed result](D30.p-week-recovery.md);
[shared-word limit](D30.v2-shared.md).

For the engine, use the [feature table](ENGINE_FEATURE_TABLE.md) with each row's
definitions and control warnings. Logged rhythm can include device and recording
context and belongs to owner captures. Web-page style uses different inputs.
An unmeasured reliability curve is unavailable, not zero or an invented record
requirement. A result measured with Sounding Line's word list does not transfer
automatically to a different list.
