# Stage 15 theory-days evidence

The [operative campaign](../../../docs/design/stage-15/theory-days/README.md)
is CPU-only and bounded by its own clock and 15 CPU-hour cumulative budget.
Scientific designs are committed before outcomes. Raw records remain private.

[Index](INDEX.md) distinguishes validation, source admission, execution and
scientific completion. Every completed result receives full internal write-through;
the curator receives one final digest at drain, with corrections first.
No intermediate per-artifact score licenses a claim.
