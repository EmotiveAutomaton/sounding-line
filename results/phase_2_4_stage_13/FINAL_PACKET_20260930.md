# Stage 13 final evidence packet

Assembled September 30, 2026, ahead of the October 1, 06:00 PDT endpoint.
The scientific queues are drained. All runnable admitted work and the feasible
corrective families have finished. Failed admissions and unavailable comparisons
remain deficits; they are not successes. The fixed checkpoint helper and
four-hour operational watcher continue through the reporting endpoint.

The question was whether reading a contribution's structure improves detection,
recovers that contribution, and benefits from coupling goals and processes.
**Method:** compare frozen detectors on human originals and machine revisions,
evaluate retrospective human process records and their cheap rivals, replay
retained model forecasts, and run separately frozen detector, memory and reader
repairs. Complete consumers, saved scores and file bindings were audited without
repeating scientific inference during final assembly.

The practical answers are:

1. **Detection improved over the matched window-feature rival in a bounded
   setting.** The stricter frozen operating point replicates the advantage on a
   separate source partition of the same corpus. The full-tuned detector still
   has higher recall with more human false positives. Equal-error superiority
   and the original promotion rule are not established.
2. **General contribution reconstruction did not improve demonstrably.** Exact
   location recovery remains weak in the detector study. Human handling has useful
   cheap alignment references, but the primary neural reader failed known-answer
   admission. Correctly empty slots and extraction of supplied answers do not
   establish recovery of an unseen process.
3. **Reciprocal goal/process benefit is unestablished.** The human revision-purpose
   screen has tradeoffs across losses; its assisted operation result survives
   severing the link. The CoAuthor CPU variants have no learned goal support and
   therefore cannot test that coupling. These failures do not refute the broader
   theory or establish anything about human private goals or values.

## Warrant and pursuit

The warrant ledger states what is supported now; pursuit names worthwhile future
engineering questions and does not enqueue a new campaign.

| Topic | Warrant now | Worth pursuing next |
|---|---|---|
| Detector structure | Bounded matched-feature gain at the stricter frozen operating point; recall/error tradeoff against the strong detector | Predefine equal-error comparison and a material human-error tolerance before new sources |
| Contribution reading | Useful cheap alignment references; no admitted general neural reader; extraction and empty-slot scores have narrow meanings | A compact semantic interface that passes changed-fact and absent-relation controls, with whole-family capacity established first |
| Coupling | Reciprocal benefit unestablished; CoAuthor goal contrast void without goal input; Schola operation assistance remains privileged | An evidence-matched, independently labelled coupling test that keeps answer assistance separate |
| Memory | Same-information rendering affects forecasts; no overall memory benefit on the larger partitions | Treat no-memory, misassociation and padding controls as mandatory rivals, with genuinely independent source components |
| Human interpretation | Recorded handling, annotation correspondence and private goals are different targets | Obtain independent target and source structure before claims about mental history or values |

Three unresolved design questions remain: Can a compact reader reliably use
explicit negative facts before it is asked to infer missing ones? Can joint
reconstruction beat alignment and retrieval at matched evidence and cost? Can
located detection retain a useful gain at a prespecified human-error tolerance
while also recovering positive contribution spans?

All currently admitted science is exhausted. Next activity is scheduled endpoint
reconciliation and preservation of this packet, not another scientific launch.
