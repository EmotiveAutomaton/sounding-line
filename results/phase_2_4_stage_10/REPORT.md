# Stage 10: useful prediction, without a general reconstruction advantage

**Hypothesis.** A compact model of a maker's procedures and goals, combined with remembered examples and executable checks, improves prediction of a withheld choice beyond direct reading and equally budgeted deliberation.

**METHOD.** Compared the six prescribed reader strategies on frozen human revision/handling records and public constructed Ghost tasks. Retained literal requests, malformed answers, executable proposals, development-only fits and all costs. Replayed the complete local queues and comparison consumers without new inference. Human results are descriptive: these are exposed corpora, few source groups and dependent evidence views. The separately completed [Gear 3 Round 1 report](../gear3/G3-S10-READER-1/REPORT.md) remains its own experiment.

**Found.** Structured assistance provides some useful predictive increments, especially with the right earlier evidence. It does not provide a general advantage over simple baselines. A training-frequency prior beats every tested model on both main CoAuthor views and in 27 of 30 ScholaWrite project/target/view cells. Execution helps on selected constructed tasks, but executing a legal proposed route is much easier than recovering the recorded artifact or historical process. The study has finished its finite local selection, including the scoped repair. Its original failed admission remains in the record.

## What was compared

Direct reading predicts from the declared evidence. Retrieval supplies examples. Matched deliberation spends a comparable output allowance on reconsideration. Executable hypotheses turn proposed rules into predictions. Procedure memory combines reusable descriptions with concrete examples; grounded and opaque descriptions remain separate. Adaptive effort chooses whether to retrieve or execute using a development-frozen benefit/cost policy, with fixed and confidence-only policies as controls.

The main local reader is the pinned Qwen 3.5 9B package; a Llama 3.1 8B package supplies the second local comparison. These are package comparisons, not isolated tests of parameter count. Forecast probabilities are elicited output vectors, not token likelihoods or measurements of an internal posterior. Limits match declared allowances; realized call counts, tokens and time differ by route and remain recorded.

Primary tables use **half multiclass Brier loss**, a probability error score from zero to one; lower is better. Invalid forecasts receive system loss one and stay in the denominator. Valid-only loss, logarithmic loss, zero-probability failures, generated-choice accuracy, probability accuracy, calibration, grouped contrasts and costs remain separate in the machine-readable table. A generated choice can contradict the probability vector; neither readout silently replaces the other.

## Answers and next decision

1. **Did artifact-conditioned models improve withheld prediction over direct reading and examples?** In some complete source/evidence cells, yes. This is bounded assistance: strong priors still dominate the main human comparisons, and the constructive support comes from very few known-law cases.
2. **Did executable structure add beyond equally budgeted deliberation?** Sometimes. It helps on the constructed populations and selected process/history views, loses on other human views, and adds no general advantage over a cheap domain model.
3. **Did the maker's own history help more than matched other-writer evidence?** Yes descriptively for the controlled CoAuthor history swap, especially for grounded procedures. This remains evidence relevance within the recorded cohort, not historical causal identification or persistent personal-state recovery.
4. **What is the next obstacle?** Representation and proposal coverage, calibration and data/target alignment. The pipeline executes and retains failures, but short surface rules and weakly supported effort fits do not establish a useful persistent maker model. Missing intervention controls remain unresolved comparisons.

The next curator decision is whether to commission a new bounded study aimed at richer, independently scored maker-specific consequences beyond a strong generic baseline. This packet does not silently expand the queue, reopen old confirmations or request additional cloud spending.
