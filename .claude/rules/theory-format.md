# Theory-folder format, the judgment-shaped rules (the linter holds the mechanical ones)

- Blockquotes are the curator's words ONLY. Literature and superseded claims are never
  blockquoted.
- Sections run in decreasing load-bearing order; corrections fold in, never append; no dated
  changelog narrative in afterwords.
- Afterwords are standing interpretations a cold reader can use alone, ending in the fixed
  Confidence vocabulary; a changed table means a revisited afterword in the same edit.
- Identifiers are stable, never reused; disconfirmed ideas get one line; voids one line at the
  bottom.
- No em or en dashes in prose (quote blocks keep en dashes); colons only in headers and labels.
- Full spec: docs/theory/README.md. Re-read it before a batch of theory edits.
